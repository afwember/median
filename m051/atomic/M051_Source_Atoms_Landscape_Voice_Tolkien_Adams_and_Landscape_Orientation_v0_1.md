# 1. Landscape Voice

## Identity and purpose

### M051-LV-001 — Proposed

MEDIAN may employ a situated narratorial device provisionally named Landscape
Voice.
**Prose reference:** 1.1

### M051-LV-002 — Proposed

Landscape Voice renders authoritative game events as they are experienced
through bodies moving through a particular place.
**Prose reference:** 1.1

### M051-LV-003 — Proposed

Landscape Voice is not limited to static scenic description.
**Prose reference:** 1.1

### M051-LV-004 — Proposed

Landscape remains narratively relevant during action because terrain, scale,
weather, light, sound, cover, infrastructure, and available movement shape how
action is experienced.
**Prose reference:** 1.1

### M051-LV-005 — Proposed

Landscape Voice may change narratorial tempo and selective attention without
changing the truth of the resolved event.
**Prose reference:** 1.1

### M051-LV-006 — Proposed

Landscape Voice should render the story produced by player action and
persistent world state rather than inventing an independent plot.
**Prose reference:** 1.1

## Attachment-forward function

### M051-LV-007 — Proposed

Landscape Voice can strengthen attachment by binding an event to its particular
Citizens, place, conditions, possessions, risks, and history.
**Prose reference:** 1.2

### M051-LV-008 — Proposed

Narrated experience may give mechanical consequence a personal and spatial
surface without replacing that consequence.
**Prose reference:** 1.2

### M051-LV-009 — Proposed

MEDIAN's ordinary player actions already produce facts capable of becoming
story without a separately authored plot interrupting play.
**Prose reference:** 1.2

### M051-LV-010 — Proposed

Party membership, purpose, route, cargo, encounter, Approach, consequence,
condition, withdrawal, and Homecoming are candidate factual inputs to immediate
narration.
**Prose reference:** 1.2

## Narrative architecture

### M051-LV-011 — Proposed

Landscape Voice should reuse MEDIAN's existing narrative transformation for
the Record, Memories, Citizen Tales, Item Tales, and the Chronicle.
**Prose reference:** 1.3

### M051-LV-012 — Proposed

Landscape Voice should not establish an unrelated prose generator or parallel
narrative authority.
**Prose reference:** 1.3

### M051-LV-013 — Proposed

Authoritative facts should enter the Record before they are rendered as
narration.
**Prose reference:** 1.3

### M051-LV-014 — Proposed

A narratorial passage may be rendered from Record facts, established history,
current sensory conditions, and a selected narratorial orientation.
**Prose reference:** 1.3

### M051-LV-015 — Proposed

A passage experienced during play may enter the existing Memories process with
references to its source Record facts, participants, and location.
**Prose reference:** 1.3

### M051-LV-016 — Proposed

Existing salience rules should determine whether a narrated passage remains an
immediate memory, contributes to a personal Tale, or qualifies for civic
Chronicle treatment.
**Prose reference:** 1.3

### M051-LV-017 — Proposed

Immediate narration should not make every narrated moment Chronicle-worthy.
**Prose reference:** 1.3

### M051-LV-018 — Proposed

The Record remains authoritative when its facts receive narratorial
interpretation.
**Prose reference:** 1.3

### M051-LV-019 — Proposed

The exact passage first experienced by the player should normally be preserved
with its Record references rather than silently regenerated into a
contradictory account.
**Prose reference:** 1.3

## Pacing and species attention

### M051-LV-020 — Proposed

Narratorial sentence length, perceptual breadth, rhythm, and selected detail
may express expansive, compressed, suspended, exhausted, or urgent experience.

**Prose reference:** 1.4

### M051-LV-021 — Proposed

Narratorial pacing should be derived from actual conditions rather than
selected solely to manufacture excitement.
**Prose reference:** 1.4

### M051-LV-022 — Proposed

Rabbit-oriented narration may favor cover, exposed distance, shared ground,
warning, and continuity of familiar Places.
**Prose reference:** 1.4

### M051-LV-023 — Proposed

Mouse-oriented narration may favor seams, edges, cavities, fine routes,
enclosure, and small structural failures or opportunities.
**Prose reference:** 1.4

### M051-LV-024 — Proposed

Squirrel-oriented narration may favor anchors, height, gaps, momentum, route
continuity, and distributed refuge.
**Prose reference:** 1.4

### M051-LV-025 — Proposed

Species-oriented narratorial tendencies should not become cognitive limits or
rigid prose templates.
**Prose reference:** 1.4

### M051-LV-026 — Proposed

Species may attend differently to one shared physical world and one resolved
event.
**Prose reference:** 1.4

## Knowledge and presentation boundaries

### M051-LV-027 — Proposed

Landscape Voice may use authoritative Record facts, known world state,
perceivable conditions, Party state, established history, species attention,
and resolved consequences.
**Prose reference:** 1.5

### M051-LV-028 — Proposed

Landscape Voice should not disclose hidden motives, undiscovered hazards,
unearned object knowledge, absent relationships, unresolved outcomes, or
unsupported internal emotions.
**Prose reference:** 1.5

### M051-LV-029 — Proposed

Landscape Voice should not disguise required mechanical instructions as
literary prose.
**Prose reference:** 1.5

### M051-LV-030 — Proposed

Landscape Voice should remain authoritative about situated perception and
restrained about interpretation.
**Prose reference:** 1.5

### M051-LV-031 — Proposed

Landscape Voice should appear only at spatially or dramatically earned
thresholds rather than at every screen or map boundary.
**Prose reference:** 1.6

### M051-LV-032 — Proposed

Potential Landscape Voice thresholds include major arrivals, overlooks,
Crossing emergence, consequential flight, dawn after a journey, Homecoming,
and return to a materially changed familiar place.
**Prose reference:** 1.6

### M051-LV-033 — Proposed

Landscape Voice should not impose a fixed thirty-second wait or become a
cutscene by default.
**Prose reference:** 1.6

### M051-LV-034 — Proposed

The visual world should remain primary while narratorial prose enters and
leaves unobtrusively.
**Prose reference:** 1.6

### M051-LV-035 — Proposed

The game should remain mechanically intelligible when a Landscape Voice
passage is omitted.
**Prose reference:** 1.6

### M051-LV-036 — Proposed

Landscape Voice should deepen existing transitions rather than become a sixth
Register, second Record, separate memory ledger, or new mandatory event class.

**Prose reference:** 1.7

# 2. Tolkien and Adams

### M051-TA-001 — Proposed

Tolkien and Adams should function as critical and creative lenses for MEDIAN
rather than as project authorities.
**Prose reference:** 2.1

### M051-TA-002 — Proposed

The Source does not treat a specific line of direct literary influence as
biographically proven.
**Prose reference:** 2.1

### M051-TA-003 — Proposed

Landscape may operate as syntax in which terrain shapes perception, available
choices, movement, and consequence.
**Prose reference:** 2.1

### M051-TA-004 — Proposed

A selected observation point and visually coherent composition can produce
prose that functions as both image and navigable mental map.
**Prose reference:** 2.2

### M051-TA-005 — Proposed

MEDIAN can learn methodologically from painterly landscape composition without
imitating Tolkien's prose.
**Prose reference:** 2.2

### M051-TA-006 — Proposed

Animal-height perception transforms ordinary hedges, ditches, roads, grass,
and culverts into walls, routes, exposure, boundaries, refuge, and monumental
infrastructure.
**Prose reference:** 2.3

### M051-TA-007 — Proposed

Animal landscape is inhabited, read, feared, used, remembered, and socially
interpreted rather than merely traversed.
**Prose reference:** 2.3

### M051-TA-008 — Proposed

MEDIAN extends animal-height landscape attention into human infrastructure
whose original purposes are unrelated to its animal inhabitants.
**Prose reference:** 2.4

### M051-TA-009 — Proposed

Human infrastructure may become animal geography, ecology, boundary, refuge,
danger, and political territory.
**Prose reference:** 2.4

### M051-TA-010 — Proposed

MEDIAN should preserve its own vocabulary, systems, species, corridor, and
voice while learning from Tolkien's and Adams's seriousness about place.
**Prose reference:** 2.4

### M051-TA-011 — Proposed

A place may acquire behavioral and emotional character through physical
organization without receiving a simple moral label.
**Prose reference:** 2.5

### M051-TA-012 — Proposed

Different corridor landscapes should make animal life and civilization behave
differently rather than differing only in artwork.
**Prose reference:** 2.5

### M051-TA-013 — Proposed

Landscape Voice should not flatten terrain into safe-versus-evil,
reward-versus-punishment, or biome stereotypes.
**Prose reference:** 2.5

# 3. Landscape Orientation

### M051-LO-001 — Proposed

Landscape Orientation is a proposed method for developing MEDIAN places,
environmental art, authored situations, narrative passages, and future Source
material.
**Prose reference:** 3.1

### M051-LO-002 — Proposed

Landscape Orientation begins from governing Specs and known world state.
**Prose reference:** 3.1

### M051-LO-003 — Proposed

A significant MEDIAN location should be constructed as a concrete place rather
than only a generic terrain category.
**Prose reference:** 3.1

### M051-LO-004 — Proposed

Landscape Orientation selects an animal-scale observation point before
determining what the observer can perceive.
**Prose reference:** 3.1

### M051-LO-005 — Proposed

Landscape Orientation considers cross-section, grade, material, vegetation,
drainage, light, weather, sound, visibility, and human infrastructure.
**Prose reference:** 3.1

### M051-LO-006 — Proposed

Landscape Orientation determines what is visible, concealed, reachable,
traversable, useful, dangerous, familiar, or unknown from the selected point.

**Prose reference:** 3.1

### M051-LO-007 — Proposed

Landscape Orientation may derive candidate situations, narrative observations,
and Source propositions from a concrete environment.
**Prose reference:** 3.1

### M051-LO-008 — Proposed

Every proposition derived through Landscape Orientation remains subject to its
governing Specs and explicit review.
**Prose reference:** 3.1

### M051-LO-009 — Proposed

A visually compelling plate does not silently establish a mechanic,
terminology rule, ecological fact, or canonical exception.
**Prose reference:** 3.2

### M051-LO-010 — Proposed

A visual plate may reveal an overlooked affordance or development need and may
therefore generate a developmental Source proposition.
**Prose reference:** 3.2

### M051-LO-011 — Proposed

The safe authority sequence is governing Spec, constructed landscape,
situated observation, candidate Source proposition, and explicit review.
**Prose reference:** 3.2

### M051-LO-012 — Proposed

One persistent physical feature may present different salient affordances to
different species without changing identity.
**Prose reference:** 3.3

### M051-LO-013 — Proposed

Species-specific landscape attention should arise from body, purpose, history,
and current need rather than from separate realities.
**Prose reference:** 3.3

### M051-LO-014 — Proposed

Landscape form should affect perception, safety, exposure, sound, weather,
water, route continuity, refuge, settlement, carrying, defense, human
intersection, and memory.
**Prose reference:** 3.4

### M051-LO-015 — Proposed

Landscape should not become decoration added after systems design.
**Prose reference:** 3.4

### M051-LO-016 — Proposed

Systems should not become abstract mechanics later covered with interchangeable
scenery.
**Prose reference:** 3.4

### M051-LO-017 — Proposed

A future Landscape Voice implementation should leave the game functional when
narration is removed and deepen the player's apprehension when narration is
restored.
**Prose reference:** 3.6

### M051-LO-018 — Proposed

The Record may give an event integrity, Landscape Voice may give it experience,
and Memory may give it duration.
**Prose reference:** 3.6
