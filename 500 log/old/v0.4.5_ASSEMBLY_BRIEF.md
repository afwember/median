## Working discipline

**The book states what is, never what was rejected** (V4-1). This is the rule the last two editions have been enforcing, and assembly is where it is most easily broken — writing a new passage invites explaining why the old one went. Do not. Rationale belongs in Appendix B.

**Watch for the pattern that twice evaded a search:** negations phrased as positive rules. *There is no…* · *X must not be…* · *Why not…* · *replacing older…* · any parenthetical explaining a change rather than a fact.

**Log anything that surfaces; do not solve it.** New questions go to a v0.4.6 log. Assembly must not become another design thread.
