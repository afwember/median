## Notes on this contents

**Spot illustrations are not listed.** They attach to body-text pages throughout and are built as plates run, since most plates generate usable offcuts (P2-24). The nine GSS vignettes seed the class.

**No end plate.** The old plan's "Corridor Secured" closer was built on roadside-PSA signage, which Appendix B rejects on record. Part IX now gives the book an ending, so a decorative closer is no longer doing necessary work. The pull-tab-and-needle prop survives independently.

**Certainty levels apply per section, not per plate** — fully, structurally, or evocatively specified. Crossing is the clearest case of the third: it is not unfinished, it is evocatively specified.
