# MEDIAN v0.6.0 — Organic Narrative Recurrence

**Status:** Deferred v0.6 development note
**Authority:** Noncanonical; requires future authorial review before affecting a
MEDIAN specification
**Origin:** Comparison between the directly authored Story Colony histories and
the histories MEDIAN's game systems could produce through play

## Decision carried forward

The Story Colony accounts for New Elderbank, Rushbottom, and Morningside remain
deftly authored Sourcebook histories. Their narrative strength should not be
reduced to imitate an unedited simulation log.

The corresponding design question is deferred to v0.6:

> How can MEDIAN's ordinary play produce enough particular, recurrent, and
> semantically preserved history that an equally compelling retrospective
> account could be composed from what actually happened?

This note does not amend MEDIAN v0.5.0. It identifies a later opportunity in
Campaign Memory, Citizen expression, MEET presentation, and retrospective
composition.

## Assessment

The distance between the authored histories and organic play depends upon what
is being compared.

- The distance between the histories' **underlying events** and MEDIAN's
  existing systems is relatively small. Roles, relationships, Focus, Projects,
  Places, Keepsakes, Guests, Wounds, Maiming, After-names, Tharn, Homecoming,
  Tier recognition, Chronicle, and Tales can already produce most of the facts
  used by the three accounts.
- The distance between a **raw play log** and their finished literary form is
  substantial. Direct authorship supplies retrospective selection, thematic
  recurrence, symbolic economy, compression, and composed resolution.

The later design task is therefore not to make every moment sound literary. It
is to preserve truthful particulars, recognize meaningful recurrence, and
compose selected history when the game reaches an appropriate narrative
occasion.

The intended rhythm is:

> **Lived event → preserved fact → recurrence → recognition → composed
> memory**

## Existing foundation

MEDIAN v0.5.0 already establishes much of the necessary substrate:

- every Citizen remains named and historically continuous;
- Roles may change while prior service remains part of civic history;
- relationships, Tools, Keepsakes, Wounds, Maiming, and other consequences
  remain attached to particular lives;
- Places, Projects, improvements, damage, and repair remain persistent facts;
- MEET Focus makes one life visible within collective action;
- Homecoming receives material, bodily, relational, and remembered
  consequence;
- the hidden Record remains factual authority;
- Chronicle and Tales select and translate parts of that Record;
- Moment Briefs carry the smallest sufficient projection of truth into
  narrative or visual presentation; and
- Tier recognition, Shared Meal, Guest welcome, recovery, After-name
  recognition, and other civic occasions already provide natural moments for
  looking backward.

The v0.6 question concerns how these pieces select and relate history, not the
creation of a separate narrative simulation.

## Governing proposal: recurrence from the Record

When a situation merits narrative treatment, its Moment Brief may draw upon:

1. the current event;
2. one relevant prior event involving the same Citizen, relationship, Place,
   object, or civic question; and
3. the material change between those moments.

This is a retrieval and presentation principle. A recurrence remains grounded
in existing Record facts. It does not create a visible Theme score, Story
currency, relationship meter, plot state, or independent narrative authority.

Potential forms of recurrence include:

- a Citizen returning to a Place associated with an earlier consequence;
- a Tool or Keepsake appearing again under changed circumstances;
- a Role change recalling the Citizen's earlier civic service;
- a repaired or improved Place carrying forward who established or depended
  upon it;
- two Citizens sharing another event after an earlier rescue, disagreement,
  Homecoming, or loss;
- a later ceremony recalling the Colony's founding, first Residence, first
  Garden, first Guest welcome, or first major return; and
- Chronicle or Tale placing two distant moments beside one another because
  their relationship is now legible.

Recurrence should be selective. Most repeated facts require no literary
attention, and some campaigns will develop no clean central theme. Silence and
unevenness remain preferable to manufactured significance.

## Expressive Citizen identity

The Story Colony identities suggest a useful nonmechanical minimum for later
Citizen presentation:

- a recognizable voice;
- a habit or form of attention;
- an attachment to a Citizen, Place, object, or ordinary activity;
- an unease, unresolved question, or recurring concern; and
- established history accumulated through play.

These identity anchors may shape contextual dialogue, EMBODY behavior,
Chronicle or Tale expression, and which Citizen naturally voices a valid MEET
option. They create no hidden aptitude, production modifier, willingness
score, refusal behavior, or immunity from player choice.

Citizens remain fractional player characters. When different Citizens express
different understandings of a civic decision, every valid option remains the
player's to choose. Character perspective gives a decision human texture
without turning the player into a mayor negotiating with autonomous units.

## Narrative occasions

Later development should test how existing occasions can select a small amount
of applicable history:

- **Homecoming:** what returned, what changed, and which earlier departure or
  promise now matters;
- **Tier Recognition:** a few defining changes in the Colony rather than an
  exhaustive summary of growth;
- **After-name recognition:** the event, its witnesses, and why the name
  endured;
- **Shared Meal:** ordinary proximity among Citizens with accumulated shared
  history;
- **Guest welcome:** prior meetings, fulfilled hospitality, and the transition
  into Citizenship;
- **Recovery and Rehabilitation:** the present body placed in continuity with
  the event and care that preceded it;
- **Place improvement or repair:** the Place's original establishment and the
  need that changed it;
- **Nesting, Maturity, and arrival:** how a new life enters an already specific
  Home; and
- **Chronicle and Tale:** retrospective selection across longer intervals.

These occasions should remain sparse enough that ordinary life can acquire
meaning without the game interpreting every action for the player.

## Story Colony benchmark traces

The three Story Colony histories can serve as qualitative benchmark traces for
v0.6 development. The target is the possibility of comparable emotional
density, not reproduction of their exact plots.

### New Elderbank

- Can Bramble's service as founding Leader remain available when later Teacher
  responsibility gives it new meaning?
- Can Fennel's rescue, Wound, Maiming, recovery, and relationship to Sharpnose
  remain one traceable history rather than separate log entries?
- Can Nutmeg's different recollection of Elderbank enter civic expression
  without establishing an objective master memory?
- Can the inherited Colony name acquire accumulated present-tense associations
  through actual play?

### Rushbottom

- Can a dense Mouse population remain a collection of particular lives rather
  than a long roster?
- Can Barley's attention to names and Pip's distrust of an apparently complete
  count recur through different situations without becoming mechanical
  bonuses?
- Can Crumb's Tharn become part of a relationship and later memory, not merely
  a resolved status event?
- Can Tier recognition select a few meaningful names and changes without
  claiming that the Record is complete when it is not?

### Morningside

- Can actual spatial and Project choices produce a continuing question about
  reach and redundancy?
- Can Rowan, Cobb, and Gale express different perspectives on the same valid
  player decision without refusing or commandeering it?
- Can the consequences of Strained Carry, connection failure, reinforcement,
  and Outpost construction recur as one civic history?
- Can a later Grand Web recognition acknowledge mutual dependence without
  resolving the Colony's argument into a single correct doctrine?

## Record-to-Presentation work for v0.6

A future pass should investigate the following in order:

1. **Semantic sufficiency audit.** Determine whether the Record preserves the
   actors, witnesses, relationships, Places, objects, choices, consequences,
   and before-and-after states required for truthful recurrence.
2. **Identity-anchor treatment.** Establish the smallest expressive Citizen
   description that supports voice and continuity without becoming a
   personality simulation.
3. **Recurrence retrieval.** Define how a Moment Brief finds one genuinely
   relevant prior fact without creating a new tracked narrative layer.
4. **Occasion treatment.** Test Homecoming, Tier Recognition, Shared Meal,
   After-name recognition, recovery, and Chronicle or Tale as distinct forms of
   retrospective composition.
5. **Authored realization.** Build strong non-generative templates and authored
   content capable of expressing those briefs.
6. **Optional model-assisted realization.** Where used, constrain narrative and
   imagery to the Moment Brief and established Citizen and Colony canon. A
   model may realize presentation; it may not invent canonical history.
7. **Campaign trace review.** Examine complete play histories and determine
   whether a concise account can be written without supplying motives,
   causation, or events absent from play.

## Acceptance tests

Future work should satisfy all of the following:

- **Traceability:** every presented historical claim resolves to Record facts.
- **Particularity:** replacing the named Citizens with arbitrary others would
  materially change the account.
- **Recurrence:** later presentation can recognize a relevant earlier moment
  and the change between them.
- **Player authority:** Citizen voice never becomes refusal, hidden preference,
  or loss of player choice.
- **Mechanical restraint:** expressive identity creates no concealed aptitude
  or productivity system.
- **Asymmetry:** campaigns may remain lopsided, quiet, unresolved, or centered
  upon an unexpected Citizen or Place.
- **Selective composition:** the game omits most events and resists declaring
  every recurrence meaningful.
- **Canonical discipline:** model-assisted prose or imagery expresses supplied
  truth and never becomes its source.
- **Attachment:** the resulting history makes particular lives, Places,
  objects, and returns easier to know and remember without demanding an
  emotional response.

## Deferred boundary

This note authorizes no v0.5 change, implementation system, content pipeline,
personality model, narrative director, or generative dependency. It preserves
one future design question and a constrained direction for investigating it in
MEDIAN v0.6.0.
