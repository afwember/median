## Full contents, with the integrated art programme and a page estimate

**A production document.** The GDD v0.4.6 section tree, with every **INFOGRAPHIC**, **ARTWORK**, **CREATED VISUAL** and **NARRATIVE PANEL** set at the point in the text where it belongs, and a page figure against each. Part totals at the foot of each Part; a book total at the end.

## How the page figures were reached

**Not guessed.** Every text figure is derived from the actual word count of that section in v0.4.6, plus a surcharge for display matter — tables, pull-quotes, `OPEN` blocks and bulleted lists, which in this document are heavy and eat space a word count does not see.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | 16:9 landscape, three-column text grid, no spreads (P2-39) |
| **Set text** | **300 words per full page** — a sourcebook density, allowing for generous leading, margins, headings, and the spot-illustration class running beside body text |
| **Display matter** | one table row, blockquote line or bullet ≈ **1/28 page** |
| **Floor** | **0.5 pages** minimum for any section that carries text. *A 0.5 means the section shares a page with its neighbour, not that it gets a half-page to itself* |
| **Bare headings** | numbered parents with no body text of their own — §7, §16, §18, §19 and the like — are marked *heading* and carry **no allocation**; they sit above their first child |

**Plate costs.** One page each for an INFOGRAPHIC or a full-page ARTWORK, with a handful at two where the content demands it — seven Active Guests, nine Ambient Guests, four Tiers, three Road Work beats, four Seasons. The Eight Biomes runs at eight, one establishing plate per biome, as `Contents.md` already specifies. A **CREATED VISUAL** costs 0.5, because it is an inline sidebar with an image rather than a plate. A **NARRATIVE PANEL** costs a full page: the mockup is one page, prose left and mechanic card right.

**Total body text measured:** 24,140 words across 133 sections. That yields **123.5 pages of set text** — which lands almost exactly on `Contents.md`'s stated *"140 pages of systems"*, and is the best available check that the density figure is right.

## Two notes on the figures

**Part IV is 49.5 pages and that is correct, not a fault to fix.** Part IV is the citizens — six of the fifteen panels, five full-page artworks, ten infographics, and the two longest sections in the document (§21.1 Active Guests, §20 Equipment). The book's argument is that the player will know these animals by name; Part IV is where that argument is made, and it should be the heaviest part of the book.

**Part VI is 8 pages and that is a flag.** The Metropolis has a Part to itself on the grounds that it is unlike anything else in the world, and it currently carries less text than §21.1 Active Guests alone. Either the Part earns more material — the OPEN item in `story_colonies.md` schedules a dedicated session for exactly this — or it folds into Part V and the book has eight Parts instead of nine. Worth deciding before layout, because a Part opener in front of eight pages reads as a promise the section does not keep.
