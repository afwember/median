# Gull Expedition Guest Proposal

**Artifact class:** Developmental Source
**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** August 8, 2026
**Status:** Exploratory and non-authoritative
**Scope:** MEDIAN m051 and later development

This Source preserves a proposal to explore Gull as an eighth Expedition Guest.
It does not amend the locked m050 Guest roster, establish a final species entry,
or independently authorize changes to the shared Away framework. Its companion
atomic representation is derivative. This prose document remains the canonical
home of the proposal until later authorial review, reconciliation, testing, and
possible promotion.

---

# 1. Why a Second Aerial Expedition Guest May Matter

MEDIAN already appears to have a shared Flyer mechanic for aerial Expedition
Guests, but the m050 roster expresses that mechanic through Crow alone. Gull may
provide a second aerial Expedition Guest so flight reads as a reusable bodily
grammar rather than as one species' exceptional power.

More than one aerial Guest may be valuable for balance even before their
individual Signatures are compared. Repeated expression can distinguish the
shared consequences of a flying body from the qualities of a particular
species. Crow and Gull might both participate in overhead movement, aerial
access, landing constraints, exposure, and the other established Flyer rules
without sharing a Signature.

This is not intended as taxonomic collection or numerical symmetry for its own
sake. The proposal is useful if a second Flyer makes the existing mechanic more
legible, gives overhead expedition play more than one manifestation, and allows
both birds to express stronger species identities without adding another Away
subsystem.

# 2. Provisional Division of Identity

## 2.1 Gull — Long View

Gull may inherit the provisional Signature **READ — Long View**. Its expression
could emphasize broad open geography: distant movement, weather, water, human
congregation, traffic patterns, and the relationship among widely separated
places.

Gull's Long View would not provide omniscience, exact remote mapping, aerial
fast travel, transport for the Party, a safe Crossing bypass, or automatic
resolution of a distant problem. It would offer situated context before the
ground Party commits.

The proposal may fit a Ring-billed Gull or another regionally credible inland
gull particularly well. Such a bird could plausibly understand highways,
parking lots, reservoirs, rivers, fields, landfills, rooftops, and food sites as
one connected human-shaped ecology rather than as isolated Reaches.

## 2.2 Crow — Trialwork

Moving Long View to Gull may free Crow to emphasize corvid intelligence,
curiosity, memory, experimentation, and opportunistic object use. One
provisional replacement is **HANDLE — Trialwork**.

Trialwork could permit the Crow to use an available object as an intermediary
for one bounded test or indirect manipulation: probing an opening, drawing
something closer, placing a marker, dropping an object to provoke or measure a
response, or using one found thing upon another.

Trialwork would not make Crow a universal puzzle solver, establish elaborate
manufactured-tool engineering, invent absent materials, or guarantee that an
experiment succeeds. The player would still need to recognize a plausible
opportunity, and the world would retain the consequences of the attempt.

## 2.3 Distinction from Raccoon

Crow's proposed Trialwork should remain distinct from Raccoon's existing
**HANDLE — Latchwork**.

> **Raccoon manipulates the human-made object according to its construction.
> Crow recruits one object to act upon another.**

Raccoon may remain the specialist in direct handling of lids, catches,
containers, knots, and simple mechanisms. Crow may instead enable indirect
testing or manipulation through a plausible found object. Two HANDLE entries
would be justified only if this difference remains mechanically and
expressively legible.

The resulting provisional identities are:

- Gull understands what lies beyond.
- Crow discovers how something may work.
- Raccoon knows how to open and handle it.

# 3. Gull as a Citizen

Gull may bring a culture of distance to the Colony. Wind, broad water, rooftops,
parking lots, refuse cycles, storms, and widely separated feeding sites may all
belong to one ordinary lived range. A Gull Citizen's sense of what is nearby,
reachable, or connected could therefore differ sharply from that of Mouse,
Rabbit, Squirrel, and Crow.

That difference may support attachment rather than remaining expedition
utility. Gull might be conspicuous, loud, comfortable in open weather, uneasy
with enclosure, and accustomed to leaving the Colony's perceptual horizon. Its
housing might require a broad exposed landing place or Wind Court rather than a
second version of Crow's High Perch / Open Nest.

A central emotional question may be:

> **How does the Colony make a home for someone whose natural world is too large
> to remain inside it?**

These traits are exploratory directions, not a required personality for every
Gull Citizen. Later development would need to distinguish species-grounded
body and ecology from stereotype, individual character, and authored example.

# 4. Conservation Test and Open Questions

The proposal may satisfy Conservation of System if it reuses the existing
Flyer and shared Away mechanics, transfers an existing Signature, and gives
Crow another bounded Permission inside an existing family. It should not add a
Gull-only movement system, another Register, or a parallel aerial expedition
loop.

Before promotion, development should resolve:

1. Which gull species or level of specificity best fits MEDIAN's geography?
2. What shared Flyer rules actually apply equally to Crow and Gull?
3. In which terrain is Gull's Long View available or constrained?
4. Does Trialwork remain clearly distinct from Latchwork in representative
   Nodes and encounters?
5. What Independent Impulse, House, example Citizen, relationships, and Home
   Role make Gull a particular Citizen rather than an aerial service?
6. Does the eighth Expedition Guest improve roster balance enough to justify
   its sourcebook, artwork, animation, housing, dialogue, and testing burden?

The proposal should advance only if Gull establishes the shared Flyer category
while Gull and Crow remain individually consequential after flight is factored
out.
