# MEDIAN v0.5.0 authorial source library

This directory is the active bridge from the completed information-management
work to authorial GDD creation.

`M050_Authorial_Atom_Corpus_MEDIANv0_5_0.jsonl` contains 5,382 effective claims
from 18 sources. Each row keeps its source address, exact source text,
normalized and effective claim, and authorial triage/rewrite disposition.

The corpus is evidence, not prose and not canon. Retired MSID classifications
and the 170-atom partial reconciliation remain in the historical archive rather
than being carried into the live authoring library. Asa’s judgment in the
developing GDD controls contradiction, supersession, inclusion, emphasis, and
final wording.

The manifest binds the corpus hash, source-document hashes, source counts, and
the m050 boundary. Historical extraction and reconciliation evidence lives in
`600 archive/m050-information-management/` and is not an ordinary composition
input.
