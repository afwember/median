# 1. Residence and population

- **M051-RP-001 — Hypothesis — §1:** Population may remain open rather than be
  hard-capped by Residence count.
- **M051-RP-002 — Hypothesis — §1:** A Citizen may join a Colony that does not
  yet possess adequate Residential Readiness.
- **M051-RP-003 — Hypothesis — §1:** Every resident Citizen may add Residential
  Load.
- **M051-RP-004 — Hypothesis — §1:** Completed, usable Residence Practices may
  provide Residential Readiness against Residential Load.
- **M051-RP-005 — Hypothesis — §1:** Residence may represent the Colony's
  maintained ability to shelter accepted lives rather than function merely as
  a population slot.
- **M051-RP-006 — Hypothesis — §1:** Residence Margin may equal Residential
  Readiness minus Residential Load.
- **M051-RP-007 — Hypothesis — §1:** A nonnegative Residence Margin may be
  displayed as Readiness.
- **M051-RP-008 — Hypothesis — §1:** A negative Residence Margin may be displayed
  as Residence Deficiency.
- **M051-RP-009 — Hypothesis — §1:** Residence Readiness may answer Residential
  Load without erasing the underlying demand created by resident Citizens.

# 2. Deficiency and Colony Pressure

- **M051-RP-010 — Hypothesis — §2:** Residence Deficiency may identify persistent
  vulnerability before producing injury, departure, or damage.
- **M051-RP-011 — Hypothesis — §2:** Residence Deficiency may contribute to
  global Colony Pressure.
- **M051-RP-012 — Hypothesis — §2:** Colony Pressure may primarily influence the
  frequency of World-related Colony Encounters.
- **M051-RP-013 — Hypothesis — §2:** Colony Pressure may avoid functioning as
  individual mood, personality, or generalized happiness.
- **M051-RP-014 — Hypothesis — §2:** Residence Deficiency may persist until
  relevant Residential Readiness answers Residential Load.
- **M051-RP-015 — Hypothesis — §2:** Unrelated surplus or civic success may help
  resolve an Encounter without silently cancelling Residence Deficiency.

# 3. Encounter manifestation

- **M051-RP-016 — Hypothesis — §3:** Global Colony Pressure may primarily
  determine when the World presses upon the Colony.
- **M051-RP-017 — Hypothesis — §3:** One selected principal Weakness may
  determine where global Pressure finds purchase in an Encounter.
- **M051-RP-018 — Hypothesis — §3:** Current World conditions may determine the
  concrete manifestation of an Encounter.
- **M051-RP-019 — Hypothesis — §3:** Residence Deficiency may manifest through
  weather exposure, crowding, displacement, arrivals, evacuation, or repair.
- **M051-RP-020 — Hypothesis — §3:** The main Encounter transaction may concern
  the principal Weakness.
- **M051-RP-021 — Hypothesis — §3:** A secondary choice may invoke another
  relevant Practice or Role without displacing the principal Weakness.
- **M051-RP-022 — Hypothesis — §3:** Resolving an immediate Encounter may protect
  Citizens, redirect harm, or buy time without removing its underlying
  Residence Deficiency.
- **M051-RP-023 — Hypothesis — §3:** Permanent correction of Residence Deficiency
  may require restoration of the relevant Residential Readiness.

# 4. Attachment-forward consequence

- **M051-RP-024 — Hypothesis — §4:** Population growth may be permissive while
  remaining materially consequential.
- **M051-RP-025 — Hypothesis — §4:** A Colony may welcome a Citizen before it is
  fully prepared and then bear increased Pressure while becoming ready for
  that life.
- **M051-RP-026 — Hypothesis — §4:** The Residence relationship may express that
  attachment creates responsibility rather than treating new Citizens as a
