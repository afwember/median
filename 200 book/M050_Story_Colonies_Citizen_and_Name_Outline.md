# MEDIAN v0.5.0 — Canon Story Civilizations and Narrative Character Identities

**Status:** Current m050 authoring stake
**Function:** Single identity and continuity home for MEDIAN's three recurring
story civilizations and their named narrative subjects
**Mechanical authority:**
`m050/gdd/M050_GDD_Provisional_Structure_MEDIANv0_5_0.md`

## Purpose and boundary

The concept book uses three continuous story civilizations to make MEDIAN's
systems particular. New Elderbank, Morningside, and Rushbottom recur throughout
the book through the same Citizens, relationships, Places, possessions, and
accumulating histories. An event shown here is true of that story civilization;
it demonstrates a possible campaign without requiring every player campaign to
repeat it.

These civilizations are Sourcebook narrative subjects rather than fixed game
content. Their Colony and ancestral-Home names, named Citizens, Places,
relationships, possessions, and authored histories do not appear as a
prewritten campaign in the game. The game generates the player's ancestral
Colony, Founders, later Citizens, permanent Home name, Places, and continuing
history through the governing systems. The story civilizations show what that
kind of generated campaign can become.

This document governs the identities and continuity of those recurring
narrative subjects. The GDD governs all mechanics. A biography, Role history,
Guest Signature, Residence, Tier recognition, or other story fact never changes
the general rule that owns it. Individual expression creates no personality
score, hidden aptitude, productivity modifier, or preferred optimization.

All earlier artwork is visual-development evidence. Story-civilization artwork
for the m050 concept book must be remade or deliberately reinterpreted against
the current GDD and the identities below. Full-page civilization art appears
only between Parts and selected Chapters. Solo civilization plates are planned
in matched Mouse, Rabbit, and Squirrel sets; partial-page narrative art is
balanced across the book by substantive attention rather than incidental
cameos.

# I. New Elderbank

## Civilization identity

- **Core Species:** Rabbit
- **Home Reach:** Ashcross
- **Colony:** New Elderbank
- **Lost ancestral Home:** Elderbank
- **Spatial expression:** GATHER around protected open common space
- **Memory register:** disputed recollection becoming shared story
- **Founding population:** Bramble, Twig, and Shadepatch

New Elderbank carries the clearest tribute to *Watership Down* while remaining
entirely inside MEDIAN's own world and systems. Its citizens inherit a name that
looks backward. The Colony gradually makes that name refer to the Home they
have actually built.

## Founders

### Bramble

- **Appearance:** A compact tawny Rabbit with a shallow notch along one ear and
  a knotted bootlace usually looped close to the body.
- **Voice:** Clear, brief, and practical. Bramble tends to give the next
  necessary sentence rather than a complete explanation.
- **Temperament:** Uncertainty is answered by beginning. Bramble is not
  fearless, but can keep acting after everyone has become frightened.
- **Habits and preferences:** Measures openings and short distances with the
  bootlace; prefers work that can be shown to another Citizen; seeks protected
  common ground where the whole Colony remains visible.
- **Relationships:** Twig is Bramble's oldest and closest friend. Their shared
  life later includes Daisy as family. Sharpnose trusts Bramble in an emergency
  and resists being instructed afterward.
- **Keepsake:** A knotted bootlace formerly used to measure passages at
  Elderbank.
- **Established history:** Bramble received Focus during New Elderbank's Chaos
  MEET, served as its first Leader at Founding, and later made teaching, Laws
  and Sayings, and shared memory the center of that Leader responsibility. The
  change in responsibility is civic history, not aptitude progression.

### Twig

- **Appearance:** The smallest of the three founding Rabbits, grey-brown with a
  pale throat and a folded scrap of blue tarp kept close in wet weather.
- **Voice:** Patient and richly spatial. Twig remembers rooms by their turns,
  smells, and neighboring chambers, sometimes describing details nobody else
  can confirm.
- **Temperament:** Reflective without being passive. Twig can remain with a
  difficult memory longer than the others and is slow to replace an uncertain
  account with a convenient one.
- **Habits and preferences:** Traces remembered layouts into loose ground;
  checks edges before settling in an open Place; prefers repaired material that
  continues to show where it came from.
- **Relationships:** Bramble is Twig's closest friend and later family. Daisy
  belongs to that family. Twig listens to Nutmeg's different account of
  Elderbank without deciding that only one of them may be sincere.
- **Keepsake:** A scrap of blue tarp carried from Elderbank.
- **Established history:** Twig remembers Elderbank more extensively than any
  other Founder. Those memories support New Elderbank's Chronicle while
  remaining recollection rather than an objective map.

### Shadepatch, called Sharpnose

- **Appearance:** A dark brown Rabbit with a deeper patch across the muzzle and
  one pale break in the fur near the nose. Sharpnose wears no identifying
  object.
- **Voice:** Dry, clipped, and resistant to ceremony. Silence is often more
  characteristic than speech when a formal recitation begins.
- **Temperament:** Daring, skeptical, and quick to test what others have treated
  as settled. Sharpnose dislikes being made into an emblem even after becoming
  one.
- **Habits and preferences:** Inspects the least comfortable exit first;
  chooses exposed watch positions when a protected one would conceal too much;
  prefers a direct disagreement to a rehearsed consensus.
- **Relationships:** The three Founders share survival history that is never
  reducible to ordinary friendship. Nutmeg later becomes Sharpnose's closest
  companion and the person most able to challenge Sharpnose's account of the
  old Home.
- **Keepsake:** None. Sharpnose deliberately carried nothing away.
- **Established history:** Shadepatch is the Given Name. **Sharpnose** is a rare
  After-name earned after locating Fennel inside a flooded culvert when water,
  traffic, and obscured sight concealed the trapped Rabbit. The Colony confers
  the name later through a Gathering Place observance, following the current
  MEET and Campaign Memory grammar.

# II. Morningside

## Civilization identity

- **Core Species:** Squirrel
- **Home Reach:** Elmwater
- **Biome:** Wooded Median shaped by a narrow drainage run
- **Colony:** Morningside
- **Lost ancestral Home:** Nine Beeches
- **Spatial expression:** CONNECT through redundant anchors and ways
- **Memory register:** unresolved argument becoming civic ambition
- **Founding population:** Rowan, Cobb, and Gale

Morningside is named for what lies ahead rather than what was lost. Its
Citizens share a belief in connection while disagreeing about how far the Web
should reach and how much redundancy must be preserved before it extends.

## Founders

### Rowan

- **Appearance:** A russet-grey Squirrel with a pale outer edge to the tail and
  a pressed beech leaf protected inside a simple wrap.
- **Voice:** Persuasive, forward-looking, and fond of stating a possibility as
  though everyone has already begun moving toward it.
- **Temperament:** Hopeful by discipline rather than innocence. Rowan accepts
  uncertainty more easily than confinement and can mistake continued reach for
  continued safety.
- **Habits and preferences:** Takes the easternmost usable perch at first
  light; looks from a present anchor toward the next credible one; prefers a
  plan that leaves the Colony capable of another choice afterward.
- **Relationships:** Cobb is Rowan's closest civic counterpart and most durable
  disagreement. Gale shares the desire to know what lies farther away but not
  Rowan's confidence that knowledge should always become expansion.
- **Keepsake:** A beech leaf pressed flat after leaving Nine Beeches.
- **Established history:** Rowan received Focus during Morningside's Chaos MEET,
  became its first Leader, and spoke the Colony's name at Founding.

### Cobb

- **Appearance:** A broad, heavy-pawed Squirrel with a blunt tail tip and a bent
  brass screw carried in a fitted loop.
- **Voice:** Literal, concise, and often corrective. Cobb asks what will hold,
  what has been tested, and what remains if the first way fails.
- **Temperament:** Protective, skeptical, and exceptionally patient with
  material work. Resistance to expansion comes from attachment to what already
  exists rather than lack of imagination.
- **Habits and preferences:** Tests bindings after others have declared them
  finished; notices alternate crossings and weak anchors; prefers redundant
  connections to elegant single spans.
- **Relationships:** Rowan's ambition and Cobb's caution form a lifelong civic
  argument held inside deep loyalty. Gale trusts Cobb's construction and
  resents being asked to use only the safest line.
- **Keepsake:** A bent brass screw worked free at Nine Beeches.
- **Established history:** Cobb served among Morningside's first Builders and
  made its earliest connections physically credible. Cobb later changes to
  Leader and conducts the Grand Web Recognition MEET. Neither history creates
  a hidden aptitude.

### Gale

- **Appearance:** A lean Squirrel with a long, wind-ruffled tail and a length of
  weathered fishing line wound close to the chest.
- **Voice:** Sparse and observational. Gale reports what was seen before saying
  what it might mean.
- **Temperament:** Restless, private, and more comfortable with uncertainty in
  the world than with speculation at Home.
- **Habits and preferences:** Reaches a high point before DAWN when possible;
  watches moving weather and traffic longer than companions expect; prefers
  direct evidence to inherited explanation.
- **Relationships:** Rowan sees possibility in Gale's observations. Cobb sees
  risks that Gale has not yet agreed to count. Gale belongs fully to both
  friendships while keeping part of the escape unspoken.
- **Keepsake:** A length of fishing line found during the first quiet
  inspection of Elmwater after the final montage Crossing.
- **Established history:** Gale was the only Founder to look back toward Nine
  Beeches. That remembered act shapes later Watchkeeper responsibility and Away
  participation without granting superior perception.

# III. Rushbottom

## Civilization identity

- **Core Species:** Mouse
- **Home Reach:** Pipe End
- **Colony:** Rushbottom
- **Lost ancestral Home:** Chaff End
- **Spatial expression:** JOIN through adjacency and accumulated interior
- **Memory register:** exact names and records protecting lives from anonymity
- **Founding population:** Barley, Thimble, Pip, and Husk
- **Immediate opening arrivals:** Weft and Warp

The mice named the plants; the Corridor named the concrete. Rushbottom begins
when four Mice escape Chaff End and, immediately after Founding, welcome Weft
and Warp. Six individual Citizens create the same three Body Units as the three
Rabbit or Squirrel Founders. Sump already inhabits Pipe End but becomes a Guest
Citizen only after the new Colony can fulfill the applicable Terms of
Hospitality.

## Founders

### Barley

- **Appearance:** A brown Mouse with a dark dorsal line, pale forepaws, and a
  worn bottle cap carried flat against the body.
- **Voice:** Exact and enumerating. Barley repeats a number when another
  Citizen rounds it and uses names wherever a generic category would conceal a
  person.
- **Temperament:** Steady through precision. Barley can become rigid when an
  answer remains approximate, especially when the uncertainty concerns who was
  present or who returned.
- **Habits and preferences:** Counts the Colony at familiar transitions;
  arranges small records so another Citizen can follow them; prefers a long
  accurate answer to a reassuring estimate.
- **Relationships:** Thimble shares Barley's practical work without sharing the
  need to account for every detail. Pip challenges the belief that a complete
  count can make a dangerous situation controllable. Husk notices when Barley
  has counted everyone except Barley.
- **Keepsake:** A bottle cap that was already being carried when Chaff End was
  destroyed and became important because there was no moment to put it down.
- **Established history:** Barley received Focus during Rushbottom's Chaos MEET,
  became its first Leader, and began the remembered count of Chaff End. The
  hidden Record and later Chronicle—not the Almanac—carry that history. Barley
  later conducts the Grand Manor Recognition MEET, preserving uncertainty
  wherever the remembered count remains incomplete.

### Thimble

- **Appearance:** A warm brown Mouse with quick forepaws and a dull metal
  thimble secured close enough to touch while working.
- **Voice:** Fast, procedural, and collaborative. Thimble naturally divides a
  difficult act into short intelligible handoffs.
- **Temperament:** Practical, sociable, and impatient with solitary heroics.
  Thimble trusts many small competent actions more than one dramatic effort.
- **Habits and preferences:** Passes objects toward the next available set of
  paws; keeps frequently used material near shared edges; prefers adjacent work
  and short carrying distances.
- **Relationships:** Barley supplies exact continuity while Thimble keeps the
  present moving. Pip is both exasperating and beloved. Husk becomes Thimble's
  partner in the two-Mouse Builder commitment that establishes Rushbottom's
  first Workshop.
- **Keepsake:** The thimble for which Thimble had already been named.
- **Established history:** Thimble helped turn the Founders' escape behavior
  into Rushbottom's culture of short handoffs. This remains civic character,
  not a separate Relay mechanic or carrying bonus.

### Pip

- **Appearance:** The smallest Founder, with bright copper-brown ears and a
  length of red twine retied into several visible knots.
- **Voice:** Quick, loud, and often begun before the full thought is ready.
  Pip's urgency can sound cheerful or frightened without becoming less sincere.
- **Temperament:** Impulsive in the direction of other Citizens. Pip returns,
  calls, and looks again when prudence would make an absence easier to accept.
- **Habits and preferences:** Announces arrival from outside a room; touches
  familiar Citizens while passing; prefers crowded work and dislikes an
  unexplained silence.
- **Relationships:** Barley worries about Pip's disregard for a settled count.
  Thimble understands the work inside Pip's urgency. Husk can quiet Pip without
  issuing an instruction.
- **Keepsake:** Red twine carried from Chaff End and repeatedly repaired rather
  than replaced.
- **Established history:** Pip turned back during the Founding Escape to search
  for Citizens who did not emerge. The act remains part of Pip's Prior-Life Tale
  and later relationships; it creates no automatic rescue effect.

### Husk

- **Appearance:** A pale oat-colored Mouse with a narrow face and a hollow seed
  husk protected inside a scrap wrap.
- **Voice:** Quiet, spacious, and rarely repeated. Husk often answers by making
  room for another Citizen to continue.
- **Temperament:** Accommodating without being submissive. Husk notices unused
  space, unclaimed work, and the person who has not been included.
- **Habits and preferences:** Leaves one accessible empty place in crowded
  stores and gatherings; sleeps close to an outer edge; prefers company that
  does not require continuous speech.
- **Relationships:** Husk's quiet steadies Pip and interrupts Barley's tendency
  to turn care into accounting. Weft and Warp first find ordinary space in
  Rushbottom through Husk's attention.
- **Keepsake:** An empty seed husk taken from Chaff End's far store.
- **Established history:** Husk is the fourth Mouse to escape Chaff End, not a
  prior resident of Pipe End. Husk joins Thimble in the first Workshop's
  Builder Project so the two half Civic Shares demonstrate ordinary Mouse
  parity without pairing their identities.

# Book-wide continuity use

- The three civilizations recur as accumulated histories, not interchangeable
  names pasted onto isolated examples.
- Every new narrative use checks the present roster, life stage, relationships,
  possessions, injuries, Tier, Guest arrival, and established earlier events.
- Founders carry the greatest recurrence burden, while later Citizens prevent
  each civilization from feeling permanently frozen at its opening scale.
- Each civilization must receive meaningful Home, Away, MEET, species,
  progression, relationship, and Campaign Memory treatment across the book.
- Narrative balance is audited across the entire book. It does not require a
  rigid Rabbit–Squirrel–Mouse cycle within every Chapter.
- One civilization's worked example cannot silently settle a universal rule.
- Roles may change through the story. A recorded Role is civic history, not a
  fixed class, hidden aptitude, or character build.
- Guest mechanics always follow the current Chapter 4.3 roster. The identity
  notes above distinguish particular Citizens without adding another Signature
  or expanding the species contract.
- Canon images preserve the identities and established state recorded here.
  New setups may change pose, framing, light, and immediate situation while
  keeping bodies, belongings, relationships, and lasting consequences
  recognizable.
