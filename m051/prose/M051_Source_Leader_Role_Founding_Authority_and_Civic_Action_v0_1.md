# MEDIAN m051 Source
## Leader Role, Founding Authority, and Civic Action

**Artifact class:** Developmental Source
**Status:** Exploratory and non-authoritative
**Date:** August 9, 2026
**Target:** MEDIAN v0.5.1 and later development

> **MEDIAN v0.5.0 is locked. This Source preserves m051 development concerning
> the Leader Role. It does not amend m050 or independently establish m051
> canon. Its proposals remain available for authorial revision, testing,
> reconciliation, or rejection.**

---

# 1. Central proposition

The Leader may be the Colony's first civic institution.

```text
The Leader is the Town Center.
```

A founding Colony does not need a separate Town Center building or Town Center
Practice. One named Citizen initially carries the civic responsibility that a
larger Colony may later distribute through Practices, records, and
institutions.

Leader remains a Role rather than a class, aptitude, personality type, rank, or
permanent career. A Citizen assigned as Leader contributes one ordinary Home
Capacity. Leadership does not make that Citizen mechanically superior to the
other founders.

The Role may nevertheless differ from other Home Roles in one structural
respect: the Colony always has at least one unit of Leader Load. Leadership is
a founding obligation rather than a later contextual specialization.

---

# 2. The Founding Escape and the first Leader

## 2.1 Random founding trio

The Founding Escape begins with three randomly created starting Citizens. The
game does not ask the player to choose a Leader on a setup screen.

At an authored moment during the Escape, one randomly selected member of the
trio receives Focus. The sequence presents that Citizen as the one who carries
the immediate responsibility of keeping the three together and bringing the
group through the founding ordeal.

That Citizen begins Home as the Leader. Leader is the only preselected starting
Role. The player assigns the other Roles and may later replace or reassign the
Leader.

Focus establishes narrative and historical prominence. It does not reveal a
hidden leadership statistic, improve Home Capacity, or convert the Citizen
into a permanent protagonist.

## 2.2 The Hazel function

Every founding trio needs a Citizen who performs the narrative function that
Hazel performs in *Watership Down*: accepting responsibility for the group
before a formal institution exists to confer it.

The Source uses **Hazel function** as developmental shorthand, not as a required
personality template. The founding Leader need not be the strongest, boldest,
most charismatic, or most technically capable Citizen.

Bramble performs the Hazel function in New Elderbank. Sharpnose resembles
Bigwig, but that resemblance is incidental character history rather than a
required second founding archetype. Every trio needs a founding Leader; it does
not need a Bigwig equivalent.

## 2.3 Leadership remains transferable

The founding Leader's later reassignment does not erase the Founding Escape.
The Record, Chronicle, Tales, memories, and later presentation may continue to
recognize who first led the Colony Home.

The player may assign another Citizen as Leader whenever ordinary Role rules
permit. The player may also leave Leader vacant. Neither choice rewrites the
founding history.

---

# 3. Baseline Leader Load and vacancy

Leader may be the explicit exception to the usual rule that a Role is not
deficient merely because it exists and is unstaffed.

```text
Every Colony carries at least 1 Leader Load.
```

Under regular conditions, the weight of an unstaffed Leader deficit should
make leaving the Role vacant punitively unattractive. A leaderless Colony is a
permitted state, not a normal optimization.

The cost should enter the existing Pressure and deficiency machinery rather
than create a separate succession, politics, or governance subsystem. The
player-facing explanation should remain causal: shared commitments are not
being carried, collective responses are delayed, and Colony-wide decisions
lack an authorized civic voice.

Leader vacancy may still be meaningful during founding disruption, death,
absence, grief, deliberate refusal, or another authored transition. The
possibility remains because a Colony can become leaderless even though it
cannot remain comfortably so.

Home does not simulate individual political personalities, factions,
persuasion, private preferences, or interpersonal disputes. Named Citizens may
sweeten the presentation of Home events, but personality is not an input to
Home calculations.

---

# 4. The first Practice and the naming of Home

A Place is the physical circle on the ground. A Practice is the recognized use
established on that Place. Workshop and Gathering Place are Practices.

Gathering Place should be the first Practice constructed in the founding
Colony.

The initial Leader gives its earliest use a civic character. The three founders
return there to recognize common responsibilities, interpret immediate danger,
and make decisions that belong to the Colony rather than to one Citizen.

When construction of the Gathering Place is complete, the player may name the
Colony under the naming rules governed elsewhere. Naming is therefore an
earned civic act rather than a setup-screen field.

A Teacher need not be present in the earliest Colony. When population and
civic life grow enough to warrant a Teacher, Leader and Teacher may share the
Gathering Place just as Builder and Crafter may share Workshop.

Their uses remain distinct:

- Leader carries civic decision, commitment, and Colony-facing authority.
- Teacher carries Laws, Sayings, stories, instruction, memory, and cultural
  transmission.

The shared Practice does not require a separate Town Center Practice.

Council is not proposed as another standing Practice. A Council is a bounded
Home MEET or presentation form used when a Colony-level decision deserves
explicit player attention.

# 5. Positive Leader affordances

Staffing Leader must provide value beyond suppressing the Pressure caused by an
empty Role. Otherwise leadership becomes a compulsory tax that the player may
begrudge.

The proposed value is bounded civic agency rather than wildcard labor,
universal productivity, cross-Role Load reduction, or a personality bonus.

## 5.1 Early warning and preemptive Council

A qualifying early-warning Affordance may reveal the family of an approaching
World Pressure before the Colony commits its posture.

Groundhog's Seasonal Telegraph is the principal existing example. It may
identify hard frost rather than ordinary cold, flood rather than ordinary rain,
heat stress, disruptive human work, or another major seasonal turn without
giving exact timing or severity. Owl's Night Sky-watch is narrower and concerns
earlier or clearer warning of nocturnal predators or unusual night movement.

When qualifying warning exists and Leader is staffed, there may be a chance for
the Leader to call a preemptive Council MEET. The MEET lets the Colony spend an
existing resource on preparation before the Pressure resolves.

Effective preparation should cost less than the untreated event might wreak.
It need not erase the Pressure, guarantee silent success, or reveal exact
future consequences.

The warning Guest does not become a Leader and the Leader does not generate the
warning. The Guest makes the circumstance legible; the Leader may make
collective use of it.

The chance calculation, eligible warning families, preparation costs, and
possible reductions remain later tuning questions. They should use existing
state where possible rather than introduce a leadership aptitude or forecast
subsystem.

## 5.2 Colony-binding diplomatic commitments

The current Leader receives default starting Focus in a diplomatic Colony
MEET. Default Focus makes the Leader the presumptive civic speaker; it does not
exclude other Citizens from narrative participation.

A diplomatic choice that would bind the whole Colony may appear greyed out
when no Leader is assigned. Examples may include a Colony-wide Promise,
guarantee of refuge, continuing Terms, future assistance, access, or another
ongoing civic obligation.

When Leader is staffed, the option may become available. Choosing it creates
the ordinary Promise, relationship, Terms, or future obligation supported by
the relevant existing system. Leadership supplies authority to bind the
Colony; it does not remove the cost or consequence of the promise.

Formally receiving newcomers may be expressed through narrative and Focus. It
does not require a separate reception mechanic.

## 5.3 Tier transition

Leader should be required for every Leader-facing part of Tier transition.

The Colony must still satisfy every population, equilibrium, seasonal, civic,
Away or Network, and continuity proof required by the governing progression
model. Leader does not substitute for a missing proof, purchase advancement,
or manufacture readiness.

Once the proofs are present, the Leader may call or preside over the Council or
Gathering through which the Colony recognizes what it has become. The current
Leader may receive Focus during the transition, and the resulting history may
associate that Citizen with the recognized era.

The exact transition sequence remains governed by the progression system. This
Source proposes only that Tier recognition cannot proceed without a staffed
Leader.

# 6. Community Board and the Almanac

Community Board may be a discovered midgame Practice whose player-facing
function is to provide a diegetic viewport into the Almanac.

The Almanac describes current world and Colony conditions in a form that
remains to be developed. Community Board may make an appropriate subset of
those current conditions visible at Home.

Community Board should derive from the Almanac rather than duplicate or become
another authority for current state. Its exact contents, unlock, species
expressions, and interaction remain later questions.

Community Board does not replace the Leader, become a Town Center building,
create wildcard Capacity, or guarantee agreement. It externalizes current
civic and world information that an expanding Colony can no longer hold only
through immediate gathering and one Citizen's presence.

---

# 7. Explicit exclusions

This Source does not propose:

- a Town Center building or Town Center Practice;
- Council as another standing Practice;
- a permanent ruler, hereditary office, leadership class, or leadership
  aptitude;
- additional Home Capacity for the founding Leader;
- individual personality, faction, preference, persuasion, or dispute
  simulation at Home;
- a generic multi-Role mobilization mechanic;
- a generic Civic Priority subsystem;
- a separate founding-decision mechanic beyond the Founding Escape;
- formal newcomer reception mechanics;
- a separate Leader permission to begin ordinary major Projects;
- wildcard Capacity, universal productivity, or cross-Role Load reduction;
- a weather simulation, exact forecast, or guaranteed preemptive response;
- a second current-state ledger parallel to the Almanac.

Named Citizens, opinions, disagreement, ceremony, and institutional feeling may
appear in narrative presentation without becoming Home crunch inputs.

---

# 8. Open development questions

Later authorial development should determine:

1. The exact authored moment when the randomly selected founder receives Focus.
2. How each species presents the Hazel function without imposing one
   personality template.
3. The weight and timing of unstaffed Leader Pressure.
4. Whether the first Leader assignment is revealed immediately or only upon
   reaching Home.
5. Which warning Affordances can open preemptive Council and how chance is
   calculated from existing state.
6. The representative preparation costs and untreated consequences needed to
   verify that prevention is meaningfully cheaper.
7. The exact diplomatic obligations that require Colony-binding authority.
8. The precise Leader requirements at each Tier-transition step.
9. The Community Board discovery, Almanac contents, and species-specific
   presentation.
10. How the Record and later narrative remember founding and subsequent
    Leaders without creating permanent mechanical rank.

The proposal should advance only if Leader remains continuously important,
positively useful, narratively particular, mechanically bounded, and fully
compatible with the equal-Capacity Home architecture.
