## What to check

- **Is the body text a comfortable reading measure**, or does the three-column grid at 16:9 run too wide or too narrow?
- **Does the epigraph device work?** This tests the proposal that the Laws carry the book's connective tissue as section epigraphs.
- **Does the spot illustration sit *into* the page** or on top of it?
- **Does the page feel like a book or like a poster with more words?** That is the whole question.
