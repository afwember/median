# MEDIAN Phase 2 — Infographic Remake Handoff (new thread)

## A. Provisioning block (paste as the first message of the new thread)

> We are starting **Phase 2** of MEDIAN: the infographic and artwork remake pass. Design is settled; this thread is **production**, not design.
>
> **Project.** MEDIAN is an atmospheric animal colony-builder set on highway median strips — "Age of Empires minus combat, with the heart of *Watership Down*." The player guides one small civilization of rabbits, squirrels, or wood mice, builds a permanent low-population colony in the green island between carriageways, and risks named citizens on expeditions across live traffic and along a corridor of changing median biomes.
>
> **Current canon is `MEDIAN_GDD_v0.4.2.md`**, governed by a settled record of **85 design decisions**. It is the single source of truth for this phase. The existing infographic materials were built against **v0.4**, which is two revisions stale — v0.4.1 added Decisions 67–72, and v0.4.2 added 73–85 *and renumbered every section in the document*. Assume every cross-reference in the old Plan is dead until checked.
>
> **A standing caveat on the existing plates — read this before mining them for anything.** The **language and concepts** on the existing infographics were improvised by ChatGPT and **carry no canon weight whatsoever.** Labels, callout copy, headings, taxonomies, threat lists, sub-area names, and any concept a plate appears to assert are all unverified filler until checked against the GDD, and most of it will not survive that check. Do not treat a plate as evidence of anything.
>
> What *is* potentially valuable is the **visual** work: layouts and compositions, drawings of animals, objects, and places, info-box and callout structures, diagram forms, iconography, and framing devices. **Mine the plates for pictures and arrangement. Take nothing from them as fact.**
>
> **The task, in three stages. Do not skip ahead.**
>
> **Stage 1 — Amend the Infographic Plan to fit v0.4.2.** Read the GDD and the existing Plan in full, then produce an amended Plan. It must include a **plate-by-plate disposition table** with a verdict for every existing plate — *keep / revise / retire / supersede* — plus the new plates v0.4.2 requires. Tag each plate with its **visual pipeline** (see Stage 2), because which pipeline a plate belongs to is a plan-level fact, not a styling choice. Flag any plate whose subject matter is now an open design item rather than settled canon (Appendix D lists seventeen).
>
> **Stage 2 — Establish overall style conventions, in this order.** The sequence matters: each item constrains the ones after it.
>
> **2a. Anthropomorphism — first, before anything else.** How much clothing, carried equipment, upright posture, facial expression, and gesture the citizens get. This **gates every plate showing an animal**, which is most of them, and it is a genuinely open item in the GDD (§33.1, Appendix D) rather than something to look up. What is already settled: the citizens are heavily anthropomorphized *as characters* — names, families, grief, folklore, standing jobs — so a flat prohibition on clothing and carried objects sits oddly against that, and some amount of both goes a long way toward readable silhouettes and legible roles. The fixed constraints are that the world is not a costume drama, animals do not dress as miniature humans by default, and **nothing may read as arming a citizen**. The line itself is ours to draw here.
>
> **2b. Portrait versus landscape.** Plate orientation and dimensions, settled before any layout work begins. Decide it against two uses at once: plates stand alone, *and* they will eventually be embedded in the GDD, which is expected to roughly double in length as artwork lands. An orientation that works for one and fights the other is a problem discovered too late.
>
> **2c. Then everything else** — palette and color logic, overall vibe and tonal register, header and title treatment, typography, grid, iconography, labeling and callout conventions, caption voice, and how much text a plate is allowed to carry.
>
> Throughout, hold the **two visual languages** MEDIAN uses:
> - **Rendered-embodied** — the Colony, Encounter, and Crossing registers. Grounded stylized realism at animal scale; warm, tactile, storybook at home and colder and more cinematic away.
> - **Illustrated-surveyed** — the Field register. A naturalist's field-survey plate: warm parchment ground, painterly terrain, visible hand, node markers as pins over the illustration, the highway always in frame. Explicitly *not* schematic or austere.
>
> **Stage 3 — Iterate plate by plate**, working through the amended Plan in order. Reuse existing **visual** elements wherever they are worth keeping — layouts, drawings, info-box forms — and do not rebuild from zero out of habit. **All copy is written fresh from the GDD**; none of the existing plate text is reusable (see the caveat above).
>
> **Output format.** All infographics are produced as **layered, editable files — PowerPoint (.pptx) unless we decide otherwise**. Text must remain live text, never flattened into an image. This is a standing requirement recorded in the GDD (§33.1 and Appendix D) and it applies to the whole pass, not just the wordmark. Note the practical limit: any generated *illustration* inside a plate will be a raster layer; the editable requirement covers text, labels, callouts, and shape elements sitting over it.
>
> **Working discipline — this is the part that keeps the phase from sprawling.**
> - The GDD is authoritative. Where a plate and the GDD disagree, **the GDD wins** and the plate changes.
> - When a plate exposes a genuine design gap, **log it; do not solve it inline.** Maintain a running `MEDIAN_Phase2_art_raised_questions.md` that feeds a future GDD pass. Phase 2 must not quietly become another design thread.
> - Maintain a running `MEDIAN_Phase2_running_decisions.md` for style conventions and plate decisions as they lock, so the thread can be resumed or handed off without replaying the conversation.
>
> **Before producing anything:** read all uploaded assets in full, then propose your Stage 1 approach and get my confirmation.
>
> Keep my style: concise and direct.

## C. What changed since the Plan was written

Reference material for Stage 1. Not exhaustive — read the GDD — but these are the deltas most likely to invalidate an existing plate.

|---|---|

### Entirely new since v0.4 — likely to need new plates

The four registers · the Laws and Sayings of the Median · the Giants / Roaring Iron / Rivers of Thunder vocabulary · citizen and place naming systems · The Records (Almanac, Chronicle, Likeness, Tale) · Keepsake / Tool / Supply · Bonds, Hearths, and Nesting Season · wandering joiners · Choice-Event Cards · the Construction Queue · named building upgrade ladders · Traffic-as-Weather's named hazards (Wind Draft, the River's Spume, Litter) · the Edge Effect and Quiet Zone · Approaches, Turns, and exposure · fog of war on scouting · the Founding Escape as a multi-register sequence.

### Changed content in existing subject areas

- **Guest roster is 7, not 6** — the raccoon is added; the Deer Mouse is gone. Guests are now **option-openers**: each opens one Approach or Turn action and closes another.
- **Encounters are no longer a single roll.** One to three rounds, N−1 Turns, five Approaches (Contest, Evade, Parley, Yield, Withdraw), adversary as a *Presence* rather than a counted roster.
- **The Metropolis** is a Field territory with venues as nodes, and raccoons are its historic founding species — cultural standing only, never political control.
- **Outposts** clear fog on completion but only reduce contest risk through **outbuilding investment**.
- **The stat frame is integers** — carry 10 / 20 / 6, food 10 / 10 / 5.
- **The Home Median has a Field layer**, and the colony's name is distinct from the Median's generated folk name.

### Standing art constraints in v0.4.2

- **Layered, editable text on everything.** Never flattened. (§33.1, Appendix D)
- **No explicit blood or gore.** Catastrophe is sensory-obscured — dust, noise, distance, narrowed perception.
- **Human bodies stay off-screen**, except during Road Work and Founding-Escape-class construction threats. Vehicles are always visible.
- **Vehicle and machine flavor belongs throughout the world** — a wrecked car under an interchange, an engine block in the grass, a rusted piece of abandoned equipment, retread fragments. A Margin wreck can be a node.
- **Traffic is never off-screen** in any register.
- **Species is unified within a colony** and diverse only at the Metropolis. A species-tracking review across all artwork is an explicit Phase 2 task.
- **Wordmark and logomark** — cracked-asphalt and shattered-glass letterfill; companion "//" as mossy concrete lane-marking relief.

### Open items that gate specific plates

Settle these before drawing the affected plates rather than discovering them mid-plate:

- **The anthropomorphism line** (§33.1) — how much clothing and carried equipment serves character without turning the world into costume drama. **Gates every plate showing a citizen**, which is most of them. This is **Stage 2a — the first thing discussed**, before orientation and before any styling.
- **Portrait versus landscape** — not a GDD open item, but unsettled and structurally prior to all layout work. **Stage 2b.**
- **Field Mode art specification** — palette, line weight, label typography, and how a Reach's biome changes the plate's character. Stage 2c work for the surveyed pipeline.
- **Crossing** is the least-specified system in the GDD and is flagged for its own design pass. Plates depicting it should stay conceptual.
- **The Metropolis plaza name** and species-mix specifics are deferred to this phase. Candidate `"Exit 17 Pine Ridge"` is available but not locked.
- **Raccoon-heritage visual nods** in Metropolis architecture — deliberately unspecified, to be designed here.
- **The Founding Escape's in-fiction name** is still a placeholder.
