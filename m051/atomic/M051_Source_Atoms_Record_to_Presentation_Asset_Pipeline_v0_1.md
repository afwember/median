# 1. Unified architecture

### M051-RP-001 — Proposed

MEDIAN requires conceptual in-game asset channels for narrative and imagery.

**Prose reference:** 1.1

### M051-RP-002 — Proposed

The narrative channel turns authoritative action and state into experienced
language.

**Prose reference:** 1.1

### M051-RP-003 — Proposed

The imagery channel turns authoritative action and state into experienced
pictures.

**Prose reference:** 1.1

### M051-RP-004 — Proposed

Each asset channel requires a deterministic Baseline realization that remains
available without model services.

**Prose reference:** 1.1

### M051-RP-005 — Proposed

Each asset channel may support an optional Model-assisted realization that
enriches rather than enables play.

**Prose reference:** 1.1

### M051-RP-006 — Proposed

The two channels and two realization classes should share one deterministic
projection of relevant Record facts and world state rather than becoming four
independent pipelines.

**Prose reference:** 1.1

### M051-RP-007 — Proposed

The Record remains the sole factual authority for narrative and image outputs.

**Prose reference:** 1.2

### M051-RP-008 — Proposed

Narrative and imagery should be parallel interpretations of one bounded moment
rather than a serial chain in which prose embellishment becomes image fact.

**Prose reference:** 1.2

### M051-RP-009 — Proposed

A selected passage may guide image mood or emphasis without becoming the
image's factual authority.

**Prose reference:** 1.2

### M051-RP-010 — Proposed

Model output should not become a second carrier of persistent world state.

**Prose reference:** 1.2

### M051-RP-011 — Proposed

The deterministic Baseline is the complete reliable expression of the system
rather than a degraded emergency mode.

**Prose reference:** 1.3

### M051-RP-012 — Proposed

MEDIAN should remain narratively and visually coherent when model generation is
disabled, unavailable, late, invalid, declined, or unjustified by cost.

**Prose reference:** 1.3

### M051-RP-013 — Proposed

Model-assisted output may add variation and richness but may not hold essential
information or gate continued play.

**Prose reference:** 1.3

# 2. Moment Brief

### M051-RP-014 — Proposed

`Moment Brief` is a provisional name for the bounded presentation truth derived
from the Record and current world state.

**Prose reference:** 2.1

### M051-RP-015 — Proposed

The Moment Brief is a conceptual game-design handoff rather than a requirement
for a file, schema, service, or other implementation artifact.

**Prose reference:** 2.1

### M051-RP-016 — Proposed

A Moment Brief identifies what happened or remains bounded as possible, who is
present, where and when the moment occurs, and what can be perceived.

**Prose reference:** 2.1

### M051-RP-017 — Proposed

A Moment Brief may bind relevant history, relationships, possessions,
Conditions, hidden boundaries, narratorial tempo, attachment focus, deadline,
and possible Memory relevance.

**Prose reference:** 2.1–2.2

### M051-RP-018 — Proposed

A Moment Brief should remain only as large as its moment requires rather than
becoming a second campaign summary.

**Prose reference:** 2.2

### M051-RP-019 — Proposed

The Moment Brief derives from authority and carries no independent authority.

**Prose reference:** 2.3

### M051-RP-020 — Proposed

An unresolved branch in a Moment Brief does not become history until play
resolves and the Record commits the outcome.

**Prose reference:** 2.3

# 3. Narrative channel

### M051-RP-021 — Proposed

A deterministic narrative Baseline may combine authored sentence planning,
bounded clause families, and controlled realization.

**Prose reference:** 3.1

### M051-RP-022 — Proposed

Deterministic sentence plans may express structures such as orientation,
action, consequence, pressure, escape, discovery, Homecoming, and vista.

**Prose reference:** 3.1

### M051-RP-023 — Proposed

An authored deterministic clause is eligible only when its factual conditions
are present.

**Prose reference:** 3.1

### M051-RP-024 — Proposed

Controlled narrative realization may govern names, grammar, species attention,
tense, rhythm, repetition, vocabulary, and stable event-derived variation.

**Prose reference:** 3.1

### M051-RP-025 — Proposed

A deterministic passage should be reproducible from the same event and
narrative version.

**Prose reference:** 3.1

### M051-RP-026 — Proposed

The deterministic narrative Baseline provides a truth-bearing presentation
against which optional model output can be judged.

**Prose reference:** 3.1

### M051-RP-027 — Proposed

A language model should receive a bounded moment rather than independently
inspect the complete raw Record and decide what matters.

**Prose reference:** 3.2

### M051-RP-028 — Proposed

A Model-assisted narrative should remain constrained by the Moment Brief,
established voice, terminology, permitted knowledge, prohibited inference,
length, orientation, and the requirement not to add facts.

**Prose reference:** 3.2

### M051-RP-029 — Proposed

A Model-assisted passage may replace its Baseline only when it arrives before
the experiential deadline and remains consistent with the bounded moment.

**Prose reference:** 3.2

### M051-RP-030 — Proposed

Unknown names, invented objects, hidden information, premature outcomes,
unsupported emotions, and mechanical contradictions invalidate a
Model-assisted passage.

**Prose reference:** 3.2

### M051-RP-031 — Proposed

Landscape Voice may use this narrative channel to alter situated attention and
tempo without altering truth.

**Prose reference:** 3.3

# 4. Imagery channel

### M051-RP-032 — Proposed

The deterministic visual Baseline should depict the actual moment through
repeatable composition rather than require a unique painting for every event.

**Prose reference:** 4.1

### M051-RP-033 — Proposed

A deterministic visual composition may use actual location state, camera,
Citizen presence and Condition, significant objects, weather, light, occlusion,
animal-scale viewpoint, framing, and restrained event treatment.

**Prose reference:** 4.1

### M051-RP-034 — Proposed

When reusable world rendering is unavailable, layered two-dimensional assets
may provide the deterministic visual Baseline.

**Prose reference:** 4.1

### M051-RP-035 — Proposed

The deterministic visual Baseline prioritizes truthful immediacy over unlimited
novelty.

**Prose reference:** 4.1

### M051-RP-036 — Proposed

Model-assisted imagery may use image-to-image generation without treating the
last generated illustration as sole canonical visual reference.

**Prose reference:** 4.2

### M051-RP-037 — Proposed

Repeated transformation of the last generated image risks accumulating errors
in identity, architecture, object count, terrain, and composition.

**Prose reference:** 4.2

### M051-RP-038 — Proposed

Model-assisted imagery should re-anchor visual continuity from stable Citizen
references, location references, deterministic composition, visual guidance,
object references, and the Moment Brief.

**Prose reference:** 4.2

### M051-RP-039 — Proposed

A prior generated image may contribute continuity but may not carry visual
truth by itself.

**Prose reference:** 4.2

### M051-RP-040 — Proposed

The Moment Brief may produce bounded art direction directly without requiring
another language-model pass.

**Prose reference:** 4.3

### M051-RP-041 — Proposed

An optional language-model art-direction pass may improve composition while
remaining bound to required and prohibited facts.

**Prose reference:** 4.3

### M051-RP-042 — Proposed

A generated image presents state but does not determine state.

**Prose reference:** 4.3

# 5. Predictive preparation

### M051-RP-043 — Proposed

Model generation may begin before presentation when the possible outcomes are
already bounded.

**Prose reference:** 5.1

### M051-RP-044 — Proposed

Player deliberation during an Encounter may provide preparation time for
candidate narrative and visual assets.

**Prose reference:** 5.1

### M051-RP-045 — Proposed

Unused predictive branches remain speculative assets rather than alternate
histories.

**Prose reference:** 5.1

### M051-RP-046 — Proposed

Unused predictive branches must not enter the Record, Memories, Tales, or
Chronicle.

**Prose reference:** 5.1

### M051-RP-047 — Proposed

Fully bounded outcome options may receive complete candidate passages during
player deliberation.

**Prose reference:** 5.2

### M051-RP-048 — Proposed

After selection, a candidate presentation must still match the committed
outcome before it becomes eligible for presentation or memory.

**Prose reference:** 5.2

### M051-RP-049 — Proposed

An outcome with unresolved rolls, discoveries, injuries, costs, or consequences
should not receive a falsely complete predictive account.

**Prose reference:** 5.3

### M051-RP-050 — Proposed

An unresolved branch may prepare orientation, attempted action, and a bounded
structure while holding its consequence open for deterministic completion.

**Prose reference:** 5.3

### M051-RP-051 — Proposed

Predictive preparation must not become predictive fiction.

**Prose reference:** 5.3

### M051-RP-052 — Proposed

Predictive preparation may treat narrative and imagery asymmetrically because
full generated images are generally slower and more expensive than short text.

**Prose reference:** 5.4

### M051-RP-053 — Proposed

A conservative Encounter may prepare one shared establishing image,
deterministic option compositions, candidate narrative passages, and a selected
Model-assisted image only after resolution.

**Prose reference:** 5.4

### M051-RP-054 — Proposed

A later valid illustration may serve Memory, Tale, mailing, Colony view, or
Chronicle without delaying the immediate mode transition.

**Prose reference:** 5.4

# 6. Deadline, fallback, and persistence

### M051-RP-055 — Proposed

Every optional asset request has an experiential deadline.

**Prose reference:** 6.1

### M051-RP-056 — Proposed

The deterministic Baseline appears when Model-assisted output has not arrived
and validated by the experiential deadline.

**Prose reference:** 6.1

### M051-RP-057 — Proposed

The imagined game should never wait for optional model enrichment.

**Prose reference:** 6.1

### M051-RP-058 — Proposed

A late Model-assisted result should not silently replace the passage or image
the player already experienced.

**Prose reference:** 6.2

### M051-RP-059 — Proposed

A late valid result may be discarded or presented as a later retelling,
mailing, Colony display, Tale illustration, Chronicle illustration, or
commemorative view.

**Prose reference:** 6.2

### M051-RP-060 — Proposed

An invalid model output is unavailable and does not alter the Record or create
another player-facing failure system.

**Prose reference:** 6.3

### M051-RP-061 — Proposed

Only the resolved selected moment becomes eligible for the existing Memories
process.

**Prose reference:** 7.1

### M051-RP-062 — Proposed

A preserved experience should remain traceable to its Record facts,
participants, Place, outcome, realization class, and exact experienced asset.

**Prose reference:** 7.2

### M051-RP-063 — Proposed

Presentation provenance preserves correspondence without making narration or
imagery authoritative.

**Prose reference:** 7.2

### M051-RP-064 — Proposed

Existing salience distinctions determine whether a moment remains personal,
contributes to a Tale, or qualifies for the Chronicle.

**Prose reference:** 7.3

# 8. Conservation of System

### M051-RP-081 — Proposed

The pipeline conserves system by deriving multiple expressive outputs from one
bounded interpretation of existing authority.

**Prose reference:** 10

### M051-RP-082 — Proposed

The pipeline reuses the Record, world state, Landscape Voice, deterministic
presentation, optional models, existing transitions, and existing Memory
structures.

**Prose reference:** 10

### M051-RP-083 — Proposed

The pipeline should not create separate narrative and image authorities,
model-only game information, a second Record, a parallel Memories system,
alternate branch histories, or generation-dependent continuation.

**Prose reference:** 10

### M051-RP-084 — Proposed

Conservation of System can support greater artistic ambition by finding one
small common structure capable of multiplying expressive forms safely.

**Prose reference:** 10
