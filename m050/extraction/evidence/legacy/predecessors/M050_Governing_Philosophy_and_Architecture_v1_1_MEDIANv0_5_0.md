# MEDIAN v0.5.0
## Governing Philosophy and Architecture

**The Attachment-Forward Colony Game**

> **MEDIAN is an animal-colony base-building game set in the living median strips of active highways.**

> **Build one permanent sanctuary. Send named Citizens beyond its safety. Bring consequence Home. Remember what happened.**

This document states what MEDIAN is and what structural form it must retain in order to remain MEDIAN. It is divided into three constitutional Books:

1. **Philosophy** — the values and game-wide tests that constrain every system;
2. **Architecture** — the fixed structural ontology through which those values become play;
3. **Governing Domain Doctrines** — concise statements locating the major systems without duplicating their dedicated specifications.

Where this document summarizes a dedicated system, the summary explains that system’s place in the whole. It does not become a second mechanical owner.

# BOOK I — PHILOSOPHY

`Philosophy.Philosophy`

Philosophy answers why MEDIAN must work this way. It contains the game’s central thesis, global Pillars, governing Principles, and protections against recognizable forms of design drift.

# I. The Game in One Breath

## A civilization where nobody else would call Home

MEDIAN is a game about creating, endangering, experiencing, and remembering sanctuary.

A small animal civilization makes a permanent settlement in the median strip between active highway carriageways. The player builds and tends that settlement, sends named animals beyond its safety, answers the situations they meet, and brings discoveries, materials, relationships, wounds, obligations, and memories back Home.

The settlement is the **Home Median**. The community living there is the **Colony**. Every named member of that community is a **Citizen**. Mouse, Rabbit, and Squirrel colonies build distinct forms of civilization; animals from other species may be welcomed as equal Citizens.

MEDIAN’s central dramatic promise is not conquest. It is return. Home matters because someone may have to leave it. Leaving matters because it changes particular lives. Those changes remain present when the road becomes warm, close, and familiar again.

> **SANCTUARY → EXPOSURE → CONSEQUENCE → RETURN → MEMORY**

## The two broad conditions of play

A **Mode** says where the game’s primary life and responsibility are situated.

| Mode | Primary subject | What the player does |
|---|---|---|
| **Home Mode** | The Colony | Build, tend, organize, prepare, understand, and inhabit the permanent settlement. |
| **Away Mode** | Particular Citizens and the expedition party | Cross roads, travel the corridor, meet the wider world, endure consequence, and bring something back. |

> **Home Mode is where the player builds and tends the Colony. Away Mode is where named Citizens leave the safety of Home to bring something back.**

# II. The Invented Genre

## The attachment-forward base builder

Attachment is not an ornamental reward. It is the direction in which the design points.

Most base builders treat the settlement primarily as a machine, a shield, or a platform for expansion. MEDIAN treats the base as an inhabited social protagonist: one particular place whose systems make particular lives legible and worth returning to.

The Colony is not consumed by progression. Progress accumulates around it.

| Orientation | The base is understood as… | Success tends toward… |
|---|---|---|
| Production-forward | A machine whose parts generate throughput. | Acceleration, efficiency, expansion, and replacement. |
| Survival-forward | A defensive apparatus holding collapse at bay. | Continued existence, recovery, and postponement of disaster. |
| **Attachment-forward** | A particular inhabited place with particular lives and history. | Stable ordinary life, accumulated meaning, and chosen risk on behalf of what matters. |

## The four anchors of attachment

A strong MEDIAN system strengthens one or more of four relationships:

- **Home** — the permanent place the player has made, learned, protected, and returned to;
- **Citizen** — a named life with prior history, present relationships, and a possible future;
- **Place or object** — a material thing whose use, location, and provenance have become particular;
- **Memory** — a fact that persists because it mattered, not because it grants a modifier.

> **The mechanics are arranged so that the player always has someone and somewhere to come back to.**

## The attachment chain

> **LEGIBILITY → COMPETENCE → STABILITY → ATTENTION → ATTACHMENT**

> **ATTACHMENT → CHOSEN RISK → CONSEQUENCE → RETURN → DEEPER ATTACHMENT**

Mechanical rigor and tenderness are not opposites. A Colony that never becomes comprehensible cannot become beloved. A Colony that never becomes stable cannot be inhabited emotionally. The design must first satisfy the player’s need for Game Logic; only then can attention open toward ordinary lives, familiar Places, and accumulated memory.

# III. The Eight Global Pillars

Global Pillars are game-wide tests. A system may emphasize some more than others, but no major system may casually contradict them.

## 1. Attachment-forward base-building

`Philosophy.Pillar.AttachmentForwardBaseBuilding`

The Home Median is the permanent emotional and mechanical center of the campaign. It is improved, densified, decorated, damaged, repaired, inhabited, and remembered rather than replaced.

Corridor growth creates a network around Home. It does not turn the founding Colony into an obsolete tutorial settlement.

## 2. Named Citizens, low population, durable identity

`Philosophy.Pillar.NamedCitizens`

Every Citizen is individually known. Population remains small enough for every name to count. Core-species and Guest origins share one equal class of citizenship.

Loss, bravery, habit, relationship, disability, inheritance, and prior life matter because Citizens are companions and community members, not replenishable unit tokens.

## 3. Ledger and legible

`Philosophy.Pillar.LedgerAndLegible`

Important state should be perceptible in the world and also available in clear functional form. MEDIAN rejects both opaque sentiment and spreadsheet abstraction.

The player should understand what is known, what remains uncertain, and why a decision matters without reducing every personal truth to a public number.

> **Atmosphere is not an excuse for obscurity. Legibility is what lets the player make a place their own.**

## 4. Home grows the Colony; Away grows and hazards Citizens

`Philosophy.Pillar.HomeGrowsColonyAwayGrowsCitizen`

Home develops collective capability: Places, Practices, reserves, settlement form, culture, Readiness, population, and civic continuity.

Away develops and changes particular Citizens through bodily danger, equipment, relationships, discovered access, wounds, Distinctions, obligations, and remembered acts.

Expeditions make stories and enable progress because return causes these two forms of growth to alter one another.

> **Home grows the Colony. Away grows and hazards Citizens. Return makes each answer the other.**

## 5. Consequence without disposability

`Philosophy.Pillar.ConsequenceWithoutDisposability`

Danger changes lives before it erases them. Fear, wounds, Maiming, Distinctions, Keepsakes, After-names, separation, altered relationships, and changed capability carry consequence Home.

Death remains possible, but the game does not treat Citizens as expendable inputs or convert their loss into routine inventory management.

Exposure is the accumulating burden of danger, distance, uncertainty, and prolonged alertness. **Tharn** is not merely a high value on an Exposure track; it is an acute categorical break that requires protection, care, and time. Sanctuary may permit recovery, but ordinary life is not a trauma-clearing minigame.

## 6. Conservation of Systems

`Philosophy.Pillar.ConservationOfSystems`

Fit new meaning into existing systems wherever possible. New expression should preferentially arise through context, permission, combination, species interpretation, manifestation, memory, or promotion of an existing rule—not through another meter, currency, menu, progression track, or exception procedure.

Conservation is not minimalism for its own sake. It creates a small grammar the player can understand deeply while the world continues to produce expressive variety.

> **Tabletop clarity sits beneath lived civilization.**

## 7. One world, two Modes, five Registers

`Philosophy.Pillar.OneWorldTwoModesFiveRegisters`

**Colony, Field, Crossing, Encounter, and Embodiment** are not separate games. They are five Registers through which the player relates to one persistent world, distributed across Home and Away.

Their operators are **DWELL, TRAVEL, RISK, MEET, and EMBODY**. A Register is the structural form of play; its operator is the all-caps expression through which the player acts.

The same Citizen, Place, object, route, weather, wound, promise, or memory may appear differently in each Register without becoming a different fact. Transitions are emotional beats, not neutral load screens.

## 8. Game Logic precedes Attachment

`Philosophy.Pillar.GameLogicPrecedesAttachment`

Attachment Space cannot open while the player remains preoccupied by unresolved mechanical uncertainty. The player must understand the Colony, make sound decisions, and believe that safety or readiness has been mechanically achieved before the game can reasonably ask them to linger, observe, remember, or care.

This is a causal sequence:

- legibility enables competence;
- competence enables stability;
- stability releases attention;
- released attention makes attachment possible.

> **The player must first believe the game before they can emotionally inhabit it.**

### Constitutional consequence: attachment-forward systems must do Game Logic work

Every substantial attachment-forward system must possess a genuine game-mechanical function. It must alter what can be understood, chosen, prepared, risked, protected, built, carried, restored, remembered, or brought Home.

This does not require every quiet moment to issue a reward or modifier. Ephemeral narrative texture may exist without instrumental payoff. But anything presented as a repeatable system, meaningful preparation, sustained mode of attention, or consequential player choice must justify that attention within the game’s logic.

Sentiment unsupported by Game Logic feels ornamental. Game Logic that never releases attention leaves no room for sentiment.

# IV. Governing Principles and Protections

Guiding Principles rank below the Global Pillars. They govern particular domains and resolve design choices without creating new systems merely to restate the philosophy.

## Citizenship and identity

- All Citizens are classed alike. Core and Guest species use one equal Citizen architecture.
- Arrival does not create personhood. Every named arrival already belongs to a prior life.
- A Home Role is a civic assignment, not a character class, caste, or aptitude ranking.
- Identity may matter without producing a modifier. Habit, grief, relationship, memory, and provenance do not require a buff to justify persistence.
- A Citizen remains the same person across every Mode and Register, even when the scale of mechanical attention changes.

## Choice over incidental outcome

Consequential play should center on what the player chooses to protect, risk, spend, accept, reinterpret, or relinquish.

Randomness and simulation may shape the world, but important consequences should not feel like incidental trivia that occurred while the player was looking elsewhere.

## Group resolution and Focus

The Colony or expedition answers a consequential situation together. The player chooses the group’s Approach; the scene may then follow one Citizen as the natural temporary protagonist of that choice. This temporary protagonism is **Focus**.

> **The group resolves the situation. Focus decides whose life the moment belongs to.**

Focus follows the chosen Approach through a relevant Tool, Keepsake, relationship, or authored circumstance. It does not create an option merely so that a preferred Citizen can be selected as leader.

## Memory

- History persists because it mattered, not because it grants a bonus.
- The Chronicle preserves selected civic history.
- Each Citizen’s Tale preserves the shape of one life.
- Campaign Memory supplies continuity across Modes and Registers without turning every remembered event into progression.
- Ephemerality is permitted. A quiet moment may be valuable without entering a permanent log.
- Memory must remain grounded in what actually occurred and what still exists in the world.

## Objects and attachment

- Persistent named objects are simple to use but not entirely anonymous.
- Tools express ordinary capability and assumed identity.
- Keepsakes express personal meaning.
- Special Artifacts express civic history.
- Provenance and consequential use create attachment more reliably than rarity tiers or numerical upgrades.
- Named and meaning-bearing objects require legible scenes when they are permanently endangered, transferred, or lost.

## Care

- Care must not collapse into a buff economy.
- The player should sometimes attend to a Citizen because that life matters.
- A Citizen retains expressive ownership. The player may accompany, prepare, choose, and protect, but cannot command affection or exact emotion.

## Presentation

- Camera expresses the player’s relationship to the world; it is not merely a zoom level.
- Folk meaning and functional truth should remain visible together.
- The world should be atmospheric without hiding its operation.
- The interface should present meaningful available choices rather than every theoretically possible action.
- Gameplay visibility may use privileged transparency without implying that the world is literally open or exposed.

> **Open to the player is not open to the world.**

## Design protections

- **Protect attachment chains.** Feature density is not progress unless a new element strengthens Home, a Citizen, a Place or object, or accumulated memory.
- **Make quiet mechanically credible.** Endless entropy prevents the player from noticing the life being protected.
- **Protect player attention.** Use comprehensible accounting, compressed state, and situated scenes rather than continuous alarm management.
- **Keep Home collective and Away particular.** Do not import productivity aptitude into Home or flatten Away consequence into anonymous party math.
- **Turn deficiency into situated consequence.** Do not charge the same shortfall through constant damage, escalating frequency, severity, and compulsory repair without a major authored reason.
- **Use existing grammar before inventing a subsystem.** Conservation of Systems is the default response to new content.
- **Require Game Logic from attachment-forward systems.** A sustained or repeatable attachment feature must perform real mechanical work without reducing care to a reward economy.
- **Care must not become an optimization chore.** Meaning is allowed to remain non-instrumental.
- **Ecology informs rather than dictates.** Bodily credibility should sharpen civilization without hiding the game or reducing culture to zoology.
- **Operational clarity leads literal concealment.** The player must be able to understand the Colony they are being asked to build and love.
- **Comparability is useful; convergence is not automatically progress.** A familiar genre convention must be tested against what it would erase.
- **Conceptual validity is not identical to immediate production capacity.** A manifestation should be coherent, dramaturgically supported, heartfelt, and technically plausible; present staffing limits do not by themselves define the game’s imaginative ceiling.

# BOOK II — ARCHITECTURE

`Architecture.Architecture`

Architecture answers what MEDIAN’s fundamental structural parts are and how they relate. Architecture does not contain every system. It defines the constitutional frame within which systems receive their substantive MSIDs and dedicated owners.

# V. One Persistent World

MEDIAN contains one persistent world rather than five disconnected games. Registers alter the scale, pace, subject, and form of player attention; they do not create separate versions of a Citizen, Place, route, object, wound, promise, or memory.

A fact learned in one Register remains a fact elsewhere. A consequence incurred Away returns Home. A Place built through Colony remains available to Embodiment. An event resolved through Encounter may alter Field, Crossing, Colony, or a Citizen’s Tale.

> **Registers reveal and act upon one world. They do not partition reality.**

# VI. The Two Modes

A **Mode** is one of the game’s two broad conditions. It says where the game’s primary life and responsibility are situated.

## Home Mode

`Architecture.Mode.Home`

Home Mode is the condition in which the player’s primary responsibility is the founding Colony and the permanent sanctuary it creates. Its substantive domain is `Home.Home`.

## Away Mode

`Architecture.Mode.Away`

Away Mode is the condition in which the player’s primary responsibility is a party of particular Citizens beyond the sanctuary of Home. Its substantive domain is `Away.Away`.

| Mode | Primary subject | Primary action | What can grow or change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Home** | The Colony | Build, tend, organize, prepare, understand, and inhabit. | Places, Practices, reserves, culture, Readiness, population, and collective capability. |
| **Away** | Particular Citizens and the expedition party | Travel, cross, meet, choose, endure, and return. | Equipment, relationships, discovered access, wounds, Distinctions, obligations, and personal history. |

# VII. The Five Registers

A **Register** is a distinct structural form of play. It tells the player what kind of attention is required now. A Register is defined by subject, action, pace, dramatic function, and resolution contract—not by one mandatory camera or interface.

Each Register has:

- a concise architectural MSID under `Architecture.Register.*`;
- one or more substantive Home/Away MSIDs where its game properties live;
- an all-caps operator through which the player acts.

| Register | Architectural MSID | Substantive MSID | Operator | Mode | Primary relationship |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Colony** | `Architecture.Register.Colony` | `Home.Colony` | **DWELL** | Home | Understand, build, and arrange collective life. |
| **Field** | `Architecture.Register.Field` | `Away.Field` | **TRAVEL** | Away | Move through and read extended territory while carrying particular lives through distance and environmental change. |
| **Crossing** | `Architecture.Register.Crossing` | `Away.Crossing` | **RISK** | Away | Preserve life through committed timing, route, burden, refuge, and motion across immediate danger. |
| **Encounter** | `Architecture.Register.Encounter` | `Home.Encounter`; `Away.Encounter` | **MEET** | Home or Away | Answer a bounded consequential situation through visible stakes, constrained choice, cost, and aftermath. |
| **Embodiment** | `Architecture.Register.Embodiment` | `Home.Embodiment` | **EMBODY** | Home | Experience ordinary animal life made possible by achieved sanctuary. |

A manifestation may translate these relationships through screens, boards, cards, narration, touch, or other procedures. The interface may change. The dramatic function must remain recognizable.

## Register and operator are not interchangeable

The Register is the noun-form structural object. The operator is the all-caps expression of player action.

```text
Colony       : DWELL
Field        : TRAVEL
Crossing     : RISK
Encounter    : MEET
Embodiment   : EMBODY
```

A development source may use an operator as though it were the Register name. Such wording is historical terminology, not controlling ontology.

## Register topology

- **Colony** and **Embodiment** belong intrinsically to Home.
- **Field** and **Crossing** belong intrinsically to Away.
- **Encounter** is cross-modal and takes substantive form under both Home and Away.
- A borrowed presentation does not change Mode or Register identity. An Away Stopover may use a Muted DWELL composition without becoming Colony or Home.

> **Danger makes sanctuary necessary. Colony makes sanctuary functional. Embodiment makes sanctuary felt. Memory makes sanctuary personal.**

# VIII. The Home–Away–Return Cycle

`Architecture.Cycle.HomeAwayReturn`

The campaign’s governing cycle is:

> **HOME → LAUNCH → FIELD → CROSSING / ENCOUNTER → RETURN → HOMECOMING → HOME**

This is not a mandatory fixed sequence of screens. It is the constitutional dramatic circuit:

- Home establishes what matters and prepares particular Citizens;
- Away places those Citizens beyond sanctuary;
- Field carries them through extended territory;
- Crossing and Encounter create commitment and consequence;
- return preserves burden rather than erasing it;
- Homecoming reintegrates material, civic, bodily, relational, and remembered outcomes;
- the changed Colony becomes the ground of the next choice.

Home remains permanent. Corridor expansion, Outposts, Stopovers, and distant relationships do not supersede or duplicate the founding sanctuary.

# BOOK III — GOVERNING DOMAIN DOCTRINES

These sections give the major substantive domains their constitutional place. They do not replace dedicated specifications.

# IX. Home

`Home.Home`

Home is not merely a safe location. It is the permanent civic condition produced by the founding Colony in the Home Median.

The player’s task is not to prevent perpetual collapse. It is to establish and periodically restore the material, civic, and social conditions under which ordinary life can continue.

Good Home play produces **successful stillness**: a broad, credible plateau in which the player no longer needs to intervene continuously. Stability is not a failure of pacing. It is an achieved condition that releases attention for ambition, observation, companionship, and attachment.

Persistent capacities remain global. Acute consequences become situated in a particular Place, household, relationship, or scene. Shortfall creates exposure to trouble; it need not inflict automatic damage merely because another Dawn has arrived.

## Colony

`Home.Colony`

The Colony is the Home crunch subject. Through **DWELL**, the player understands and arranges collective life: Places, Practices, Projects, Roles, resources, Readiness, civic continuity, and preparation.

Citizens contribute equal, named shares of civic commitment. Home Roles are assignments of responsibility rather than hidden productivity aptitudes. Ordinary life is not idle production capacity; rest, conversation, play, and familiar routine are parts of the civilization being built.

Colony is legible as one inhabited landscape. Its characteristic elevated three-quarter presentation reads the settlement’s form and relationships without implying that walls, roofs, brush, or foliage are absent from the world.

## Embodiment

`Home.Embodiment`

Embodiment is the intimate Home Register through which achieved sanctuary becomes perceptible at animal scale. The player acts through **EMBODY**.

Embodiment is available because the Colony has made ordinary life possible, not merely because no enemy is visible at this instant. Home establishes the global permission; a particular Citizen, Place, Practice, relationship, or sensory condition supplies the point of entry.

### Practice and Presence

| Form | Player relation | Typical expression |
|---|---|---|
| **Practice** | Participate in a bounded animal-scale activity already contained within ordinary Colony life. | Move along a Squirrel route, help with familiar stores, pass through a Mouse wing, or bound across a Rabbit court. |
| **Presence** | Accompany or witness a Citizen through repose, play, tenderness, tension release, or sensory grace. | Watch weather from a familiar Place, listen to rain at a hearth, bask in a court, or settle at a junction. |

Embodiment favors authored, bounded experiences with a beginning, sensory focus, small change, and natural release. Direct control may be light or kinetic, but Embodiment is not a general traversal sandbox, collectible hunt, or second optimization game.

Embodiment must perform Game Logic work when it becomes a substantial or repeatable system, but it must not become mandatory optimization. It may make a Place, Practice, relationship, route, recovery state, or ordinary capability newly legible without secretly producing superior resources, exclusive healing, or compulsory relationship bonuses.

The experience is valuable because the Citizen’s life is valuable. Most Embodiment moments may remain ephemeral; significance, not completionism, determines whether one enters Campaign Memory.

# X. Away

`Away.Away`

Away is where named Citizens leave the safety of Home to bring something back. The Citizen is the Away crunch subject. Equipment, bodily difference, fear, history, and consequence resolve through particular lives.

The expedition is a collective undertaking whose consequences remain particular. Most Citizens come Home; not all come Home unchanged. Withdrawal remains meaningful. The player may preserve lives by yielding an objective rather than being forced into all-or-nothing heroics.

No irreversible personal loss should occur off-screen. The player must be present when a named life is permanently changed or lost.

## Field

`Away.Field`

Field is the Away Register of extended territory, route, distance, and environmental reading. The player acts through **TRAVEL**.

Field carries particular Citizens through the corridor. It reveals territory, conditions, routes, Nodes, opportunities, and changing ecology through movement rather than through a detached Knowledge currency.

Its characteristic presentation may be a naturalist survey, route plate, map, corridor view, or another manifestation-appropriate form. Presentation may compress known or safe ground, but it must not erase burden, distance, or the meaningful conditions of return.

## Crossing

`Away.Crossing`

Crossing is one of MEDIAN’s five Registers. The player acts through **RISK**. Highway is Crossing’s defining world manifestation.

Crossing concerns committed action inside immediate spatial danger: observation, route selection, timing, commitment, motion, refuge, burden, and whole-party consequence. Its phases and processes live under the substantive MSID, including units such as:

```text
Away.Crossing.Phase
Away.Crossing.Phase.Observe
Away.Crossing.Process
Away.Crossing.Process.Jostle
```

Crossing is uniquely forked by Core Species in a way no other Register is. Mouse, Rabbit, and Squirrel bodies do not merely receive modifiers to one universal crossing procedure; each species produces a distinct grammar of safety, route, timing, and motion.

```text
Away.Crossing.Mouse
Away.Crossing.Rabbit
Away.Crossing.Squirrel
```

The dedicated Crossing specification owns exact phases, execution grammar, adversity, species forks, and tuned procedures.

# XI. Encounter

`Home.Encounter`

`Away.Encounter`

Encounter is the cross-modal Register through which a bounded condition in the world becomes a consequential choice. The player acts through **MEET**.

> **MEET is the moment when a condition in the world becomes a choice the player must answer.**

A Colony may meet heavy wind, illness, floodwater, a dispute, a stranger, or a threatened Place. An expedition may meet a blocked route, predator, community, resource, demand, injury, or unexpected obligation.

“Meeting” does not require two opposing characters. It means that a bounded circumstance has become consequential enough to deserve the player’s judgment.

## Why one resolver matters

Encounter is a central expression of Conservation of Systems. MEDIAN does not require separate primary subsystems for negotiation, predation, storms, civic disputes, resource opportunities, structural failure, hospitality, rescue, and aftermath.

They share a common grammar:

- visible stakes;
- meaningful but constrained choices;
- cost or commitment;
- consequence;
- changed world state;
- remembered aftermath when warranted.

A MEET scene asks what the Colony or party will protect, pursue, surrender, spend, reinterpret, or accept. It is not a broad tactical command bar and not a combat engine with peaceful scenes reskinned around it.

Home Encounter and Away Encounter share a Register but not necessarily identical transaction procedures. Home situations may remain open and involve Colony-scale civic commitments. Away situations generally resolve under the immediate presence and limited capacity of the party.

| Domain | Example circumstance | What the choice concerns |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Heavy winds threaten a roofline and exposed stores. | Which value receives scarce civic attention, and what consequence is accepted elsewhere? |
| Away | A stranger controls access to a needed route. | What relationship, obligation, resource, or risk will the party accept? |
| Away | An uncontested food source is larger than the party can safely carry. | What is recovered, what is left, and what return burden is chosen? |
| Home | A newcomer’s arrival exposes disagreement inside the Colony. | How belonging, hospitality, memory, and limited capacity are reconciled. |

# XII. Species Personality and Spatial Preference

## Three civilizations built from three bodily ideas of safety

Mouse, Rabbit, and Squirrel are not cosmetic factions or alternate stat packages. Each body reads the same median differently.

> **BODY → PERCEPTION OF SAFETY → SPATIAL PREFERENCE → CIVILIZATIONAL PERSONALITY**

| Species | Operator | Bodily idea of safety | Spatial and social personality | Civilizational promise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mouse** | **JOIN** | Contact, enclosure, adjacency, and continuous passage. | Domestic accumulation; attached rooms; short covered links; shared walls; inward warmth; many lives belonging to one inhabited body. | There is room for you under our roof. |
| **Rabbit** | **GATHER** | Mutual visibility, shared ground, nearby concealment, and collective movement. | Neighborhood life; courts and greens; entrances facing common space; social openness held against protective edges. | We will make another neighborhood for you. |
| **Squirrel** | **CONNECT** | Reachability, elevated refuge, alternate routes, and non-isolated nodes. | Route-minded civilization; platforms, bridges, caches, junctions, redundancy, lookout, and confidence across separation. | We will bring you into reach. |

JOIN, GATHER, and CONNECT influence how each civilization imagines privacy, care, council, storage, hospitality, warning, celebration, grief, and expansion.

Spatial preference becomes cultural metaphor:

- a Mouse promise sounds like enclosure and belonging;
- a Rabbit promise sounds like neighborhood and company;
- a Squirrel promise sounds like route and reach.

Real-world ecology supplies bodily priors and design prompts, not literal laws for fictional civilization. Ecology helps identify what each species’ body reads as safety, danger, route, refuge, and usable structure. MEDIAN’s cultures translate those priors into architecture, movement, memory, hospitality, civic Practice, and political life.

> **Ecology suggests what safety feels like. Civilization decides what to build, remember, and promise from it.**

# XIII. The World, Its Tone, and the Player

## Modern infrastructure, animal-scale civilization

### The highway is not scenery

MEDIAN takes place inside active human infrastructure, not after its abandonment.

Traffic, maintenance, noise, runoff, heat, salt, wind, vibration, barriers, drainage, verge growth, and human work continually reorganize animal life. The highway is barrier, climate, resource edge, world manifestation of Crossing, and ecological organizer at once.

The Median is therefore a moral and spatial paradox: a strip designed as nobody’s destination becomes a permanent polity.

Human scale remains vast, mostly indifferent, and only partly legible. The animals do not conquer that world or reproduce it in miniature. They make a non-industrial scrap culture inside its seams.

## The tonal balance

| MEDIAN seeks… | Rather than… |
|---|---|
| **Sanctuary** | “Cozy” safety detached from credible material and social conditions. |
| **Peril** | Combat fantasy or heroic violence as the primary solution. |
| **Civilization** | Miniature human industrialization, electricity fantasy, factory chains, or medieval adventurer society. |
| **Tenderness** | Sentimentality that demands a buff, reward, or compulsory emotional response. |
| **Legibility** | A spreadsheet that hides the inhabited world behind menus. |
| **Quiet** | Emptiness, inactivity, or the absence of meaningful play. |

## The player’s position

The player is not principally a commander, mayor, production optimizer, or possessed avatar. The player moves among several ethical positions:

- **Steward** — arrange finite civic attention so the Colony can function and become safe;
- **Chooser** — decide when a need or ambition justifies sending particular friends beyond sanctuary;
- **Witness** — remain present when consequence belongs to a named life rather than an abstract unit;
- **Companion** — in Embodiment, accompany rather than possess; guide attention while the Citizen retains expression;
- **Rememberer** — preserve what mattered without converting every emotional fact into advantage.

# XIV. Compact Governing Doctrine

| Doctrine | Canonical statement |
|---|---|
| **Pitch** | Animal-colony base building on active highway median strips. |
| **Genre** | Attachment-forward base builder. |
| **Home** | Build and tend one permanent Colony until ordinary life can genuinely continue. |
| **Away** | Send named Citizens beyond sanctuary to bring something and someone changed back. |
| **Growth** | Home grows the Colony; Away grows and hazards Citizens. |
| **Success** | Good Home play produces successful stillness. |
| **Deficiency** | Shortfall creates exposure to situated trouble; it need not cause constant attrition. |
| **Systems** | Conserve mechanics; multiply meaning through context, manifestation, and memory. |
| **Logic** | Game Logic precedes Attachment. Every substantial attachment-forward system must perform real Game Logic work. |
| **Citizenship** | All Citizens are classed alike; arrival does not create personhood. |
| **Choice** | Choice over incidental outcome; group resolution with personal consequence. |
| **Modes** | Home and Away are the two broad conditions of play. |
| **Registers** | Colony, Field, Crossing, Encounter, and Embodiment are five forms of play inside one persistent world. |
| **Operators** | DWELL, TRAVEL, RISK, MEET, and EMBODY are the actions paired with the five Registers. |
| **Species** | Mouse JOINs, Rabbit GATHERs, and Squirrel CONNECTs because their bodies understand safety differently. |
| **Camera** | Open to the player is not open to the world. |
| **Memory** | What mattered may persist without becoming a modifier. |
| **Ecology** | Bodily influence, not literal civilizational canon. |
| **Manifestation** | Preserve the dramatic functions and invariants; translate the interface honestly into the medium. |

# Closing Proposition

MEDIAN is a base builder in which the highest achievement is not a larger machine but a more inhabited world.

Its systems exist to make sanctuary credible, danger personal, return consequential, and memory cumulative. The player should finish a campaign not merely knowing what was built, but knowing who lived there, what the place became, who left it, who returned, and why the quiet was worth earning.

> **BUILD A SANCTUARY. HAZARD PARTICULAR FRIENDS. BRING CONSEQUENCE HOME. REMEMBER WHAT HAPPENED.**
