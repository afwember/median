## 2. Reason for existence and content role

Embodiment is the dedicated game-semantic owner for MEDIAN's fifth Register and
the Home-only `EMBODY` operator. It defines the Register's purpose, access gate,
Practice and Presence forms, experience families, species spatial identity,
bounded interaction grammar, relationship to Quiet Equilibrium, Exposure and
Tharn, Campaign Memory, the MEET boundary, authoring requirements, examples,
non-goals, and acceptance tests.

The extractable identity is source-bounded game-semantic evidence. The source
does not independently own MSID validity, constitutional precedence, ecological
premises, authorial orthography, final implementation, manifestation-specific
controls, publication layout, or semantic reconciliation.

  scope but does not automatically supply Embodiment mechanics.
