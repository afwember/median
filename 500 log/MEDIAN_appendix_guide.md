# MEDIAN — Appendix Guide

Running specification for the appendices. **Nothing here is written into a compile.** The book carries a manifest — title and one line of scope each — and the appendices are authored as a project of their own.

Maintained alongside `MEDIAN_change_guide_v0.4.4_to_v0.4.6.md`. Where that guide says *log it*, it lands here.

Entries define what each appendix must contain rather than the prose it will contain. Where a phrase here is already right, use it — integrated into the appendix's own voice, and without the reasoning around it.

---

## The principle

**Document history leaves the book. World and manifestation stay.**

The old A through D were project apparatus — a canon checklist for contributors, a record of what was set aside, a record of what each edition did, and a list of what is unresolved. All four exist to serve the person building MEDIAN. None serves the person reading it. They move to `500 log/` and lose nothing.

What remains divides in two, four and four:

- **The world's own reference material** — the vocabulary the setting generates and the terms it needs.
- **The manifestations** — MEDIAN rendered into a specific medium, one visual page each. Generated imagery belongs here rather than in a method appendix: it is a way the world is made visible, on the same footing as a screen, a table or a deck.

# Part one — the world

## A · Building upgrade-name ladders
*Named rungs, two to four per family. Humble at Tier I, grand by Tier IV — grandeur through scrap-mastery, never machinery.*

**Species-specific ladders, three species each — the four spatially distinct families only:** the central hub (Warren · Den · Burrow) · the food store and dry reserve · shelter and sleeping chambers · the lookout and warning network.

**One shared ladder, used by all three campaigns:** workshop and scrap store · infirmary · nursery · Staging Post · Guest House · Story Circle · Community Board · species-signature structures.

Roughly sixty rung names rather than a hundred and thirty. Three worked examples already stand in the body: Rabbit Lookout, Squirrel Food Store, Wood Mouse Infirmary.

## B · Folk place-name component banks
*The parts every generated place name assembles from, so register is guaranteed structurally rather than policed afterward.*

Materials · flora · qualities · water features · fauna-trace modifiers · landform suffixes · the pattern set that combines them. Homely and plain throughout.

**Separate banks, scoped to colony naming alone:** the species-metaphor layer, and the **Grounded**, **Aspirational** and **Tribute** registers. Tribute needs Ancestral Warren / Den / Burrow components.

## C · The hyphen-compounds
*The convention, and a growing glossary.*

In-world compound nouns take a capitalised first element and a hyphen. The hyphen marks a concept that is one word in the animals' tongue and needs two in English — a translator's mark rather than an ornament.

**Scope.** Hyphenate what the animals say; leave plain what the book says. Compound nouns only: *Roaring Iron* and *the Rivers of Thunder* are adjective-and-noun phrases and stay as they are. Capital-lowercase, on the pattern of *Elf-friend*.

**Glossary, growing as the narrative is written:** After-name · Far-hole · Gap-waiting · Sky-watch · Hearth-kin · Road-edge · Wall-edge.

**This is the convention's only address.** The body never explains it. It is observed in the narrative, in the vocabulary, and diagrammatically on infographics wherever the words appear.

## D · The descriptive bands
*The band names, in order, and what each one means.*

Descriptive rather than numeric · the same set across all three species · ordered, so two citizens can be compared without a figure · plain enough to sit in a Record beside a name without reading as a rating.

**Speed is the first stat that needs them.** It is quantified on the back end and never shown (C-08b), so the bands are what the player actually reads for it. Aptitude, resilience, stealth and stress threshold follow the same way.

This is what stands in place of a number everywhere the interface declines to show one. Without it, *broad visible aptitude descriptors* is an instruction rather than a system, and a Citizen Record cannot be laid out.

# Part two — the manifestations

*One visual page each. MEDIAN is a concept book told through a video-game lens; these are what the world looks like rendered four ways. A shared treatment: a page of image with a few principles set into it, never a specification.*

## E · Played on a Screen
*The native lens.*

**Depiction, not specification** — the specification is already the book. An annotated view per register, a described minute of the corridor's sound, one expedition rendered as actual play. Zero new rules.

This is the page that does what the rest of the document cannot: it shows a minute of MEDIAN rather than defining one.

## F · Played at a Table
*TTRPG.*

A few governing principles. What a roll is. What the GM holds and what the players hold. What survives the translation — the Records, exposure widening the distribution in both directions, the five Approaches, the Laws as table culture. What does not — the Crossing as an action mode, which becomes something else entirely.

## G · Played with a Deck
*Card RPG.*

A few governing principles. The deck already exists as Choice-Event Cards, so the translation starts further along than the others. Presence as a card rather than a number. Exposure as a track. The three-round ceiling as a hand limit.

## H · Pictured by the Machine
*Generated imagery.*

**A continually updated canonical image, carried forward by image-to-image.** A citizen has one canonical portrait; a permanent change is applied *to that image* rather than generated afresh; the result becomes the new canonical reference and the next change applies to that. Identity persists because the image is amended, never re-invented. §22.2's *one record, two outputs* is the same claim from the text side.

**Contents:** the reference-and-amend cycle · why continuity of species anatomy, orientation, existing scars and physical quirks outranks fidelity to any single prompt · how a structured description and a reference image work together, and which governs when they disagree · what degrades across repeated passes, and why text degrades faster than art · observed failure modes, including specified strings surviving or not, drift introduced by upscaling, and corruption of rendered type.

**Excluded:** API calls, model names, vendors, authentication, cost, latency, service continuity. Implementation ages badly and is the least interesting thing about the method.

**Source material outstanding.** To be written from working practice. The Phase 2 art log's entries on the plate pipeline and the reference-plus-annotation approach are the record.

# Candidates for the remaining slots

*Proposed, not settled. All are world-relevant rather than apparatus.*

**The Laws and the Sayings, collected.** The folklore in one place, growing as the narrative pass writes more. §6.2 states the pool grows; nine were added in this pass and there is nowhere to put them but the body. A sourcebook wants its folklore gatherable.

**Given Name banks.** §23.1 names a *species-specific name-component bank* as the non-generative baseline for citizen names, and nothing is deferred for it — the place-name banks cover places only. This is a real gap rather than an addition.

**A corridor gazetteer.** The Reaches as they accumulate, each with its folk name, its biome, its lane count and its Anchor Points. Grows with the three story colonies rather than being authored up front.

**The three story colonies.** If they do not become a part of their own, they want an appendix: three colonies, their founding, their citizens, their losses.

**A sample Record.** One Almanac page, one Chronicle passage, one Likeness, one Tale — the four Records shown rather than described. Arguably belongs in the body beside §22.

# Standing questions

1. **Does the aptitude-band table belong with the world or with the screen?** It is a game-mechanics reference, but the bands are how a citizen is described in their own Record, which is diegetic. Currently placed with the world.
2. **Do the manifestation pages need the word "Played"?** Three of the four are played; the fourth is pictured. Either the group tolerates the asymmetry or the titles drop the verb — *On a Screen · At a Table · With a Deck · By the Machine*.
3. **How many of the candidates are wanted?** Four world appendices and four manifestations is a clean shape. Every candidate adopted breaks it.
