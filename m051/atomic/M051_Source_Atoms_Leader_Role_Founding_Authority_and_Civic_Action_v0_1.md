# 1. Leader as the first civic institution

### M051-LR-001 — Proposed

The Leader may be the Colony's first civic institution.

**Prose reference:** 1

### M051-LR-002 — Proposed

The Leader may function as the founding Colony's Town Center without requiring
a separate Town Center building or Town Center Practice.

**Prose reference:** 1

### M051-LR-003 — Proposed

Leader remains a current civic Role rather than a class, aptitude, personality
type, rank, or permanent career.

**Prose reference:** 1

### M051-LR-004 — Proposed

A present Citizen assigned as Leader contributes one ordinary unit of Home
Capacity rather than superior or multiplied Capacity.

**Prose reference:** 1

### M051-LR-005 — Proposed

Leadership may differ structurally from other Home Roles because every Colony
always carries at least one unit of Leader Load.

**Prose reference:** 1; 3

# 2. Founding Leader

### M051-LR-006 — Proposed

The Founding Escape begins with three randomly created starting Citizens.

**Prose reference:** 2.1

### M051-LR-007 — Proposed

The player should not select a Leader through a setup-screen character or Role
menu.

**Prose reference:** 2.1

### M051-LR-008 — Proposed

At an authored moment during the Founding Escape, one randomly selected member
of the trio may receive Focus.

**Prose reference:** 2.1

### M051-LR-009 — Proposed

The Focus sequence may present the selected Citizen as carrying responsibility
for keeping the trio together and bringing it through the founding ordeal.

**Prose reference:** 2.1

### M051-LR-010 — Proposed

The Citizen who receives founding Focus begins Home as the Leader.

**Prose reference:** 2.1

### M051-LR-011 — Proposed

Leader may be the only preselected starting Role.

**Prose reference:** 2.1

### M051-LR-012 — Proposed

The player may assign the other starting Roles and later reassign or replace
the Leader under ordinary Role rules.

**Prose reference:** 2.1; 2.3

### M051-LR-013 — Proposed

Founding Focus establishes narrative and historical prominence without
creating a hidden leadership statistic, added Capacity, or permanent
protagonist.

**Prose reference:** 2.1

### M051-LR-014 — Proposed

Every founding trio may need a Citizen who performs the Hazel function of
accepting responsibility before a formal institution exists.

**Prose reference:** 2.2

### M051-LR-015 — Proposed

The Hazel function should not require that the founding Leader be the strongest,
boldest, most charismatic, or most technically capable Citizen.

**Prose reference:** 2.2

### M051-LR-016 — Proposed

Bramble performs the Hazel function in New Elderbank.

**Prose reference:** 2.2

### M051-LR-017 — Proposed

Sharpnose's resemblance to Bigwig is incidental character history rather than
a required founding archetype.

**Prose reference:** 2.2

### M051-LR-018 — Proposed

Every trio may require a founding Leader without requiring a Bigwig-equivalent
Citizen.

**Prose reference:** 2.2

### M051-LR-019 — Proposed

Reassigning the founding Leader should not erase the Founding Escape or the
historical identity of the Colony's first Leader.

**Prose reference:** 2.3

### M051-LR-020 — Proposed

The Record, Chronicle, Tales, memories, and later presentation may preserve the
identity of the Colony's first Leader without preserving permanent mechanical
rank.

**Prose reference:** 2.3; 8

# 3. Leader vacancy and Home abstraction

### M051-LR-021 — Proposed

Leader may be the explicit exception to the rule that an existing unstaffed
Role has no Load unless context demands it.

**Prose reference:** 3

### M051-LR-022 — Proposed

Leaving Leader vacant may create a heavily weighted contribution to the
existing deficiency and Pressure machinery.

**Prose reference:** 3

### M051-LR-023 — Proposed

A leaderless Colony may remain a permitted state without becoming a comfortable
or ordinary optimization.

**Prose reference:** 3

### M051-LR-024 — Proposed

The cost of Leader vacancy should be expressed causally through uncarried
commitments, delayed collective response, and absent civic authority rather
than through a separate governance subsystem.

**Prose reference:** 3

### M051-LR-025 — Proposed

Leader vacancy may support authored transitions involving disruption, death,
absence, grief, or deliberate refusal.

**Prose reference:** 3

### M051-LR-026 — Proposed

Home calculations should not simulate individual political personalities,
factions, persuasion, private preferences, or interpersonal disputes.

**Prose reference:** 3; 7

### M051-LR-027 — Proposed

Named Citizens may sweeten Home presentation without personality becoming a
Home crunch input.

**Prose reference:** 3; 7

# 4. Gathering Place, Teacher, and Council

### M051-LR-028 — Proposed

A Place is the physical circle on the ground, while a Practice is the
recognized use established on that Place.

**Prose reference:** 4

### M051-LR-029 — Proposed

Workshop and Gathering Place are Practices rather than physical Place
categories.

**Prose reference:** 4

### M051-LR-030 — Proposed

Gathering Place may be the first Practice constructed in the founding Colony.

**Prose reference:** 4

### M051-LR-031 — Proposed

The initial Leader may give the early Gathering Place its civic use by carrying
decisions and responsibilities belonging to the Colony as a whole.

**Prose reference:** 4

### M051-LR-032 — Proposed

Completing the Gathering Place may unlock the player's authority to name the
Colony under separately governed naming rules.

**Prose reference:** 4

### M051-LR-033 — Proposed

Colony naming may be an earned civic act rather than a setup-screen field.

**Prose reference:** 4

### M051-LR-034 — Proposed

A Teacher need not be present in the earliest Colony and may become warranted
only after population and civic life grow.

**Prose reference:** 4

### M051-LR-035 — Proposed

Leader and Teacher may share Gathering Place just as Builder and Crafter may
share Workshop.

**Prose reference:** 4

### M051-LR-036 — Proposed

Leader use of Gathering Place may concern civic decision, commitment, and
Colony-facing authority.

**Prose reference:** 4

### M051-LR-037 — Proposed

Teacher use of Gathering Place may concern Laws, Sayings, stories, instruction,
memory, and cultural transmission.

**Prose reference:** 4

### M051-LR-038 — Proposed

Shared Leader and Teacher use of Gathering Place may remove the need for a
separate Town Center Practice.

**Prose reference:** 4

### M051-LR-039 — Proposed

Council may be a bounded Home MEET or presentation form rather than another
standing Practice.

**Prose reference:** 4

# 5. Early warning and preemptive preparation

### M051-LR-040 — Proposed

Staffing Leader should provide bounded civic agency beyond suppressing the
Pressure caused by an empty Leader Role.

**Prose reference:** 5

### M051-LR-041 — Proposed

Leader's positive value should avoid wildcard labor, universal productivity,
cross-Role Load reduction, and personality bonuses.

**Prose reference:** 5

### M051-LR-042 — Proposed

A qualifying early-warning Affordance may reveal an approaching World Pressure
family before the Colony commits its posture.

**Prose reference:** 5.1

### M051-LR-043 — Proposed

Groundhog's Seasonal Telegraph may identify an approaching seasonal Pressure
family without providing exact timing or severity.

**Prose reference:** 5.1

### M051-LR-044 — Proposed

Owl's Night Sky-watch may remain narrower than Seasonal Telegraph by concerning
nocturnal predators or unusual night movement.

**Prose reference:** 5.1

### M051-LR-045 — Proposed

When qualifying warning exists and Leader is staffed, there may be a chance for
the Leader to call a preemptive Council MEET.

**Prose reference:** 5.1

### M051-LR-046 — Proposed

A preemptive Council MEET may allow the Colony to spend an existing resource on
preparation before the warned Pressure resolves.

**Prose reference:** 5.1

### M051-LR-047 — Proposed

Effective preemptive preparation should cost less than the untreated event
might cost while avoiding guaranteed immunity or exact foreknowledge.

**Prose reference:** 5.1

### M051-LR-048 — Proposed

The warning Guest makes the circumstance legible, while the Leader may make
collective use of that warning.

**Prose reference:** 5.1

### M051-LR-049 — Proposed

Preemptive Council should reuse existing state where possible rather than add a
leadership aptitude, forecast meter, or weather subsystem.

**Prose reference:** 5.1

The current Leader may receive default starting Focus in a diplomatic Colony
MEET.

**Prose reference:** 5.2

Default diplomatic Focus may make the Leader the presumptive civic speaker
without excluding other Citizens from narrative participation.

**Prose reference:** 5.2

### M051-LR-052 — Proposed

A diplomatic option that binds the whole Colony may appear visible but greyed
out when no Leader is assigned.

**Prose reference:** 5.2

### M051-LR-053 — Proposed

A staffed Leader may make a Colony-binding Promise, guarantee, continuing
Terms, future assistance, access, or comparable civic obligation available for
selection.

**Prose reference:** 5.2

### M051-LR-054 — Proposed

Leadership may supply authority to bind the Colony without removing the cost or
consequence of the commitment.

**Prose reference:** 5.2

### M051-LR-055 — Proposed

Formal newcomer reception may remain narrative presentation rather than become
a separate mechanic.

**Prose reference:** 5.2

A staffed Leader may be required for every Leader-facing part of Tier
transition.

**Prose reference:** 5.3

### M051-LR-057 — Proposed

Leader should not substitute for a missing Tier proof, purchase advancement, or
manufacture progression readiness.

**Prose reference:** 5.3

### M051-LR-058 — Proposed

Once all Tier proofs are present, the Leader may call or preside over the
Council or Gathering through which the Colony recognizes its transition.

**Prose reference:** 5.3

### M051-LR-059 — Proposed

The current Leader may receive Focus during Tier recognition and become
historically associated with the recognized era.

**Prose reference:** 5.3

### M051-LR-060 — Proposed

The progression system should govern the exact Tier-transition sequence while
this proposal governs the requirement for staffed leadership.

**Prose reference:** 5.3

standing Council Practice, permanent ruler, hereditary office, leadership
class, or leadership aptitude.

**Prose reference:** 7

### M051-LR-067 — Proposed

The Leader proposal should not introduce individual personality or faction
simulation, a generic multi-Role mobilization mechanic, or a Civic Priority
subsystem at Home.

**Prose reference:** 7

### M051-LR-068 — Proposed

The Leader proposal should not introduce separate founding-decision, newcomer
reception, ordinary major-Project permission, forecast, weather, or parallel
state systems.

**Prose reference:** 7

### M051-LR-069 — Proposed

The proposal should advance only if Leader remains continuously important,
positively useful, narratively particular, mechanically bounded, and compatible
with equal Home Capacity.
