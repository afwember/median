# 1. Governing distinctions

- **M051-MB-001 — Hypothesis — §1:** Every proposed biome may remain available
  to every core species throughout the extended corridor.
- **M051-MB-002 — Hypothesis — §1:** A biome may avoid belonging exclusively to
  Mouse, Rabbit, or Squirrel.
- **M051-MB-003 — Hypothesis — §1:** A biome may define a Reach's broad
  environmental grammar through ground, vegetation, water, exposure,
  infrastructure, spatial rhythm, shelter, and obstruction.
- **M051-MB-004 — Hypothesis — §1:** A Reach may be one particular occurrence of
  a biome with its own arrangement, history, landmarks, and local conditions.
- **M051-MB-005 — Hypothesis — §1:** A founding Reach may be a curated Reach
  whose topology makes the selected species' colony grammar immediately
  legible.
- **M051-MB-006 — Hypothesis — §1:** Founding compatibility may belong to a
  particular site configuration rather than confer exclusive biome ownership.
- **M051-MB-007 — Hypothesis — §1:** The same biome may serve as a compatible
  founding Reach in one campaign and as an unchanged corridor Reach for
  another species.
- **M051-MB-008 — Hypothesis — §1:** The shared world may remain unchanged by
  species selection while the founding sequence selects a suitable place
  within it.
- **M051-MB-009 — Hypothesis — §1:** Starting-territory contents may be
  randomized without randomizing the spatial grammar that makes the selected
  species meaningful.

# 2. Species-relative founding requirements

  existing edges, enclosure, seams, cavities, or infrastructure capable of
  becoming one protected inhabited body.
- **M051-MB-011 — Hypothesis — §2.1:** Mouse expansion may proceed from room to
  wing to a connected second house.
- **M051-MB-012 — Hypothesis — §2.1:** A Mouse founding site may need continuous
  or joinable boundaries and sheltered low-level circulation.
- **M051-MB-013 — Hypothesis — §2.1:** A Mouse founding site may need
  opportunities to thicken an inhabited envelope.
- **M051-MB-014 — Hypothesis — §2.1:** A Mouse founding site may distinguish dry

- **M051-MB-015 — Hypothesis — §2.1:** Mouse settlement growth may become more

- **M051-MB-016 — Hypothesis — §2.1:** Detached picturesque huts may fail to

## 2.2 Rabbit — GATHER

- **M051-MB-018 — Hypothesis — §2.2:** A Rabbit founding Reach may require cover,
  several plausible household refuges, common safe ground, and multiple paths
  back into concealment.
- **M051-MB-019 — Hypothesis — §2.2:** Rabbit expansion may proceed from a
  complete court to a daughter court and later connection between social
  centers.
- **M051-MB-020 — Hypothesis — §2.2:** A Rabbit founding site may need a shared

- **M051-MB-021 — Hypothesis — §2.2:** Brush, banks, roots, deadfall, or comparable

- **M051-MB-022 — Hypothesis — §2.2:** A Rabbit founding site may need more than

- **M051-MB-023 — Hypothesis — §2.2:** Rabbit communal life may require room to
  gather without monumental construction.
- **M051-MB-024 — Hypothesis — §2.2:** A Rabbit court may feel protected by its
  surrounding terrain rather than exposed like a human village square.

## 2.3 Squirrel — CONNECT

- **M051-MB-025 — Hypothesis — §2.3:** A Squirrel founding Reach may require
  multiple anchors, differences in height, gaps, interrupted surfaces, or

- **M051-MB-026 — Hypothesis — §2.3:** Squirrel expansion may proceed from reach

- **M051-MB-027 — Hypothesis — §2.3:** A Squirrel founding site may need several

- **M051-MB-028 — Hypothesis — §2.3:** A Squirrel founding site may need both

- **M051-MB-029 — Hypothesis — §2.3:** Bridges, runs, trunks, ledges, posts,
  cables, or comparable opportunities may support Squirrel routes.
- **M051-MB-030 — Hypothesis — §2.3:** Alternate paths may emerge as a Squirrel
  colony develops.
- **M051-MB-031 — Hypothesis — §2.3:** The continuity of a Squirrel settlement may
  reside in its network.
- **M051-MB-032 — Hypothesis — §2.3:** A central tree or tower may serve as a
  founding anchor without becoming a Town Center surrounded by incidental

# 4. Detailed biome candidates

## 4.1 Rootbank Meadow

- **M051-MB-055 — Hypothesis — §4.1:** Rootbank Meadow may alternate visible
  common ground with immediate refuge rather than form an uninterrupted lawn.
- **M051-MB-056 — Hypothesis — §4.1:** A strong Rabbit founding instance may
  contain a modest central clearing bounded by banks or roots that create
  household pockets without isolating them.
- **M051-MB-057 — Hypothesis — §4.1:** Rabbit habitation may give existing safe
  ground social meaning through shelters, household edges, paths, Stores, and
  communal Practices.
- **M051-MB-058 — Hypothesis — §4.1:** Rabbit growth may preserve the court as
  visually primary and later establish a daughter court behind another bank or
  root mass.
- **M051-MB-059 — Hypothesis — §4.1:** Mouse may read root seams and hollows as
  discontinuous shelter, while Squirrel may read shrubs, roots, posts, and
  branches as a limited low network.
- **M051-MB-060 — Hypothesis — §4.1:** Rootbank Meadow may avoid manicured ground,
  perfect shelter spacing, a human village-green composition, and loss of
  irregular cover or escape paths.

## 4.2 Bramble Hollow

- **M051-MB-061 — Hypothesis — §4.2:** Bramble Hollow may use dense cover, short
  visibility, and unexpectedly safe openings to feel larger from within than
  from above.
- **M051-MB-062 — Hypothesis — §4.2:** Rabbit households may face a shared
  protected interior without requiring rigid architecture.
- **M051-MB-063 — Hypothesis — §4.2:** Rabbit expansion may clear and tend
  passages without simply removing the thicket.
- **M051-MB-064 — Hypothesis — §4.2:** A daughter court may lie beyond a dense
  screen while retaining multiple protected routes to the first court.
- **M051-MB-065 — Hypothesis — §4.2:** Mouse may read the lower stem mesh as
  covered micro-runs, while Squirrel may read upper bramble and deadfall as a
  secondary network.
- **M051-MB-066 — Hypothesis — §4.2:** Bramble Hollow may remain spatially legible
  without becoming a decorative hedge around an exposed or overbuilt court.

## 4.3 Culvert Garden

- **M051-MB-067 — Hypothesis — §4.3:** Culvert Garden may center on active
  drainage, seasonal flow, damp soil, raised dry banks, stones, plants, and
  material deposited by runoff.
- **M051-MB-068 — Hypothesis — §4.3:** Water level and flood history may remain
  legible through staining, debris lines, scouring, and high-water refuge.
- **M051-MB-069 — Hypothesis — §4.3:** A culvert may participate directly in
  Mouse JOIN.
- **M051-MB-070 — Hypothesis — §4.3:** Buildings touching the culvert or
  coherently connected through it may form one aligned Mouse estate.
- **M051-MB-071 — Hypothesis — §4.3:** Mouse development may occupy banks before,
  beside, above, and around the culvert rather than flatten onto its face.
- **M051-MB-072 — Hypothesis — §4.3:** Culvert flow may leave meaningful dry
  player space while preserving credible flood pressure.
- **M051-MB-073 — Hypothesis — §4.3:** Rabbit may read the culvert as shelter,
  escape, boundary, and dangerous pocket, while Squirrel may read it as a low
  node requiring routes upward.
- **M051-MB-074 — Hypothesis — §4.3:** Culvert Garden may avoid a flat dollhouse
  facade, a broad river or bog, and preemption of player-selected building
  space.

## 4.4 Concrete Trench

- **M051-MB-075 — Hypothesis — §4.4:** Concrete Trench may feel engineered at
  human scale and involuntarily habitable at animal scale.
- **M051-MB-076 — Hypothesis — §4.4:** Mouse chambers may accrete against seams,
  beneath lips, inside drainage voids, and along connected protected runs.
- **M051-MB-077 — Hypothesis — §4.4:** Mouse growth may increasingly inhabit the
  trench from within without disguising its concrete origin.
- **M051-MB-078 — Hypothesis — §4.4:** Rabbit may find shade and hard boundaries
  but limited digging and channelized escape, while Squirrel may find ledges
  and anchors but few living routes.
- **M051-MB-079 — Hypothesis — §4.4:** Concrete Trench may preserve active modern
  infrastructure and avoid post-apocalyptic ruin or miniature stone-city
  imagery.

## 4.5 Wooded Median

- **M051-MB-080 — Hypothesis — §4.5:** Wooded Median may retain perceptible
  highway noise, vehicle flashes, barriers, and openings within its trees and
  short sightlines.
- **M051-MB-081 — Hypothesis — §4.5:** Several trees or large branches may form
  initial Squirrel nodes.
- **M051-MB-082 — Hypothesis — §4.5:** Squirrel development may proceed through
  provisional reaches, reinforcement, alternate paths, vertical separation,
  and loops.
- **M051-MB-083 — Hypothesis — §4.5:** A founding composition may provide
  multiple viable anchors and a meaningful first-connection choice rather than
  one dominant tree.
- **M051-MB-084 — Hypothesis — §4.5:** Mouse may read roots, bark, and trunk bases
  as fragmented enclosure, while Rabbit may read root pockets, deadfall, and
  brush clearings as refuge.
- **M051-MB-085 — Hypothesis — §4.5:** Wooded Median may avoid generic woodland
  fantasy, premature complete rope webs, and bridges without credible nodes or
  future redundancy.

## 4.6 Rock Cut

- **M051-MB-086 — Hypothesis — §4.6:** Rock Cut may present usable but incomplete
  spatial islands through stone layers, fissures, shelves, channels,
  vegetation, and abrupt gaps.
- **M051-MB-087 — Hypothesis — §4.6:** Rock Cut may express CONNECT without
  relying on a forest.
- **M051-MB-088 — Hypothesis — §4.6:** Ledges may become Squirrel nodes joined by
  branches, roots, posts, and constructed runs.
- **M051-MB-089 — Hypothesis — §4.6:** Rock Cut may present several plausible
  route alignments instead of one predetermined bridge.
- **M051-MB-090 — Hypothesis — §4.6:** Mouse may read fissures as excellent but
  difficult-to-join shelters, while Rabbit may read shelves as constrained or
  exposed refuge.
- **M051-MB-091 — Hypothesis — §4.6:** Rock Cut may retain highway scale,
  barriers, drainage, and regional vegetation rather than become mountain
  scenery.

## 4.7 Thin Grass Ribbon

- **M051-MB-092 — Hypothesis — §4.7:** Thin Grass Ribbon may derive identity from
  low vegetation, strong wind, long sightlines, and constant awareness of both
  roads.
- **M051-MB-093 — Hypothesis — §4.7:** Minor roadside objects may carry unusual
  spatial importance because substantial cover is scarce.
- **M051-MB-094 — Hypothesis — §4.7:** Thin Grass Ribbon may test exposure,
  distance, observation, and the value of small cover.
- **M051-MB-095 — Hypothesis — §4.7:** Mouse may seek scarce low edges, Rabbit may
  balance feeding and warning distance against exposure, and Squirrel may face
  isolated anchors with little redundancy.
- **M051-MB-096 — Hypothesis — §4.7:** Thin Grass Ribbon may remain sparse but
  navigable without filling itself with picturesque debris or softening road
  proximity.

## 4.8 Creek Split

- **M051-MB-097 — Hypothesis — §4.8:** Creek Split may be organized longitudinally
  by a persistent stream, banks, islands, crossings, undercuts, deposits, and
  seasonal high-water routes.
- **M051-MB-098 — Hypothesis — §4.8:** Creek Split may differ from Culvert Garden
  because water organizes the whole Reach rather than emerging from one
  dominant structure.
- **M051-MB-099 — Hypothesis — §4.8:** The creek may create parallel territories
  and make local crossings important as familiar paths change.
- **M051-MB-100 — Hypothesis — §4.8:** Mouse may seek bank seams and protected
  crossings, Rabbit may see parallel cover systems, and Squirrel may use banks
  and islands as connected nodes.
- **M051-MB-101 — Hypothesis — §4.8:** Creek Split may keep its watercourse small,
  longitudinal, and initially lacking a built bridge at every crossing.

## 4.9 Pond Hollow

- **M051-MB-102 — Hypothesis — §4.9:** Pond Hollow may be a changing low basin of
  standing water, reeds, saturated margins, mud, insects, amphibians, concealed
  approaches, and temporary dry ground.
- **M051-MB-103 — Hypothesis — §4.9:** Pond Hollow may emphasize water margins,
  uncertain footing, concealment, ecological presence, and possible disease or
  contamination.
- **M051-MB-104 — Hypothesis — §4.9:** Pond Hollow may make damp-ground and
  water-associated Guests feel native to the corridor.
- **M051-MB-105 — Hypothesis — §4.9:** Mouse may find rich but moisture-vulnerable
  edges, Rabbit may need reliable dry refuge, and Squirrel may network above
  difficult ground.
- **M051-MB-106 — Hypothesis — §4.9:** Pond Hollow may retain a visible corridor
  cause and distinguish ordinary wetland from flood event.

## 4.10 Interchange Expanse

- **M051-MB-107 — Hypothesis — §4.10:** Interchange Expanse may arise where nearby
  roadways divide, merge, curve, or cross.
- **M051-MB-108 — Hypothesis — §4.10:** Interchange Expanse may create orientation
  pressure through scattered infrastructure, conflicting sightlines, isolated
  vegetation, and several apparent travel directions.
- **M051-MB-109 — Hypothesis — §4.10:** Interchange Expanse may support route
  choice, mistaken direction, distant landmarks, predator visibility, and a
  sense of highway country.
- **M051-MB-110 — Hypothesis — §4.10:** Mouse may confront separated shelter,
  Rabbit may confront exposed gaps between courts, and Squirrel may confront
  fragile long-distance anchors.
- **M051-MB-111 — Hypothesis — §4.10:** Interchange Expanse may preserve legible
  animal-scale routes and highway-caused disorientation rather than become a
  generic large meadow.

## 4.11 Overpass Shadow

- **M051-MB-112 — Hypothesis — §4.11:** Overpass Shadow may create a distinct
  architectural climate through columns, beams, abutments, joints, drainage,
  echo, vibration, artificial shade, and sharp light boundaries.
- **M051-MB-113 — Hypothesis — §4.11:** Overpass Shadow may support vertical
  exploration, weather shelter, darkness, runoff, debris, correspondence or
  flight encounters, and routes above ordinary sightlines.
- **M051-MB-114 — Hypothesis — §4.11:** Mouse may read protected seams, Rabbit may
  read broad shade with hard boundaries, and Squirrel may read an artificial
  canopy.
- **M051-MB-115 — Hypothesis — §4.11:** Overpass Shadow may remain continuous with
  an active corridor rather than become an underground dungeon or abandoned
  ruin.
- **M051-MB-116 — Hypothesis — §4.11:** Overpass Shadow's exceptional visual
  language may support later discovery without making it an automatic Squirrel
  start.

## 4.12 Abandoned Works

- **M051-MB-117 — Hypothesis — §4.12:** Abandoned Works may be an interrupted
  highway project old enough for ecology to develop but recent enough for its
  human intention to remain legible.
- **M051-MB-118 — Hypothesis — §4.12:** Abandoned Works may offer incomplete
  shelter, abundant material, blocked routes, hidden voids, sharp boundaries,
  and evidence of local rather than global human withdrawal.
- **M051-MB-119 — Hypothesis — §4.12:** Mouse may see separated prefabricated
  chambers, Rabbit may see earth cuts and returning vegetation, and Squirrel
  may see a precarious artificial network.
- **M051-MB-120 — Hypothesis — §4.12:** Abandoned Works may avoid implying a
  post-apocalypse or an implausibly intact active site without humans.
- **M051-MB-121 — Hypothesis — §4.12:** Material abundance in Abandoned Works may
  avoid erasing the biome's danger and incompleteness.
