# Superseded GDD editions

Every prior edition of the design document, kept whole.

**`MEDIAN_GDD_v0.1.md`** is the first document the project ever had, transcribed from the original `.docx` and kept unedited. Worth reading once: eleven sections, thirty-one subsections and eleven tables, and a surprising amount of it survives to the current edition — the sound wall as a real world boundary, the low named cast, road-crossing as a deliberate choice rather than an obstacle course, and *"Age of Empires, minus combat, with the heart of Watership Down"* already stated as the touchstone in its first paragraph.

What did not survive is equally instructive: a fourth shared stat, an escape sequence with invisibly weighted survival odds, action-mode skill growth, and the zone vocabulary that later became the Median Reach. The `.docx` is retained alongside it as the true original.

**Naming:** `MEDIAN_GDD_v0.4.1.md` — keep the version in the filename, since
these are precisely the files where mistaking one for another matters.

Also belongs here: the supporting documents that were folded into an edition
and no longer stand alone — the v0.4.1 and v0.4.2 running decision logs, the
Decision 67 raccoon paper, and the encounter and mode specification documents
that were absorbed whole into v0.4.2.
