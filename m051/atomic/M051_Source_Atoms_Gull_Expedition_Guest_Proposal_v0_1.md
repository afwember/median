# 2. Provisional Signatures

### M051-GULL-007 — Proposed

Gull may receive **READ — Long View** as its provisional Signature.

**Prose reference:** 2.1

### M051-GULL-008 — Proposed

Gull's Long View may emphasize broad open geography, distant movement,
weather, water, human congregation, traffic patterns, and relationships among
separated places.

**Prose reference:** 2.1

### M051-GULL-009 — Proposed

Gull's Long View should offer situated context before commitment rather than
omniscience, exact mapping, Party transport, aerial fast travel, or a safe
Crossing bypass.

**Prose reference:** 2.1

### M051-GULL-010 — Proposed

A regionally credible inland gull may understand highways, parking lots,
reservoirs, rivers, fields, landfills, rooftops, and food sites as one connected
human-shaped ecology.

**Prose reference:** 2.1

### M051-GULL-011 — Proposed

Moving Long View to Gull may allow Crow's Signature to emphasize corvid
intelligence, curiosity, memory, experimentation, and opportunistic object use.

**Prose reference:** 2.2

### M051-GULL-012 — Proposed

Crow may receive **HANDLE — Trialwork** as a provisional replacement
Signature.

**Prose reference:** 2.2

### M051-GULL-013 — Proposed

Trialwork may permit Crow to use an available object for one bounded test or
indirect manipulation.

**Prose reference:** 2.2

### M051-GULL-014 — Proposed

Representative Trialwork may include probing, drawing an object closer,
placing a marker, dropping an object to provoke or measure a response, or
using one found thing upon another.

**Prose reference:** 2.2

### M051-GULL-015 — Proposed

Trialwork should not make Crow a universal puzzle solver, create absent
materials, establish elaborate manufactured-tool engineering, or guarantee
success.

**Prose reference:** 2.2

### M051-GULL-016 — Proposed

The player should still need to recognize a plausible Trialwork opportunity,
and the world should retain the consequences of the attempt.

**Prose reference:** 2.2

### M051-GULL-017 — Proposed

Crow's Trialwork should remain distinct from Raccoon's **HANDLE — Latchwork**.

**Prose reference:** 2.3

### M051-GULL-018 — Proposed

Raccoon may remain the direct specialist in lids, catches, containers, knots,
and simple mechanisms while Crow enables indirect manipulation through a
plausible found object.

**Prose reference:** 2.3

### M051-GULL-019 — Proposed

Two HANDLE Expedition Guests may be justified if Trialwork and Latchwork remain
mechanically and expressively legible as different verbs.

**Prose reference:** 2.3

### M051-GULL-020 — Proposed

The provisional distinction is that Gull understands what lies beyond, Crow
discovers how something may work, and Raccoon knows how to open and handle it.

**Prose reference:** 2.3
