# MEDIAN v0.4 — Infographic Plan & Backlog

**Purpose:** Turn the five reviewed decks into a concrete production plan for the v0.4 visual set — a comprehensive, well-supported suite where every major GDD system has a plate, built in the "v0.3 layout + v0.1 Set 2 feel" house style proven by the *Turn of the Seasons* poster.

**How to read this.** Part 1 audits every existing plate title-by-title (verdict: **Remake / Harvest / Retire**). Part 2 is the master list of needed v0.4 plates, grouped by GDD area, each with what it must show, its source, and the canon corrections to apply. Part 3 is a build-order roadmap. Part 4 is a coverage check proving the set is complete.

**Three standing rules for every plate** (from the analysis doc, Section H):
1. **House style** = *Turn of the Seasons* (warm parchment, painted panels, hand-lettered headers, diegetic in-world signage) — not the navy-blueprint/military-HUD chrome.
2. **Keep v0.3's bones** — dense single-poster layout, annotated hero images, cross-sections, true-scale reference bars.
3. **Correct the canon as you reskin** — the militarized vocabulary and the content drift lived together, so replacing the skin and fixing the facts is one job.

## Part 2 — The v0.4 master infographic list

Each entry: **must-show** · **source** · **canon fixes** · **priority (P1 core / P2 supporting / P3 brand-meta)**.

### A. World & geography

**P01 — Median Reach Cross-Section** · *P1* · **← your explicit ask.**
A sideways section of one Median Reach: **Sound Wall → Margin → Highway → Home Median → Highway → Margin → Sound Wall**, symmetrical, with the colony cut away below the central Home zone (root/forage layer, habitat chambers, deep nursery, culvert storage). Overlay the **risk gradient** (safe interior → lethal at the lanes → rich-but-exposed Margins = the "edge effect / quiet zone" logic).
*Source:* v0.3 Corridor Geometry plate (clean v0.2 version).
*Canon fixes:* danger is **lane count**, not biome; **Margin is never buildable**; **remove the subterranean culvert conduits that bypass the crossing** (they violate the no-bypass rule); label it a **Median Reach**; sound-wall height is **flavor, not "6.5m."**

**P02 — The Corridor / Chain of Median Reaches** · *P1*
The longitudinal map: Home Reach + a chain of Reaches up/down the corridor, each tagged with its **lifecycle state** (undiscovered / expedition-only / outposted) and biome. Shows Median Scout → Establish Outpost → network growth.
*Source:* right panel of the Corridor Geometry plate ("Chain of Median Strips").
*Canon fixes:* remove "subterranean linkages / silent transit"; reframe distance as **strategic**, not metric-military telemetry; add the 3-state lifecycle and outpost claim-the-whole-Reach.

**P03 — Biome Codex (8 biomes)** · *P1*
The eight canonical biomes as cards: art + resource character (Perishable/Durable/Flexible/Rigid mix) + typical Anchor Points + cover profile.
*Source:* v0.3 Habitat Matrix (6).
*Canon fixes:* expand to **8**; correct names (no "Scrub Ravine" → folded into Wooded Median); **decouple danger from biome** (danger = lanes); drop "diversity index" military framing.

**P04 — Anchor Points** · *P2*
What Anchor Points are: named terrain features suited to species/building types; **bonus not gate**; every Reach a mix; first Home Reach guaranteed to fit chosen species. (Can be a sub-panel of P03 if you'd rather not spend a full sheet.)
*Source:* new (concept only in Set 2's "placement matters").

**P05 — Highway Ecology & the Edge-Effect Gradient** · *P2/P3*
The real-ecology explainer: root network, food web, microclimates, movement corridors, and the **edge-effect (rich+risky) vs quiet-zone (deeper=safer)** principle that grounds the whole risk geography.
*Source:* Set 2 Highway Ecology.

### B. Loops & time

**P06 — The Three Modes & Core Loop** · *P1*
The spine: **Colony View ↔ Highway Crossing ↔ Field Mode**, wrapped in the domestic / progression / expedition loops. Establishes that Field Mode is a distinct third mode.
*Source:* new (Field Mode is v0.4-defining; phone-UI mockups inform the look).

**P07 — The Game Day** · *P2*
Daily beats (Morning Rush → Midday Window → Evening Rush → Night Velocity → Dawn Reckoning) + the four traffic cycles + one-expedition-per-citizen-per-day.
*Source:* new; v0.3 atmosphere matrix informs the mood row.

**P08 — The Turn of the Seasons** · *P1 (near-done)*
The seasons poster — **already the house-style template.**
*Source:* rogue poster.
*Canon fix:* present **Mowing as an any-season event**, not a fifth spoke on the season wheel (Road Work is decoupled from the calendar).

**P09 — Road Work** · *P1*
The signature disturbance system: **Telegraph → Onset → Persistence**; Margin fully wiped; severity = intensity not length; machinery as an **evade/shelter-only environmental antagonist**; recovery-work counterplay; thematic echo of the Founding Escape.
*Source:* v0.3 "Margin reset states" (before/after) + the seasons poster's Mowing column.
*Canon fixes:* non-numeric telegraph (no countdown); keep name "Road Work"; machinery never fought.

### C. Colony & citizens

**P10 — Colony Dashboard / Almanac** · *P1*
The at-a-glance home screen: population by name-count, **8 Roles**, the **correct economy**, construction-project queue, named events log, colony record (Founded / Triumph / Next Goal).
*Source:* Set 2 Colony Overview.
*Canon fixes:* replace drift roles (Farmers/Engineers/Elders) with the **8 canon Roles**; replace "Materials/Water/Knowledge/Reputation" with **Sustenance(Perishable/Durable) + Flexible + Rigid + Special Artifacts**; keep population in the nameable dozens.

**P11 — The Economy** · *P1*
The resource model: three categories + Special Artifacts, with flavor item lists; **1 slot = 1 value**, windfalls, cooperative carrying of heavy items; Perishable vs Durable seasonality.
*Source:* new (Set 1's version had currency drift).
*Canon fixes:* **no standalone currency**; hydration folds into Sustenance.

**P12 — Domestic Roles** · *P1*
The eight standing Roles as cards (Forager, Caretaker, Builder, Healer, Nursery Tender, Watchkeeper, Crafter, Teacher) with their fixed effects; the "roles are static, growth is expedition-only" rule.
*Source:* new.

**P13 — Buildings & Construction** · *P1*
Building families, per-building **upgrade ladders**, **durability states** (Excellent→Dangerous), placement/Anchor bonuses, decay & maintenance, species-flavored hub names (Warren/Den/Burrow).
*Source:* Set 2 Buildings (strong original).

**P14 — Anatomy of a Citizen (Dossier)** · *P1*
A dossier card decoded: name/portrait, **randomized personality trait**, history, **Possessions/keepsakes**, relationships, earned **Distinctions & Maimings**, current condition.
*Source:* Set 2 Citizens (excellent original) + v0.3 gear-material language.
*Canon fixes:* **descriptive bands, not star-pips** (keep values hidden); add Distinctions; no genetics/lineage.

**P15 — Fear, Harm & Growth** · *P1*
The harm ladder (Shaken → Wounded → Maimed → Death), **Tharn** (rescue beat, all three species), the **Distinction↔Maiming symmetry**, and the two growth paths (expedition-only Distinctions + colony-wide Tier stat bump).
*Source:* new (Set 2 status guide is a partial).

**P16 — The Three Species** · *P1*
Rabbit / Squirrel / Wood Mouse: stat bias, spatial identity, signature system (**Warren Flow / Cache Network / Relay Network**), mouse **Safety-in-Numbers** + **Margin access identity**, seasonal quirks (Snake winter penalty as the template).
*Source:* v0.3 Corridor Civilizations.
*Canon fixes:* **remove pheromone routing (mouse), canopy "bypasses traffic entirely" (squirrel), subterranean bypass**; every species engages the crossing.

### D. Expeditions

**P17 — The Expedition System** · *P1*
The five categories (Margin Raid / Median Scout / Establish Outpost / Outpost Visit / Special Journey) and the end-to-end loop (choose purpose → pick citizens → prepare → cross → Field Mode → return → record).
*Source:* Set 2 Expeditions (strong).

**P18 — Field Mode** · *P1 (v0.4-defining)*
The traversable-territory mode: party moves through a Reach visiting **discoverable nodes**; **reaching a node** triggers *that node's* contest check; consistent encounter screen (quiet nodes get their moment too); carry-tension and Road Work signs play out here; exit-to-return. Note it's reused across all four expedition types.
*Source:* new; opossum encounter + phone "explore" mockup as references.

**P19 — The Crossing & Return Logic** · *P1*
The Home→Away crossing action (read traffic/weather/cover, commit) + accessibility options, and the **70 / 20 / 10 Return** distribution (clean / swing / forced re-cross).
*Source:* Set 2 The Crossing.
*Canon fixes:* drop "Smoke Pouch"; 70/20/10 (not 80/10/10).

**P20 — Contest & Recruitment Funnel** · *P1*
The branching outcome model: contested? → **70% antagonist / 30% non-antagonist** → **30% amenable** → gated by check + residence cost → **slot cap**; the bell-curve of outcomes (rare bad tail = maiming; rare good tail = Distinction/artifact/Guest); **group and individual resolution are independent** (costly victory / clean failure).
*Source:* new (v0.3 showed the wrong, exact-odds military version).
*Design-doc note:* this plate may show the probabilities *for the designer*; in-game these are **never** surfaced (see P21).

**P21 — The Expedition Launcher (UI)** · *P2*
The character-centered dispatch screen: portrait cards, qualitative readiness, biome + **partial resource preview**, known traffic/weather — and explicitly **never** contest status, odds, or combat math.
*Source:* v0.3 "Expedition Control" — rebuilt as the *correct* opposite of that plate.

**P22 — Expedition Rating & Debrief** · *P2*
The post-mission grade (**Legendary / Successful / Hard-Earned / Failed**) + the **Report & Share** debrief that feeds colony knowledge.
*Source:* Set 2 Expeditions.

### E. Outposts & guests

**P23 — Outposts** · *P2*
Reach lifecycle (3 states) → outpost **claims the whole Reach**; the outpost building set (central + trickle + visibility + 1–2 citizen slots, never core Home buildings); **citizen stationing** (genuinely away, counts to the same cap); **contest reset to baseline**.
*Source:* new (partial in the chain plate).

**P24 — Guest Citizens** · *P1*
The two tiers + full rosters: **6 Expedition Guests** (Weasel, Fox, Mink, Crow, Hedgehog, Snake) with traits; **9 Ambient Guests** (Owl, Songbird, Toad, Firefly, Groundhog, Turtle, Bee, Mole, Bat); the **1-slot-per-tier** schedule (cap 4 of each, below roster size); recruitment shape + species-appropriate housing.
*Source:* v0.3 Guest Registry.
*Canon fixes:* **Expedition/Ambient** naming (not Catalyst); Snake is an **Expedition** Guest with minimal carry; Mink=water rescue, Crow=conspicuous, Bat=pest-decay, Mole downside removed; add **Hedgehog**; remove Deer Mouse & Pigeon; **Rat is antagonist-only, never a Guest.**

### F. Progression & endgame

**P26 — Progression Tiers** · *P1*
Tier I–IV (**Scavenger Camp / Fortified Warren / Independent Colony / Sovereign Network**): fantasy, range, capabilities, pressures, advancement gates, guest-slot unlocks, the Tier-graduation stat bump.
*Source:* v0.3 Game Tiers.
*Canon fixes:* **no Industrial Hub / Sovereign Empire**, no fuel/energy, no research tree; low-pop preserved; mastery = ecological invisibility, not empire.

**P27 — The Rest-Stop Metropolis** · *P2*
The distant multi-species settlement: functions (trade / craft & adaptation / culture & mythology / intelligence), "extraordinary but plausible," campaign role, and the **Grand Caravan** victory + endless continuation.
*Source:* rogue Metropolis night render as anchor art.
*Canon note:* high population is correct here (not the low-pop Home); push multi-species variety; fictional plaza name.

**P28 — The Founding Escape & Onboarding** · *P2*
The origin sequence (mature ancestral home → destroyed by road construction → played escape) and its four jobs (inciting incident / endgame preview / crossing training / *Watership Down* tribute) + the 8-beat tutorial.
*Source:* Set 2 founding storyboard.

### G. Brand & meta (front/back matter)

**P30 — Title / Front Plate** · *P2*
Wordmark (cracked-asphalt fill), **"//" logomark**, one-line pitch, and the **Core Philosophy** sidebar ("Small is grand… Stories over statistics").

**P31 — Pillars & "What it is / isn't"** · *P2*
The seven design pillars + the base-builder-first / not-a-tactical-extraction framing.
*Source:* new.

**P32 — Scale Language** · *P3*
True-scale reference: everyday objects (soda can, tire, drainage pipe) shown at real scale becoming monumental terrain; species size/weight cards.
*Source:* v0.3 scale-differential devices.

**P33 — Art & Audio Style Guide** · *P3*
The preserve/replace direction, palette, material-culture rules (assemble/lash scrap, never forge/industrialize), audio-as-system. *(This is the one-page style guide previously offered — arguably its own document.)*

**P34 — End Plate** · *P3*
"Corridor Secured" closer: the pull-tab hero prop, "Leave a Gap / Save a Life" and "All Creatures Welcome" signage, credits.
