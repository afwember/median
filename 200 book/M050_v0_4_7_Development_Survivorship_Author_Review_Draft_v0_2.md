# 1. Authority and use

This is a survivorship evidence ledger, not a specification and not a
reconciliation result.

Its functions are limited to:

1. identify potentially significant v0.4.6 propositions;
2. preserve the earlier audit's observation about their apparent status;
3. separate unresolved semantic questions from tuning, content authoring,
   presentation, implementation, and publication questions;
4. give the author a compact place to retain, revise, supersede, defer, or
   clarify each item; and
5. provide source-bounded provenance for later Grand Reconciliation.

Silence in later sources does not, by itself, retain or supersede a baseline
proposition. This ledger does not infer authority from repetition, terminology,
document titles, or the earlier audit's confidence.

## 1.1 Author disposition vocabulary

Use one of the following values during the author pass:

- **RETAIN** — the proposition survives without substantive change.
- **REVISE** — the underlying concept survives, but the author supplies or
  authorizes corrected v0.5 wording.
- **SUPERSEDE** — the proposition no longer governs v0.5.
- **DEFER** — the matter belongs to tuning, content authoring, presentation,
  implementation, publication, or another later task.
- **OPEN** — the matter is a genuine unresolved design question for Grand
  Reconciliation or later authorial judgment.
- **REMOVE FROM COMPILE** — the audit item is mistaken, immaterial, redundant,
  or not useful evidence.

Until the author fills a disposition, every item remains **UNRESOLVED**.

## 1.2 Evidence classes

- **Semantic survivorship:** a possible game rule, definition, relationship,
  constraint, or game-state proposition.
- **Tuning:** a quantity, cadence, threshold, probability, capacity, duration,
  or balance value.
- **Content authoring:** a roster, inventory, example family, vocabulary bank,
  named place, or other authored content that does not determine architecture.
- **Presentation / implementation:** interface, accessibility, audiovisual,
  tutorial, data-display, or implementation behavior.
- **Publication placement:** sourcebook, appendix, chapter, or other editorial
  destination.
- **Historical supersession:** provenance that an earlier concept appears to
  have been replaced or rejected. These entries still require verification.
