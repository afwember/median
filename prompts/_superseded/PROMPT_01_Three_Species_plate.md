# Illustration prompt — "The Three Species" plate

**Plate:** The Three Species (Part I, §4) · Letter portrait · first plate of the first slice
**Purpose:** produce the illustrated and structural layer only. All type is set afterward in PowerPoint from GDD copy.
**Method note:** the layout must *anticipate* the text — every place a label, heading, caption, or paragraph will go is reserved here as a blank panel of the correct size and position.

---

## The prompt

> Create a single-page illustrated plate from a naturalist's field sourcebook. **Portrait format, proportion 8.5 : 11.** This is a printed book page, not a screen, not a user interface, and not a poster.
>
> **CRITICAL — NO LETTERING OF ANY KIND.** Do not render any words, letters, numerals, captions, labels, titles, or handwriting anywhere in the image. Every area where text would normally appear must instead be drawn as a **clean blank panel** — a flat area of slightly warmer or cooler cream than the page, bounded by a hairline rule. These blank panels are intentional design elements, not empty space. Draw them crisply and leave them completely bare.
>
> **GROUND AND MATERIAL.** Warm cream laid paper, very lightly mottled, softly aged at the edges only. Not heavily distressed, not brown parchment, not a treasure map. It should read as a well-made modern book printed on good stock.
>
> **PAGE ARCHITECTURE, top to bottom:**
>
> 1. **Title zone**, full width across the top, about one-eighth of the page height: a single blank horizontal panel on a torn-paper label element, flanked by small restrained botanical sprigs at the two upper corners only — a few oak and clover leaves, muted, no autumn colour, no floral garlands, no vines framing the page.
> 2. **A hairline horizontal rule** beneath it, spanning the text block width.
> 3. **Three equal vertical columns**, evenly spaced with generous gutters, running most of the page height. Each column is built as a vertical stack: a small **blank rounded square badge in deep sign-green** at the top left of the column (leave the badge completely bare, no numeral); beside it a blank heading panel; below that a **large illustration panel** bounded by a hairline rule; below that two or three **blank text panels** of differing heights, separated by hairline rules.
> 4. **A footer band** across the full width at the bottom, about one-fifteenth of the page height: a single horizontal strip divided by thin vertical rules into six blank compartments of varying width, the rightmost noticeably wider.
>
> **THE THREE ILLUSTRATIONS.** Watercolour and ink, warm, painterly, with visible confident linework. Grounded stylized realism at animal scale — naturalistic anatomy, no cartooning, no big expressive human eyes. Each is a cross-section or cutaway showing how one species builds. **A highway with vehicles is visible in the background of all three**, small and distant but unmistakably present.
>
> - **Left column — rabbits.** A cutaway of an underground warren beneath a grassy highway median: connected chambers, tunnels, food stores, a nursery chamber, and — this is the important detail — **six or more separate surface exits** dotted across the ground above, plus one further exit emerging far away at the edge of the scene. Rabbits moving through the tunnels and grazing on the surface.
> - **Centre column — squirrels.** A layered vertical settlement in a wooded median: nests high in the trunks, small bridges of twisted vine and wire strung between trees and down to a steel guardrail, and numerous **small separate food caches** tucked in bark crevices, forks, and root hollows — scattered deliberately, never one central store. Squirrels moving along the routes.
> - **Right column — wood mice.** Dense micro-infrastructure at a concrete culvert mouth: many tiny interconnected rooms, root passages and grass tunnels, and a **chain of small relay stations** where bundles are handed along a route rather than carried the whole way by one animal. Many mice at work in a visible cooperative chain.
>
> **ANIMALS — STRICT RULES.** These animals wear **nothing at all**: no clothing, no cloaks, no belts, no harnesses, no packs, no armour, and absolutely no weapons of any kind. They are at home, and at home they are simply animals. They sit up on their haunches to work with their forepaws and go on all fours to travel — never standing and walking like little people. **Each column contains only its own species**; never mix rabbits, squirrels and mice in one scene.
>
> **ANNOTATION DEVICE.** Within each illustration panel, include a few **thin leader lines** running from points of interest out to the panel's edge, ending in small blank dots or blank rounded tags — pointing at things but naming nothing. In one panel, lay a **soft translucent tinted wedge** over part of the scene, as a survey plate shades a zone of interest.
>
> **PALETTE.** Cream paper, warm sepia and soft black ink, moss and olive greens, damp earth browns, weathered concrete grey, and a single restrained accent of deep sign-green with occasional muted rust-orange. Overall warm, calm and tactile. Wet asphalt and cool grey infrastructure sit against soft organic material — that contrast is the point of the picture.
>
> **DO NOT INCLUDE:** any text or lettering · any human figure or body part · brushed metal, rivets, screws, blueprint grids, targeting reticles or HUD chrome · glowing screens, wires, solar panels, sensors, batteries or any electronics · swords, spears, shields, bows or armour · animals dressed as people · cartoon styling · heavy ornamental borders or full decorative frames · rats.

## Notes for the type pass

- Expect the model to smuggle in a few marks despite the instruction. Any that appear will sit inside the reserved panels, which is exactly where the type layer covers them.
- Set every word from the GDD: §4 for the species, §4.1 for the stat frame — Rabbit carry 10 / food 10, Squirrel 20 / 10, Wood Mouse 6 / 5, all integers — and the three signature systems, Warren Flow, Cache Network, Relay Network.
- The multiple rabbit exits and the distant far exit are P2-17 rendered: multiplicity is the rabbit's readable signature, and it carries into the Field layer.
- If the plate works, the reusable devices are the green badge, the hairline-ruled stack, the leader-and-tag annotation, the tinted zone wedge, and the compartmented footer band. Those become the book's kit.
