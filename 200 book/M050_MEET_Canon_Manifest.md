# MEDIAN v0.5.0 — MEET Canon Manifest

Status: incomplete working manifest for granular authorial review

Basis: current provisional GDD, approved Story-Colony histories, approved
Augments map, and current vertical-slice material

Authority: derived inventory. The GDD remains canonical. Inclusion here records
an existing form, content commitment, or example; it does not promote an
illustrative example into mandatory campaign history.

## Purpose

This manifest inventories the MEET substance already present in MEDIAN v0.5.0
under two primary player-facing families:

- **Home Encounters**, divided into Civic Balance, Functional, and other Story
  Encounters; and
- **Away Encounters**, divided into Contested, Uncontested, and other Story
  Encounters.

Within those families it distinguishes:

- recurring systemic MEET forms;
- procedurally situated types produced by Nodes, Pressure, weather, Field
  Cards, relationships, and other owning systems;
- authored event families and campaign-bounded occurrences;
- fixed incidents in the three Story Civilizations; and
- worked Sourcebook examples that explain rules without becoming fixed history.

It is an authoring and coverage aid, not a deck, encounter table, probability
registry, or new content-generation system.

## How to read the manifest

### Primary class

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Home — Civic Balance** | A Home condition becomes a situated decision because actual Housing, Role, Place, Practice, Patient, dependent, or world Pressure requires attention. |
| **Home — Functional** | A MEET performs an explicit civic or campaign-state transaction: departure, return, Founding and naming, Recognition, arrival, move-in, or another bounded change of standing. |
| **Home — Story** | A particular authored civic, relational, ceremonial, or personal situation occurs at Home without being generated principally by Civic Balance or a Functional transaction. |
| **Away — Contested** | Another animal, group, or answering force holds a materially opposed claim. The fixed five-family contested display applies. |
| **Away — Uncontested** | The party faces a consequential choice without an answering claimant. Concrete contextual actions replace the five-family display. |
| **Away — Story** | A bounded authored Away situation changes or exceeds ordinary Node use: Remembrance, unusual Field incidents, horizon encounters, and other particular events. Weather may modify any Away situation but never owns an Away MEET. Stopover is a separate committed sleep screen and opens MEET only if a regular-Field interruption creates an actual decision. |

The primary class follows where the choice is owned and presented. A larger
event can therefore supply more than one class: Road Work may create Civic
Balance Encounters at Home, a personal Story Encounter for a Founder, and an
Away Encounter for a party caught inside its footprint.

### Secondary authority class

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **System form** | A reusable transition or operation whose subject and procedure arise from the system it serves. It is not ordinarily a Choice Event. |
| **Situated family** | A recurring class of concrete situation supplied by an owning system. Its exact subject, responses, and aftermath are authored from current facts. |
| **Authored possibility** | Bounded authored content with explicit eligibility. It may occur once, rarely, or only when selected; it is not universal procedure. |
| **Story-canon incident** | A resolved event fixed in one Story Civilization's horizontal-slice history. It illustrates what a campaign can create without requiring every campaign to repeat it. |
| **Worked example** | A specific Sourcebook or Augment state used to teach the game. It remains illustrative unless separately established as Story history. |

### Generation mode

**Procedural** does not mean assembled from interchangeable random text. It
means the owning system and current state can repeatedly produce a need for a
MEET. Each realized MEET still requires a concrete subject, participants,
place, one to three stakes, materially different responses, and persistent
aftermath.

**Authored** means the situation has a bounded premise, eligibility, response
field, and aftermath written as content. It may still read the current world
state and vary its available expressions and consequences.

### Local IDs

The IDs below are manifest handles, not MSIDs. MSID assignment should follow
only if this inventory becomes part of the durable Sourcebook reference
architecture.

## A. Universal MEET contract

Every entry below inherits the Chapter 1.5 contract unless it explicitly uses
a shortened state-transition form:

1. The owning system and eligibility make the situation real.
2. MEET frames one concrete subject, participants, known conditions, and one
   to three active stakes.
3. The player selects a response, then chooses an applicable expression.
4. Selection remains reversible until Commit.
5. Focus resolves after Commit, randomly among equal claims.
6. Shared results resolve before personal consequences.
7. Changed facts either open a later Round or return to the owning Register.
8. All aftermath returns to its actual owners in the persistent world.

Contested Away MEETs uniquely retain the fixed visible order **CONTEST → EVADE
→ PARLEY → YIELD → WITHDRAW**. Other MEET forms author the concrete actions
appropriate to their subject.

## B. Primary encounter families

### Home Encounters

#### Home — Civic Balance Encounters

These are the ordinary way shared civic strain becomes particular. Civic
Balance supplies vulnerability and available capacity; it does not supply a
generic crisis screen. Each MEET still names the exact Citizen, Residence,
Place, Practice, responsibility, reserve, or piece of ground at stake.

| Family | Existing canonical sources | Typical MEET subjects | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Housing Pressure** | Housing Load exceeding usable Core Residence Capacity; Residence Fit; damaged or unavailable housing | Who can be sheltered, what ground or material can answer the shortfall, whether another commitment is displaced | The deficit alone is not a MEET. A concrete present choice must exist. |
| **Role Pressure** | Builder, Gardener, Crafter, Caretaker, Watchkeeper, or Leader Load exceeding available Capacity | A structure, Garden, Storehouse, Patient, boundary, gathering, dependent, Project, or response that cannot all be sustained at once | Roles do not become six generic event decks. The affected life or object owns the situation. |
| **Practice and Place vulnerability** | Damaged, poorly placed, unsupported, inaccessible, or overcommitted Practices and Places | Protection, adaptation, relocation, temporary acceptance, restoration, or a changed use | Ordinary work remains DWELL. Only consequential branching opens MEET. |
| **Care allocation** | Several Wounded Citizens or intensive Hearth uses competing for limited valid Hearths | Which present need receives Hearth occupation | One valid allocation resolves automatically; no empty Care MEET opens. |
| **Weather through Civic Balance** | Ordinary rain, wind, flood, heat, cold, frost, snow, traffic weather, or drainage condition adds contextual Load, tests Readiness or Practice Strength, or exposes an existing civic vulnerability | Actual Gardens, Residences, Stores, paths, Citizens, dependents, Patients, or exposed civic functions | Weather manifests primarily at Home through Civic Balance. The condition alone is not a story episode or universal weather encounter; a concrete civic choice must exist. |

#### Home — Functional Encounters

Functional Encounters perform an explicit change in civic or campaign state.
Their dramatic force comes from the particular lives and history involved, but
their decisive transaction is legible and bounded. Some recur; others occur
only once or at voluntary campaign thresholds.

| Form | Home function | Canonical result |
|---|---|---|
| **Launch** | Commit named Citizens, Supplies, Items, Carry, and Home subtraction to one expedition | Operation transfers from DWELL to Away TRAVEL; party membership remains fixed until Homecoming, while any empty Supply position at a usable Outpost may choose one available prepared Supply from the full Colony stock. |
| **Homecoming** | Receive particular changed Citizens before reconciling Cargo, Supplies, Items, bodily state, relationships, civic contribution, and memory | One Day Band advances, the expedition ends, every fact enters its proper Home owner, and DWELL resumes. |
| **Founding and Colony naming** | Commit **Make Home**, sustain the first Leader, assemble and confirm the permanent Colony name, and establish the Founding Record event | The selected ground becomes the campaign's sole Home and ordinary DWELL begins. |
| **Tier Recognition** | Voluntarily commit Civic Dedication when population eligibility, Quiet Equilibrium, and an available Home-present Leader permit ceremony | One Day Band advances, Dedication is consumed, and the Colony's recognized Tier becomes permanent history without granting an upgrade bundle. |
| **Wanderer arrival** | Decide temporary refuge or permanent welcome for a named Core adult under the Colony's actual conditions | A welcomed Wanderer joins the Roster with a Prior-life Tale and appropriate civic standing; another resolution preserves the particular relationship without forced recruitment. The Mouse opening uses a fixed authored version for Weft and Warp without changing this general form. |
| **Guest move-in** | Reconcile fulfilled Terms of Hospitality and the promised Residence Fit, access, support, and safety | The named Guest or collective-bodied household becomes a full Citizen; prior encounter, invitation, construction, and move-in remain separate events. |
| **Nesting proposal** | Decide whether the Colony can credibly commit future Sustenance, accommodation, Caretaker Dependency, Leader Learning, protection, and Role capacity | Acceptance begins the authored arrival of a small number of named young Citizens and their support sequence. |

#### Home — Other Story Encounters

| Family or incident | Source | Function and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Chaos at the ancestral Colony** | Required Founding Escape | Once-per-campaign authored state transition that focuses the future Founders, carries Focus into first Leadership, and forces flight. |
| **Wanderer encounter before arrival** | Authored relationship situation | Rescue, discovery, recognition, uncertainty, or a request for refuge may supply Story content before the later Functional arrival decision. A named life never appears as a population pickup. |
| **Guest encounter and invitation before move-in** | Particular relationship | Contact, familiarity, trust, negotiation, and invitation remain Story content. The later fulfilled move-in is Functional. |
| **Threatening animal at Home** | Situated or authored present danger | A particular body and boundary create protection, deterrence, parley, refuge, or withdrawal choices. This may overlap Civic Balance when actual capacity is tested. |
| **Authored weather episode** | Bounded Story content with its own place, onset, development, stakes, and aftermath | A particular storm, flood, wind event, freeze, heat event, or other weather history may test Civic Balance without being generated by it. The episode remains distinct from ordinary systemic weather manifestation. |
| **Road Work civic thread** | Exceptional authored World event | Telegraph, Onset, Active Work, Withdrawal, and Aftermath can create a linked series of Home Story Encounters. Civic Balance changes available responses and consequences but does not generate or absorb the human event. |
| **Founding Escape recurrence during Road Work** | Exceptional authored possibility | At most once per campaign, present sensory force brings established escape history back through one Founder. It creates no Fear track, diagnosis, or recurring Condition. |

### Away Encounters

#### Away — Contested Encounters

Contested Encounters have an answering side or force with a materially opposed
claim. They always display **CONTEST → EVADE → PARLEY → YIELD → WITHDRAW** in
that order. The fixed families standardize reading; their concrete responses
remain authored from the situation.

| Family | Common origin | Typical subject | Persistence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Contested Node claim** | A persistent Node holds a Resource, object, shelter, access point, or territory claimed by another animal or group | Who uses, keeps, divides, protects, abandons, or changes the present thing | Node state, Cargo, relationship, claimant, bodily consequence, and visit history persist. |
| **Margin Node contest** | Bush, thrown object, scattered wreckage, wrecked car, water, shelter, or living Resource in a Margin | High-opportunity ground shaped by Roadway disturbance, River Spume, contamination, cover, and animal use | Margin does not guarantee contest generally; the first Margin Node in the authored opening does. |
| **Encountered animal or group in opposition** | TRAVEL, Node, venue, relationship, Interchange, or Metropolis | Territorial claim, exchange under incompatible terms, warning, obstruction, threat, or competing recovery | Recognition and relationship remain local to the involved animals and place. |
| **Contested special-territory situation** | Interchange, Ancestral Home, Rest-Stop Metropolis, or active Road Work footprint | The territory supplies unusual participants and stakes without changing the five-family grammar | The ordinary persistent world owns the aftermath; no separate faction or quest system appears. |

#### Away — Uncontested Encounters

Uncontested Encounters present concrete contextual actions directly. They do
not display the contested five-family strip merely to fill positions.

| Family | Common origin | Typical subject | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Consequential unopposed Node use** | A present Resource, object, shelter, ecological condition, or opportunity without another claimant | Gather, recover, study, alter, protect, wait, leave, or undertake occupying work | Quiet observation remains TRAVEL; brief obvious use may resolve in the current Band; only meaningful branching opens MEET. |
| **Unopposed Hazard or passage** | Water, unstable ground, contamination, human activity, damaged cover, or physical access | Proceed, protect, recover, abandon, wait, or take another materially distinct action | Hazard is a current condition, not a permanent Node taxonomy. Weather may modify the condition and consequence but never owns the MEET. |
| **Early Home-Median Sustenance Node** | Authored first-expedition tutorial possibility | A first Node on the Home Median may offer uncontested Sustenance | This exception teaches Node interaction and does not make later Home-Median Nodes automatically safe. |

#### Away — Other Story Encounters

These encounters are neither ordinary contested claims nor ordinary unopposed
Node use. Some are systemic forms; others are authored incidents layered onto
the current expedition.

| Family or incident | Source | Function and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Rest-Stop Metropolis encounters** | Authored campaign horizon | Arrival, venue use, hospitality, transactions, consequential contact, and departure occur through situated MEET while the city remains Away and is navigated through TRAVEL. |
| **Consequential situated interruption** | Bounded interruption during TRAVEL | Current terrain, season, time, weather, and world state select a situation between Nodes. Minor content remains a direct TRAVEL change; only a consequential choice opens MEET. The procedure has no player-facing card, deck, or named event family. |
| **Remembrance** | Eligible Founder or authored Wanderer escapee at an exact remains Node within the Ancestral Home Landmark Reach | Present ground and lived history support a consequential act of memory. It is not a universal sixth response or interaction marker. |
| **Lone ancestral escapee** | Low-frequency authored possibility | A credible present situation may reveal one hiding survivor. No icon, warning, promise, search meter, or automatic adoption. Exact recurrence remains deferred with horizon development. |
| **Away Road Work situation** | Party within or beyond an active work footprint | Wait, Stopover, travel beyond closure, address an immediate hazard, or use an Altered Crossing opportunity. Human work continues independently. |
| **Guest or stranger story encounter** | Authored contact during ordinary travel | Warning, exchange, rescue, information, temporary company, obligation, or the beginning of a later hospitality relationship | Guest species does not itself determine the scene or guarantee recruitment. This category can satisfy the required early Guest-species appearance when it occurs Away. |

### Away transformations that are not encounter families

Two consequential presentations remain outside the encounter inventory:

- **Crossing resolution** belongs to RISK and TRAVEL. Every committed party
  reaches the far side, then a brief reverse-angle Staging Post tableau within
  TRAVEL displays and applies the bounded result before direct movement resumes.
  It does not automatically open MEET.
- **Tharn during MEET** transforms the MEET already in progress. The triggering
  Round resolves; the next Round of that same MEET is reskinned around securing
  the struck Citizen and yielding or withdrawing. No second MEET is spawned.
  Remedy cannot relieve Tharn, the party retains the Citizen, and a completed
  Stopover remains necessary before another Node or Crossing.
- **Tharn from RISK** appears only after the whole party reaches the far side.
  The TRAVEL arrival tableau names the state and applies its restrictions; no
  rescue MEET opens. Direct travel toward a place where Stopover can complete
  remains available.

## K. Provisional Civic Balance authoring laboratory

This section records an authorially requested set of mechanically conservative
Home MEET candidates. It is an authoring laboratory rather than a canonical
encounter bank: none of these situations becomes fixed Story history, a
required recurring event, or a new reusable rule merely by appearing here.
Their purpose is to test encounter writing against the existing Civic Balance,
World Pressure, Place, Practice, Project, Item, Guest, and persistence rules.

Every candidate follows the same boundary:

- the leading existing shortfall identifies the primary stake
- one existing weakness or concrete circumstance supplies the collateral stake
- baseline responses protect the primary stake, protect the collateral stake,
  or endure bounded consequences to both
- Readiness and Practice Strength shape circumstances and results without
  becoming spendable points
- immediate protection may consume an authored amount of appropriate fungible
  Stores, but Home cannot draw upon expedition-assigned Supplies or
  Away-function Tools
- the MEET may make a Place unusable or change defined Stores, throughput,
  access, relationships, or bodily state only through already authorized state
- a committed Project cannot be interrupted or cancelled
- lasting construction, restoration, or adaptation remains a legal follow-up
  Project rather than completing inside the MEET
- resolution consumes world time and returns every consequence to its actual
  owner

### `MEET-LAB-H01` — Wind in the Upper Lines

Persistent wind reaches Morningside. One elevated connection and one
Storehouse covering are beginning to fail. Builder Pressure supplies the
primary vulnerability; thin Caretaker support and the exposed Storehouse
supply the collateral stake.

- **Hold the connection:** the route remains usable; the Storehouse becomes
  temporarily unusable, suspending its Practice Strength and Provisioning
  throughput, and the event may destroy an explicitly stated amount of pooled
  Stores.
- **Secure the Storehouse:** the covering and stated Stores are protected; the
  connection becomes unusable.
- **Clear the exposed ground:** no Citizen remains in immediate danger; both
  structures receive their bounded consequences.

Builder and Caretaker Readiness, placement, and the two Practices bound the
respective outcomes without creating a free additional response. Any lasting
restoration enters the Project Queue afterward.

### `MEET-LAB-H02` — Where the Water Chooses to Go

Heavy rain moves through a New Elderbank Garden toward a low Residence.
Gardener Pressure makes the cultivated ground vulnerable; Housing Balance
makes the Residence consequential.

- **Open the Garden edge:** water passes without making the Residence unusable;
  the Garden loses its present yield and may retain authored physical damage.
- **Bank the Residence side:** the Garden and current yield are protected;
  water makes the Residence temporarily unusable and reduces Housing Capacity.
- **Withdraw everyone from the affected ground:** no Citizen receives a bodily
  consequence; both Garden and Residence take their stated lesser
  consequences.

The MEET cannot create a permanent drainage line. Its aftermath may reveal a
Builder Project that would alter the water's future course.

### `MEET-LAB-H03` — The Broken Entrance

Softened ground shifts beneath a Rushbottom Residence entrance and the
neighboring Workshop approach. The Residence remains standing, but access to
both Places is becoming unsafe. Housing Capacity is the primary stake;
Workshop access and situated Practice Strength are collateral.

- **Recover the Residence entrance:** the Residence remains usable; the
  Workshop becomes temporarily inaccessible and loses its applicable Practice
  Strength and passive throughput.
- **Keep the Workshop approach open:** Workshop use continues; the Residence
  becomes inaccessible and temporarily loses Housing Capacity.
- **Close the affected ground:** Citizens avoid immediate danger; both Places
  become unusable until their respective conditions are answered.

This candidate may not pause, cancel, reclaim, or destroy a committed Project.
Lasting stabilization belongs to a later Builder Project.

### `MEET-LAB-H04` — Three Claims on Two Hearths

Two usable Hearths face three defined intensive claims: one current Young-care
commitment, one Wounded Citizen beginning recovery, and one Wounded Citizen
whose recovery is underway. The Care-allocation MEET presents the three legal
pairings.

- **Young care and the recovering Patient**
- **Young care and the newly Wounded Citizen**
- **Both Wounded Citizens**

A Wounded Citizen without a Hearth retains the Wound and Patient Load but
records no Recovery Day. A Citizen whose recovery is already underway loses no
previous Recovery Days. An unaccommodated Young-care commitment remains an
actual Caretaker obligation within the existing Pressure calculation. The
choice consumes no Remedy, creates no Project, and invents no additional
worsening or developmental penalty.

### `MEET-LAB-H05` — Bitter Under the Lid

Warm damp has reached an explicitly defined quantity within Morningside's
pooled Stores. The shared Caretaker Provisioning process cannot both preserve
all affected Perishable Sustenance and meet the current Remedy target.

- **Prioritize Preservation:** the threatened Sustenance is preserved; the
  current Remedy target is not met.
- **Prioritize Remedy Preparation:** the Remedies are prepared; the stated
  Perishable Sustenance is lost.
- **Maintain the existing allocation:** neither emergency priority is adopted;
  the MEET states the bounded loss or shortfall that follows.

If Latch is present and **HANDLE — Latchwork** applies to the actual failed
closure, the Signature may open a contextual response that better isolates the
affected material. It creates no local inventory and protects no unrelated
Stores. The Storehouse remains a physical manifestation of one pooled Stores
state.

### `MEET-LAB-H06` — No Watch Through the Reeds

Seasonal growth obscures a New Elderbank Watchpost sightline while known animal
movement approaches through the concealed ground. The same growth protects
part of a Garden edge.

- **Cut the sightline clear:** the Watchpost regains its situated strength
  against the present approach; the Garden loses its stated current yield or
  receives defined physical damage.
- **Close the concealed access:** the Garden remains intact and Citizens are
  protected from the immediate approach; that access becomes unusable while
  the obstructed sightline remains unresolved.
- **Keep the ground unchanged:** the Garden and access remain usable; the
  approaching animal reaches the stated boundary or Place under worse warning
  conditions.

At night, a present WHOOT may use **Night Sky-watch** to identify the animal or
its direction more clearly. The Signature improves information for one
response without creating another Watchpost, restoring daylight visibility,
or removing the vegetation. A permanent sightline adaptation belongs to a
Watchkeeper Project.

### `MEET-LAB-H07` — The Warm Stone Goes Cold

A cold event and damaged covering threaten two inhabited Rushbottom Places:
Sedge's Guest Residence, whose warm shelter belongs to its Residence Fit, and
one Core Residence already needed by current Housing Balance. Builder response
can protect one before the event's full Impact.

- **Protect Sedge's Residence:** Guest Residence Fit remains satisfied; the
  Core Residence becomes temporarily unusable and Core Housing Capacity falls.
- **Protect the Core Residence:** Core Housing Capacity remains; Sedge's
  Residence becomes unusable and leaves that particular Guest without valid
  accommodation.
- **Withdraw from both exposed Places:** no Citizen remains in immediate bodily
  danger; both Residence consequences occur.

The MEET transfers no abstract covering, uses no Device Supply, and does not
assign Sedge to a Hearth. Restoration follows ordinary work or an appropriate
later Project according to the authored damage.

### `MEET-LAB-H08` — Which Ground Is Protected First?

After an ordinary weather event, two inhabited Places need immediate temporary
protection, but the Colony can protect only one before the next Impact.
Citizens disagree because each choice imposes sacrifice on different named
lives. Leader Pressure makes that disagreement consequential. The two Places
must already exist and carry actual civic stakes, such as a vulnerable
Residence, a Storehouse entrance, or a Gathering Place involved in current
young-Citizen Learning.

- **Protect the first Place:** its associated lives and function are
  protected; the second receives its stated consequence.
- **Protect the second Place:** the consequences reverse.
- **Defer collective commitment:** no side is immediately overruled; the
  approaching pressure reaches both Places.

Leader Readiness improves understanding, coordination, and the handling of
uneven sacrifice. It neither solves the weather nor substitutes for the Role
that owns the material problem. Leader Pressure may place persistent strain in
the actual relationships or decision without changing the physical result.
The Gathering Place gains no emergency Housing or shelter capacity.

### `MEET-LAB-H09` — A Repair Without Prepared Binding

Rain enters a Residence through a failing covering. Crafter Pressure means no
prepared Binding exists, while pooled Stores contain Scrap able to support one
credible immediate substitution. The neighboring Workshop also contains
exposed usable material.

- **Make the substitution:** an authored amount of Scrap is consumed; the
  Residence remains usable, while the temporary fastening may expose a later
  Builder restoration Project.
- **Protect the Workshop material:** its usable material and continuing civic
  function remain secure; the Residence becomes temporarily unusable.
- **Move Citizens clear:** no bodily consequence occurs; the Residence and
  exposed Workshop material receive their stated bounded losses.

This is a situated use of fungible material under the existing rule that
resources may protect stakes during MEET. It does not prepare or consume an
expedition Supply, and no Tool Project is cancelled, dismantled, or
interrupted.

### `MEET-LAB-H10` — The Cat at the Lower Exit

A roaming cat settles beside Rushbottom's most reliable lower exit. The same
passage supplies ordinary access to a Storehouse. Late Watchkeeper warning has
left little preparation time.

- **Close the lower exit:** Citizens remain protected; the Storehouse becomes
  inaccessible and loses its applicable Practice Strength and Provisioning
  throughput while the closure persists.
- **Draw activity toward another established access:** Storehouse access
  remains; the named secondary Place or approach becomes the exposed
  collateral stake.
- **Wait inside:** one Day Band passes; the cat's authored movement determines
  whether the closure persists, while time-dependent Colony systems continue
  normally.

If Rill is present and the situation qualifies for **INTERCEDE — Drive Off**,
the Signature may open a fourth response. Rill undertakes the bodily
intervention and receives its authored risk; the Signature guarantees neither
success nor general predator immunity. The cat remains a particular animal in
a particular place rather than an enemy unit or recurring defense meter.

### Laboratory authoring test

Before any candidate graduates from this section, its full authored treatment
must answer:

1. Which canonical Pressure or external circumstance makes the situation
   possible?
2. Which existing Citizen, Place, Practice, Stores quantity, relationship, or
   bodily state owns each stake?
3. Do its responses describe particular acts without creating a reusable new
   player verb?
4. Does every cost alter an already defined state?
5. Does it avoid Home use of Away-only Items?
6. Does every lasting construction or adaptation remain a Project?
7. Does it avoid inventing temporary Capacity, Practice Strength, Housing,
   Load, or throughput?
8. Would adequate coverage keep the circumstance in routine DWELL rather than
   MEET?
9. Does another Round occur only after a material change to the situation?
10. Can the aftermath be expressed entirely through the existing Record?

If questions 3–7 expose a reusable rule, authoring stops for explicit system
development rather than allowing the encounter to establish that rule by
implication.

## L. Provisional DAWN procedure for Civic Balance MEETs

This procedure closes the present MEET-development pass. It describes how an
ordinary Home weakness may produce one consequential situation without
introducing a visible crisis score, a simulated ecology, or an encounter
generator that invents beyond the current rules. Its exact chances remain a
later tuning question.

1. **Settle DAWN.** Resolve completed whole production, retained remainders,
   consumption, Projects, recovery, development, and other ordinary DAWN
   changes before testing for a new situation.
2. **Recalculate Civic Balance.** Keep Housing and the standing Role balances
   separate. Determine their actual current shortfalls and the Citizens,
   Places, Practices, Stores, Projects, and relationships that own them.
3. **Choose one leading relevant weakness.** Select the most consequential
   present shortfall that can support a concrete decision. A deficit that has
   no current subject or materially different response remains ordinary
   Pressure rather than becoming a MEET.
4. **Assign one compatible MEET.** Bind an authored template to the actual
   owners, ground, Day Band, season, weather, traffic, recent history, and
   other already known conditions. Internal allocation situations require no
   external circumstance. External situations may use current weather,
   traffic, or animal presence only where those facts make the selected civic
   weakness concrete.
5. **Telegraph when the Colony has grounds to know.** Watchkeeper Readiness,
   current visibility, known weather, and the exact approach may reveal the
   situation before Impact. Telegraph changes the time and information
   available for legal preparation; it does not guarantee occurrence.
6. **Revalidate at Impact.** If preparation or changed state has removed the
   selected weakness or made the assigned situation impossible, the candidate
   dissolves without a roll and no replacement is selected.
7. **Make one hidden hit roll.** The roll asks whether this credible assigned
   threat becomes consequential today. A miss leaves no damage, MEET, Record
   event, or deferred encounter debt. Its underlying Pressure remains if its
   causes remain. A hit opens the already assigned MEET; randomness does not
   choose a second subject after the fact.
8. **Resolve and return facts to their owners.** The MEET uses its ordinary
   Round and Commit grammar. Every result returns to the involved Citizen,
   Place, Practice, Stores quantity, relationship, bodily state, or other
   established owner.

Ordinary Civic Balance makes one such attempt per DAWN. A miss does not cause
the procedure to try the second-largest weakness, and a hit cannot cascade
into a second ordinary Civic MEET from the same accounting. Functional MEETs,
fixed Story incidents, and an active Road Work episode remain outside this
ordinary attempt because their own rules establish when they occur.

The future hit chance may consider the magnitude and persistence of the
selected Pressure, the strength of the current circumstance, relevant
Readiness or Practice Strength, and recent repetition of the same subject or
family. Those inputs must remain bounded and separately owned; they do not
combine into a player-facing World Pressure meter or pooled Civic score.

Animal movement is treated as **biological weather** at this scale. The game
does not simulate hidden populations, routes, or migration tables. Season,
time, weather, traffic, known habitat, and current place state may make animal
presence credible. Ambient presence remains an environmental condition; a
hit that opens a MEET makes the involved animal or group particular and allows
that identity and relationship to persist. Biological weather creates
presence; MEET creates particularity.

Water and ground conditions likewise receive no independent simulation layer.
Existing weather acts through the actual grade, drainage, material, damage,
placement, and Place state already present. A resolved consequence may amend
that physical state, but the authoring procedure creates no new water table,
soil score, or general environmental ledger.
