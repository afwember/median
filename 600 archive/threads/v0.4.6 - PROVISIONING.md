## The four weights

Every entry in the guide carries one. They are the whole point.

| | |
|---|---|
| **state** | The book asserts it in prose. |
| **show** | The book must **not** assert it. An example, table, Record excerpt or caption carries it. **If you wrote a declarative sentence for a `show` entry, you executed it wrong.** |
| **say** | A Law or Saying carries the feeling, set as typography on the page. **Additive** — it never licenses cutting text that carries a concept. |
| **silent** | True of the game. Governs authoring and art. **Earns no sentence.** Add nothing. |

Roughly a third of the entries are `silent`. If your output is materially longer than the base document plus the `state` entries, something has gone wrong.

## The three fields

**STATE** is the only text permitted to become prose. It defines the complete semantic payload — integrate it into the surrounding passage's syntax and voice rather than pasting it, but do not add explanation to it.

**DO** is an edit action on v0.4.4. Execute it, then discard it. **No DO text may reach the output in any form** — not paraphrased, not inverted, not softened.

**SRC** is provenance. Ignore it.

The **ARCHIVE** at the end of the guide holds rationale, keyed by entry ID. Consult it only if you cannot understand what an entry wants. **Nothing in the Archive may enter the book.**

## Order of work

Follow the guide's phases exactly. Do not reorder them.

**Phase 0 — Global.** Eight entries. The document-history sweep, terminology replacements, the nine new Sayings, the lens note, the OPEN callout conversion.

**Phase 1 — Structure.** Moves only. **Anchor every operation on the old heading text, never on a section number.** No prose is written or rewritten in this phase.

Apply the final map, rebuild the Contents from the actual headings, repair every reference, and verify no reference resolves to a missing heading. **Complete this before writing a single word of Phase 3.**

**Phase 3 — Content.** Section by section, composing every cross-reference in **final** numbers.

**Phase 4 — Appendices.** A manifest of eight titles with one scope line each. **No appendix content whatsoever.**

**Phase 5 — Audit.** Run it. Script the checks rather than eyeballing them.

## Traps

These are failures from the previous assembly. You cannot discover them from the guide alone.

**The numbering map has a special case.** §§1–9 unchanged · old 10.3 → **10** · old 10.1–10.2 → **11** · old 11–33 → **+1** · old 34 dissolved · old 35–38 **unchanged**.

**The two shifts cancel.** Inserting at 10 and dissolving 34 offset each other, so §§35–38 keep their numbers and the document still runs to thirty-eight sections. Do not "fix" this.

**Renumber before you write.** The last assembly wrote content first and renumbered last, which meant composing every new cross-reference in the old scheme and transforming it afterward. Two bugs, caught by luck. Phase 2's freeze exists for this reason.

**§21.4 dissolves entirely (S-9b).** Both its contents leave; one surviving sentence folds into the §21 preamble. Guest Citizens ends with three subsections and the Contents follows.

**The §34 guarantee is verbatim and unshortened.** *The base game must be complete, coherent and emotionally effective without any of it. Nothing in this layer is ever load-bearing.* It moves to the front-matter sidebar. Do not paraphrase it, trim it, or improve its rhythm.

**Nine CREATED VISUAL panels, no more and no fewer.** They are a distinct visual class.

**A `silent` entry that replaces an existing thesis still needs one clause.** §34.1 currently opens by asking *how much* anthropomorphism reaches a citizen's body — the question C-97 reverses. Deleting it without replacement leaves the subsection with no thesis. C-97 carries the replacement clause. Watch for the same shape elsewhere.

**Open flags stay, as one class.** Twenty-three of them, converted to `> **OPEN — …**` callouts pointing at `500 log/open_items.md` (G-8). They are working scaffolding and they will be lifted later in one pass, which is why they all carry the same prefix.

## When the guide is wrong or silent

It will be, in a handful of places. **Do not solve it.**

1. Do the most conservative thing that lets you continue.
2. Write it to `500 log/v0.4.7_log.md` — what you hit, what you did, what the alternatives were.
3. Keep going.

**Do not reopen a settled decision.** If an entry looks wrong, log it and execute it anyway. If it is genuinely unexecutable — contradicts itself, or contradicts the base in a way you cannot resolve — stop and say so rather than inventing a reading.

Assembly must not become a design pass. That is how the last one went wrong.
