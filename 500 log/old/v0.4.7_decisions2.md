# v0.4.7 decisions

Designer decisions surfacing after the v0.4.6 assembly, on review of `MEDIAN_Civilization_Spatial_Design_Spec.docx` (see `500 log/civilization_spatial_design_spec_review.md`). Numbered V6-1 onward, in the shape of the existing V4/V5 decision logs — a claim, and the alternative it rejects. Not yet folded into a change guide; that happens when this set is ready for its own assembly pass.

---

**V6-1 · Rabbit's signature moves above ground, replacing Warren Flow**

The spatial spec's Cul-de-Sac — a court of visible burrow fronts around shared open ground, including grazing and other outdoor-use space as part of the identity, not just negative space around it — is adopted as Rabbit's readable signature. This **replaces** Warren Flow's depth-and-flow, underground-tunnel model (Section 4.1), and with it the multiplicity-of-exits / Far-hole payoff that model was built around.

*Rejects:* the underground warren as Rabbit's spatial identity. *Rejects:* "same units, attach differently" as Squirrel-only elegance — it's confirmed as the shared grammar across all three species, Rabbit included. Everything below ground is out; everything that makes the court legible — frontage, grazing ground, the shared center — is in.

**Downstream, unresolved:** Section 4.1's prose, the Field-layer promise that a Far-hole "appears in the Field layer as well," and the `OPEN — Warren Flow` callout all describe the model this closes. They need rewriting, not just updating, in the next assembly. Whatever Rabbit's new readable signature is called (not necessarily "Warren Flow" anymore) still needs a name.

---

**V6-2 · MEDIAN's existing vocabulary is used everywhere, not the spec's parallel terms**

The spatial spec's territory language — Median Interior, Shoulder Strip, Carriageway Gap, Chain of Median Strips — is retired in favor of canon's own: **Home Median**, **Margin**, **Highway**, **Median Reach**. This applies wherever the spec's language appears — the spec document itself, any art brief drawn from it, and any future assembly pass.

*Rejects:* maintaining two vocabularies for the same geography. The spec's functional distinctions (permanent domestic form vs. exposed raid territory vs. the crossing threshold vs. the chain of remote settlements) map onto Home Median / Margin / Highway / Reach cleanly enough that no new terms were doing real work.

---

**V6-3 · The missing building families are written back into the spec — Staging Post without the dressing ritual**

Staging Post, Guest House, Story Circle, and Community Board rejoin the spec's building set (alongside or within its Home / Pantry / Workshop / Gathering / Care / Watch / Nursery functions, wherever each fits the placement grammar).

**Note logged in passing, already corrected in the v0.4.6 GDD:** the Staging Post's old description — "the place a party dresses for the road" — is deprecated. It was already contradicted by the v0.4.6 assembly's own C-98 (gear is worn always, not taken up before a crossing) and had survived as a leftover cross-reference. Fixed directly in `100 canon/MEDIAN_GDD_v0.4.6.md` (Sections 16.1 and 20): the Staging Post is now stated only as the departure threshold and where Tools are kept and drawn — no dressing beat.

*Rejects:* a six-to-seven-function building grammar as complete. It wasn't; it was missing families with real mechanical weight (Guest slots, the Records' ceremonial/ambient split, the Tool economy).

---

**V6-4 · Relay Network comes off Mouse as a home-colony signature — proposed, not settled**

Relay Network (short visible carrying handoffs, stations, cooperative relay of load) "doesn't seem resonant" as what a Mouse colony's *home* base is about. Two threads, not yet reconciled:

- **Squirrel** is the better home for shuttling/relay behavior — it already carries, redundantly, between distributed caches (Cache Network, Section 4.2), which is a relay in substance if not in name.
- **Mouse** may keep Relay Network only as an **expedition-specific quirk** rather than the colony's readable at-home signature. The Manor House / Join model from the spatial spec is the front-runner for what actually reads as Mouse at home; how (or whether) relay-style logistics shows up inside a Manor silhouette is still open — see open question 2 in the spatial spec review.

*Rejects:* Relay Network as Mouse's settled home signature, as currently written in Section 4.3. **Does not yet decide** where relay logistics lands instead, or how Cache Network and a possible Squirrel-side relay coexist without becoming two names for the same thing. Flagged explicitly as proposed rather than settled, per how it was raised.

**Downstream, unresolved:** Section 4.2 (Squirrel) and Section 4.3 (Wood Mouse) both need rewriting once this settles, not just a relabeling — the "Safety in Numbers" and "Expedition Access Identity" bullets under Mouse, and the Cache Network description under Squirrel, were written assuming the current assignment.

---

## What this means for the next assembly

V6-1 through V6-3 read as settled enough to carry into a change guide. V6-4 needs one more pass of design thinking before it's guide-ready — specifically, where relay logistics actually lives once it's off Mouse. None of the four are executed against the v0.4.6 GDD in this pass, except the Staging Post correction noted under V6-3, which was a bug fix against v0.4.6's own internal consistency rather than a new design call.
