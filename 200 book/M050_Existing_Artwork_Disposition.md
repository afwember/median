## 1. Disposition language

- **Use intact** means that the rendered pixels may enter the v0.5 Sourcebook
  without changing their depicted facts. Cropping, page placement, color
  management, and separately typeset captions remain ordinary production
  work.
- **Reskin or remake** means that the composition, subject, or information
  structure is valuable, but the image must be regenerated or rebuilt against
  current canon imagery and rules. Embedded generated text is never retained.
- **Reference only** means that the asset may inform visual lineage, layout, or
  historical understanding but should not become a production target.
- **Reject** means that a known error, deprecated premise, or inferior duplicate
  makes the asset unsuitable even as the direct basis of a remake.

An intact mark is a content judgment, not a resolution or rights judgment.
Source resolution, master availability, crop tolerance, and reproduction
quality still require production checks. m051-origin imagery remains evidence
until Asa adopts its depicted facts for the m050 book.

## 6. Production rule

The audit does not license silent mixing among visual lineages. Once a Story
Colony state or Citizen portrait is selected as current canon input, later
scenes must derive from that state. A historical plate marked for reskinning
supplies composition and atmosphere; it never overrides the GDD, Story Colony
record, or current canon imagery.
