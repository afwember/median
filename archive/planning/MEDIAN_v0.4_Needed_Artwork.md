# MEDIAN v0.4 — Needed Artwork List

**What this is:** a backlog of **concept art / illustration assets** (key art, establishing shots, character vignettes, story beats) — distinct from the *Infographic Plan* (P01–P34), which is information-design plates. House style for all of it: the warm photoreal-storybook register of v0.1 Set 2 + the Concept Artwork deck (lantern-lit, tactile, grounded — never the navy-blueprint/military chrome).

**Standing canon guardrails for any new art:** single-species colonies (one core species + recruited Guests, never mixed core colonies) · no weaponization (scrap tools, not pikes/shields/rifles) · no forge/smelting/industrialization (assemble and lash scrap, don't smelt it) · machinery is evaded, never fought · humans stay off-screen as bodies (their infrastructure carries the scale) · raccoons are now canon as Guest/rival/Metropolis figures (Decision 67), never colony citizens.

---

## Why this list leads with rabbits — a quick species-balance audit

The existing art is **mouse-dominant**: the front plate, the foraging scene, the winter sled-haul, the underground workshop cutaway, the night crossing, the Rest-Stop Metropolis, and most blue-background vignettes are all mice. **Squirrels** get a fair showing (the Acorn Hollow canopy villages). **Rabbits are the thin one** — a single hauling party along a barrier, some seasonal-panel cameos, and a face in the opossum encounter. There is **no signature rabbit colony image at all**, while mice have several. Since rabbit is co-equal in canon (and usually the default first species), that's the first gap to close.

## Priority 1 — your three seeds, plus the rabbit rebalance

### Rabbits (the biggest gap — a small suite, not one image)

**A1. Rabbit warren — establishing cutaway + surface.** The rabbit equivalent of the existing mouse-city and mouse-workshop hero shots: a Home Median warren shown as **surface life above + a cross-section below** — chambers, food stores, nursery, and *multiple bolt-holes* — set between two carriageways. This is the single most important missing image. *(Canon: show the "Warren Flow" legibility — several exits, alternate paths, no weapons; the depth/flow puzzle is the rabbit's identity.)*

**A2. Rabbit expedition party staging + crossing.** A rabbit team at a Staging Post reading traffic before a Home→Margin crossing — precision, speed, coordinated hauling. Rabbits are the "one clean high-stakes crossing" species; this is their signature beat. *(Single-species party + optional Guest.)*

**A3. Rabbit citizen portraits (dossier-quality), 3–4 named individuals.** Close, characterful rabbits with earned marks (a notched ear, a limp, a keepsake) to seed the dossier system — the rabbit counterpart to Set 2's Nutmeg/Bramble cards. Feeds P14.

**A4. Rabbit Founding Escape.** The origin beat with a **rabbit** founding trio fleeing construction into their first median — since rabbit is the common default species, the onboarding key art should exist in rabbit form (mouse/squirrel variants later).

*(Secondary balance note: squirrels are adequately covered, but one **squirrel Cache Network** hero shot — dispersed canopy caches, the resilience-vs-convenience trade — would round out the three signature systems alongside A1 and the existing canopy villages.)*

### Road Work (your #2 — a signature system with no key art yet)

**A5. Road Work — the three-beat sequence.** Concept art for the game's marquee disturbance, ideally as a small set matching its structure:
- **Telegraph:** subtle wrongness in the Margin — survey marks, disturbed ground, distant idle machinery, a changed sky. Quiet dread, no numbers.
- **Onset:** the mow/clear/scrape actually hitting — cover vanishing, citizens scrambling, the machine as an overwhelming, indifferent force.
- **Persistence:** the altered aftermath — a stripped, louder, riskier wasteland with machinery looming, citizens sheltering; then the recovery/regrowth beat.
*(Canon: the machinery is an **environmental-hazard antagonist — evaded or sheltered from, never fought or negotiated**; and it should visually echo the Founding Escape's construction equipment, tying the hazard vocabulary together.)*

### Outposts (your #3 — a whole system with no art)

**A6. Establishing an outpost in a remote Reach.** The moment a construction party plants an outpost's **central building** and it **claims the whole Reach** — a lighter footprint than the Home (central building + a trickle/visibility outbuilding + a stationed citizen), clearly *not* a second full colony. *(Canon: outpost building set only, never core Home buildings.)*

**A7. Wild Reach vs. outposted Reach (before/after pair).** The same strip shown contested-and-wild, then claimed-and-pacified after an outpost — visualizing the "flag in the soil" contest reset and the lone stationed citizen tending it.

## Priority 2 — systems and beats still missing key art

**A8. Field Mode.** A party traversing a Reach's territory between discoverable nodes — the "traversable strip, not a loot menu" mode — plus one **encounter-screen** frame (a node reveal, quiet or contested). The defining v0.4 mode has no illustration yet.

**A9. A contested-node encounter (corrected).** Rebuild the opossum-style standoff **canon-clean**: a single-species party (+ Guest), a believable antagonist over a cache. Also a candidate to feature the **raccoon rival** (Decision 67).

**A10. Base Defense.** An incursion against the Home — a predator or raccoon at the perimeter, the Watchkeeper's telegraph, citizens pathing to a refuge, a defender committed. Shows the home-sanctuary-sometimes-threatened tone (not a tower-defense arena).

**A11. Going Tharn.** The signature fear beat: a citizen frozen mid-exposure (headlights, a mower), a companion turning back to reach and lead them out. High emotional value, entirely absent so far — and a strong marketing image.

**A12. A maimed veteran, valued.** A citizen with a permanent change and adaptive scrap-gear, in a meaningful role (anchor/teacher/crafter) — the "consequence without disposability" pillar made visible. Pairs with a **Distinction/triumph** counterpart (the rare good tail).

**A13. Guest roster vignettes.** Character shots for the roster, prioritizing the signatures: **Raccoon** (opening a sealed container — new, Decision 67), **Mink** (water rescue), **Crow** (the conspicuous scout / the pull-tab trade already sketched), **Fox**, **Weasel**, **Hedgehog**, **Snake**; plus a few **Ambient** residents (Owl, Toad, Firefly at dusk). Feeds P24.

**A14. The 8 biomes as establishing shots.** Hero landscapes for any biomes not yet illustrated (Thin Grass Ribbon, Culvert Garden, Creek Split, Pond Hollow, Concrete Trench, Overpass Shadow, Interchange Expanse) — atmosphere refs for P03 and in-world variety.

## Priority 3 — story, endgame & marketing

**A15. Return-home relief.** A party arriving back into the lit colony after a hard expedition — the "Return" beat and the emotional payoff the whole loop serves.

**A16. The memorial space.** An understated in-world memorial in the Home Median — quiet, earned, tying to the named-cast stakes.

**A17. The Grand Caravan / victory.** The climactic corridor-connection expedition toward the Metropolis — the campaign's aspirational endgame image.

**A18. Seasonal Home scenes (the set).** Warm four-season views of a single colony (some exist in the Concept deck) — usable as loading screens and to show the calendar's felt texture.

**A19. Brand set.** Finalize the cracked-asphalt **MEDIAN wordmark**, the **"//" logomark**, one hero key-art poster, and the "Corridor Secured" end plate — the pieces already prototyped in the Concept deck, cleaned up.

## Suggested first commission (proving the style on the gap)

If you want a tight first batch that both rebalances and fills the biggest holes: **A1 (rabbit warren cutaway), A5-Onset (Road Work hitting), A6 (outpost claim).** One rabbit hero, one Road Work, one outpost — exactly your three seeds, and together they prove the house style on a colony, a hazard, and an expansion beat.
