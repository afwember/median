# MEDIAN m051 Source
## Residence Readiness and Colony Pressure

**Artifact class:** Developmental Source

**Status:** Exploratory and non-authoritative

**Target:** MEDIAN v0.5.1

> This Source isolates one Home relationship for later adjudication. It does
> not amend the locked m050 specification. Where it differs from the broader
> exploratory Home Readiness Source, this is the newer focused candidate for
> Residence and Colony Pressure only.

---

# 1. Residence Supports Population; It Does Not Permit Population

Population is not hard-capped by Residence count. A Citizen may join a Colony
that is not yet adequately housed. Belonging is therefore not withheld by an
abstract capacity gate.

That freedom creates a material obligation. Every resident Citizen adds
**Residential Load**. Completed, usable Residence Practices provide
**Residential Readiness** against that Load. Residence is not merely a
population slot: it is the Colony's maintained ability to shelter the lives it
has accepted.

The Residence tracker compares the two:

```text
Residential Readiness - Residential Load = Residence Margin
```

A nonnegative Margin is displayed as **Readiness**. A negative Margin is
displayed as **Residence Deficiency**. Houses do not erase the existence of
their occupants' Load; they answer it with Readiness.

The exact Load contributed by different Citizens, the Readiness supplied by
different Residence forms, and all thresholds remain tuning questions.

---

# 2. Deficiency Creates Exposure Rather Than Daily Punishment

Residence Deficiency does not automatically injure Citizens, remove them, or
deal routine daily damage. It identifies a persistent Weakness: the Colony has
accepted more domestic responsibility than its current shelter can reliably
sustain.

That Weakness contributes to global **Colony Pressure**. Pressure primarily
influences how frequently World-related Colony Encounters arise. It does not
function as individual mood, personality, or a generalized happiness score.

The Deficiency remains until the relevant Residential Readiness answers the
Residential Load. An unrelated surplus or successful civic action may help the
Colony survive an Encounter, but it does not silently cancel inadequate
housing.

---

# 3. Pressure Determines When; Weakness Determines Where

Global Colony Pressure primarily determines **when the World presses upon the
Colony**. The selected principal Weakness determines **where that pressure
finds purchase**. Current World conditions determine the concrete event.

Residence Deficiency may therefore manifest through different Encounters:

- rain reaches crowded or insufficient rooms;
- damage temporarily displaces households;
- cold exposes inadequate shelter;
- a new arrival makes the existing shortfall immediately consequential;
- evacuation or repair reveals that too few secure domestic spaces remain.

The Encounter's main transaction should concern the principal Weakness. A
secondary or B-story choice may invoke another relevant Practice or Role, but
it does not replace the Residence problem or broaden every Encounter into a
test of the whole Colony.

Resolving the immediate Encounter may protect Citizens, redirect harm, or buy
time. It need not remove the underlying Deficiency. Permanent correction comes
from restoring the relevant Readiness.

---

# 4. Attachment-Forward Consequence

This relationship makes population growth permissive but consequential. The
Colony may welcome a life before it is fully prepared, then experience the
increased Pressure of becoming ready for that life.

The resulting tension is not that new Citizens are a penalty. It is that
attachment creates responsibility. Residence makes that responsibility
material, visible, and available to story.

---

# 5. Reserved for Later Tuning

This Source does not settle:

- exact Residential Load per Citizen or household;
- exact Readiness per Residence form or Tier;
- whether Guests or temporary residents contribute different Load;
- numerical Pressure weights or Encounter-frequency curves;
- thresholds between adequate, thin, and deficient housing;
- the complete inventory of Residence-related Encounter manifestations.

Those questions should be tuned without changing the core relationship among
population, Residential Load, Residential Readiness, Residence Deficiency,
Colony Pressure, and World-related Colony Encounters.
