# The Game Day — the night test

**Tests:** whether the warm paper chassis holds against a dark image. This plate spans full daylight to near-black in five panels, so if the identity survives here it survives anywhere.
**Prepend the house-style block, and attach the current reference plate as the first image.**

---

> **Core subject:** a widescreen sourcebook plate presenting the shape of a single day in MEDIAN, as five sequential panels running left to right across the page, above a full-width band.
>
> A thin header bar across the top reads "THE GAME DAY" in a clean serif, with "MEDIAN · PART II · SECTION 9" at the right-hand end.
>
> **THE FIVE PANELS.** Equal width, separated by hairline rules, each with a small blank rounded badge in deep sign-green at its upper left, a heading, one line beneath it, and an illustration filling most of the panel. **The light changes across the row, from cold early morning through full day to true night and back toward dawn** — this progression is the point of the plate.
>
> **Panel 1 — "MORNING RUSH"** · *"Consumption and recovery resolve. The colony wakes into noise."*
> Grey early light. Dense slow traffic on wet asphalt beyond the median grass, headlights still on. Rabbits at the mouths of their holes, watching, not yet moving.
>
> **Panel 2 — "MIDDAY WINDOW"** · *"The clearest window to depart, and the one the hawks also know."*
> Bright flat daylight, high sun, short shadows. Sparse fast traffic. A small party moving through open grass toward the road edge, exposed. A bird of prey small and high in a pale sky.
>
> **Panel 3 — "EVENING RUSH"** · *"Fumes, noise, erratic traffic. A poor hour to be out."*
> Low amber sun, long shadows, heavy congested traffic with brake lights massed red. Citizens returning, carrying, heads low.
>
> **Panel 4 — "NIGHT VELOCITY"** · *"Few vehicles, enormous speed. Darkness, headlights, and fear."*
> **True night, and the darkest image on the page.** Near-black, almost empty road, one set of headlights approaching very fast and throwing hard white light across wet asphalt. A single small animal frozen at the edge of the beam. An owl on a sign gantry above, in silhouette.
>
> **Panel 5 — "DAWN RECKONING"** · *"Food, healing, wear, regrowth and the calendar all resolve."*
> First cold blue-grey light before sunrise. Quiet road, mist over the grass. A colony beginning to stir; a food store being counted.

> Beneath that band, one line of italic text across the full width: "One meaningful expedition per citizen per day. Launching commits them until dawn."
>
> A narrow footer bar across the bottom reads "OBSERVE. PLAN. ADAPT. TEACH THE YOUNG. THE MEDIAN REMEMBERS."
>
> **Critical:** the page ground stays warm cream paper throughout. **The night panel is a dark image sitting on a warm page — the page does not go dark with it.** Panel borders, rules, badges, header and footer are identical across all five regardless of the light inside them.
>

## What to check

- **Does the warm chassis survive the night panel**, or does the dark image make the page look broken?
- **Does the light progression read as one day** rather than five unrelated pictures?
- **Are the panel borders and badges genuinely identical** across the full daylight range? Any drift here shows immediately across a book.
- Panel 2 puts a hawk in the sky and Panel 4 an owl on a gantry — the Fifth Law, which currently teaches a system the game does not implement. Worth seeing whether the picture makes the absence obvious.
